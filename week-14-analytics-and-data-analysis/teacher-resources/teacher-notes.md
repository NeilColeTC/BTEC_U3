# Week 14 Teacher Notes — Analytics and Data Analysis

## Curriculum intent

The repository scaffold defines Week 14 as C4: gather and interpret campaign data, identify interaction with individual posts, compare intended and actual audiences and identify high-/low-performing content. DojoLearn supplies simulated analytics and C.P6 is the assessment focus.

## The central teaching problem

Learners often mistake **reporting data** for **analysing data**. A dashboard screenshot or list of numbers is not sufficient analytical thinking.

Use the progression:

**data → comparison → interpretation → judgement**.

For written findings use:

**claim → evidence → comparison → possible explanation → limitation → implication**.

## Metric discipline

Only teach calculations that the available DojoLearn data makes meaningful. Rates require a valid numerator and denominator. Percentage change requires an appropriate non-zero original value. Missing data is not zero.

## Fair comparisons

A raw interaction count may favour content with greater exposure. Learners should consider opportunity, platform, time live, purpose and audience before ranking posts.

## Intended vs actual audience

Return to the learner's planned target audience. Compare only characteristics the available evidence can genuinely demonstrate. Encourage **insufficient evidence** as a valid analytical outcome rather than inference from usernames, avatars or stereotypes.

## High and low performers

These classifications should be relative to an objective or relevant comparison. A post can have high reach and weak interaction; another can have modest reach but strong intended-action performance.

## Correlation and causation

Campaign analytics commonly support plausible associations rather than proof of causality. Teach qualified language and alternative explanations.

## C.P6

The learner should demonstrate analysis of campaign data as required by C4, not simply reproduce analytics. Evidence should make the reasoning traceable.

## Assessment boundary

Teachers can teach metric definitions, mathematical methods and neutral examples. Do not select the learner's strongest/weakest post for them or supply assessed interpretations.

## Preparation for Week 15

Week 14 analysis should end with evidence-based optimisation questions. Week 15 can then test/refine campaign content or activity based on identified evidence rather than intuition.
