# Week 14 Retrieval Quiz — Indicative Answers

1. Recorded starting state before campaign implementation.
2. Intended performance level/outcome for a relevant measure.
3. Records showing what was actually configured, published or operated.
4. Evidence-based provisional interpretation recorded during campaign activity.
5. Any relevant examples supported by the simulation, such as reactions/likes, comments/replies, shares/reposts, clicks/actions or reach/views.
6. A raw count is a total; a rate relates an outcome to a relevant denominator/opportunity.
7. Because the posts may have had different exposure/opportunity; a smaller count may represent a larger proportion of its audience.
8. Audience the campaign was designed to reach/influence.
9. Audience evidenced by available campaign analytics/interaction data.
10. Because it is one observation and may differ in purpose, exposure, timing or other conditions from the wider campaign.

Use diagnostically; learners interpret their own assessed campaign evidence.
