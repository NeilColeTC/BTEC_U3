## Evidence discipline

Only compare characteristics that the available analytics or interaction evidence can support.

Do **not** infer age, gender, location, interests or other characteristics merely from usernames, language, avatars or assumptions unless the simulation explicitly provides reliable data for that characteristic.

## Questions

1. Which intended audience characteristics are supported by actual evidence?
2. Where is there a partial match or mismatch?
3. Which characteristics cannot be tested with available data?
4. Does any mismatch matter to the campaign objective?
5. What optimisation question follows from the audience comparison?
