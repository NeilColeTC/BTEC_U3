## Classification

Use evidence to classify content as appropriate, for example:

- relatively high-performing;
- mid-range/typical;
- relatively low-performing;
- insufficient evidence/not fairly comparable.

## Analytical structure

For significant findings use:

**claim → evidence → comparison → possible explanation → limitation → implication**

## Fair-comparison questions

- Did the items have similar opportunity/exposure?
- Were they published on the same platform?
- Did they have the same purpose?
- Were they live for comparable periods?
- Did they target similar audience behaviour?
- Is the chosen metric relevant to the campaign objective?
