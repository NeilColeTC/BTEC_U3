## Analytical standard

A strong entry should allow another reader to trace the reasoning from evidence to conclusion.

Use:

**claim → evidence → comparison → possible explanation → limitation → implication**

## Language discipline

Prefer qualified wording when the evidence cannot establish causation:

- “may indicate…”
- “is consistent with…”
- “suggests…”
- “one possible explanation is…”

Avoid “X caused Y” unless the available evidence genuinely supports that claim.
