# DojoLearn Practical — Analytics Workshop

## Purpose

Extract and analyse the simulated campaign evidence required by C4/C.P6.

## Stage 1 — Define analysis questions

Examples of question types:

- Which content appears strongest/weakest against a relevant objective?
- How do individual posts differ in interaction?
- Is the campaign reaching the intended audience according to available evidence?
- How has a relevant campaign measure changed from baseline?
- How close is performance to the target?

Learners should select questions relevant to their own campaign.

## Stage 2 — Capture analytics evidence

Record available DojoLearn metrics and retain supporting evidence as directed. Do not invent metrics that the simulator does not expose.

## Stage 3 — Organise

Populate the analytics data-capture table. Keep missing/unavailable data distinct from a measured value of zero.

## Stage 4 — Calculate where useful

Use derived rates/percentage change only when numerator and denominator are meaningful and definitions are clear.

## Stage 5 — Compare individual content

Identify high/low performers using relevant evidence, not one universal ranking metric.

## Stage 6 — Compare audience

Compare intended audience with actual audience evidence available in DojoLearn and interaction records. Use **insufficient evidence** where appropriate.

## Stage 7 — Interpret

For each major finding use:

**claim → evidence → comparison → possible explanation → limitation → implication**.

## Stage 8 — Prepare optimisation questions

Do not optimise yet merely because a number looks low. Identify evidence-based questions for Week 15, for example whether to change format, timing, message, CTA, platform emphasis or visibility technique.

## Evidence pack checklist

- campaign-level analytics snapshot/evidence;
- individual-post data;
- calculations where used;
- intended/actual audience comparison;
- high/low performer analysis;
- links to relevant implementation/community evidence;
- stated data limitations;
- optimisation questions.
