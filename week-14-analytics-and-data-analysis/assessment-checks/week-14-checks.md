# Week 14 — Assessment and Understanding Checks

## Criteria

**C.P6:** review campaign usage and interaction data.  
Week 14 also provides evidence for **BC.D2** evaluation and may demonstrate **BC.D3** responsibility/self-management through accurate, organised analytics work.

## Core checks

- [ ] collect DojoLearn analytics accurately;
- [ ] preserve analytics evidence;
- [ ] distinguish missing data from zero;
- [ ] distinguish raw counts from rates;
- [ ] use valid denominators/calculations;
- [ ] analyse individual content interaction;
- [ ] compare intended/actual audience only where evidence supports it;
- [ ] identify high/low performers fairly;
- [ ] identify patterns, anomalies and limitations;
- [ ] distinguish correlation from causation;
- [ ] generate evidence-based optimisation questions;
- [ ] maintain an organised, accountable analytics evidence trail that may support BC.D3.

BC.D3 itself is not a campaign-evaluation criterion.
