# Assignment 2 — Learning Aims B/C Grading/Readiness Checklist

## B.P3 — Plan
- [ ] Campaign plan responds to business requirements.
- [ ] Audience, objectives, platforms, content, schedule, measures, responsibilities and relevant policy/community arrangements are sufficiently clear.

## B.P4 — Review
- [ ] Plan is reviewed with others.
- [ ] Feedback is recorded.
- [ ] Feedback informs identifiable improvements/refinement decisions.

## B.M2 — Justification
- [ ] Significant planning decisions are justified against purpose/business requirements.
- [ ] Audience, platform/content evidence, constraints and trade-offs are considered.

## C.P5 — Content/implementation
- [ ] Business-related content uses appropriate social-media features.
- [ ] Implemented content meets requirements of the plan.
- [ ] Practical evidence is attributable and traceable.

## C.P6 — Usage/interaction data review
- [ ] Social-media usage and interaction data are gathered/reviewed.
- [ ] Individual content performance and relevant audience evidence are interpreted.
- [ ] Data limitations are recognised.

## C.M3 — Optimisation
- [ ] Analytics evidence drives improvements.
- [ ] Content, format and/or features are optimised in line with plan requirements.
- [ ] Before/after or equivalent evidence shows what changed and what the evidence suggests.

## BC.D2 — Evaluation
- [ ] Plan and use of social media are evaluated against business requirements.
- [ ] Evidence from planning, implementation, analytics and optimisation supports balanced judgements.
- [ ] Significant limitations and recommendations are identified.

## BC.D3 — Professional behaviours
- [ ] Individual responsibility is demonstrated through authentic process evidence.
- [ ] Creativity is demonstrated through appropriate campaign decisions/problem solving.
- [ ] Effective self-management is demonstrated through organisation, deadlines, evidence management and response to feedback/data/issues.
- [ ] Evidence is cross-referenced rather than relying only on retrospective claims.

## Final authenticity/traceability

- [ ] Requirement → plan → review → implementation → analytics → optimisation → evaluation can be followed.
- [ ] Missing historical evidence has not been invented.
- [ ] Learner decisions and evaluative conclusions remain their own.
