# Assignment 1 — Learning Aim A Grading/Readiness Checklist

## A.P1

- [ ] Explains different ways businesses use social media.
- [ ] Goes beyond feature lists to explain how/why uses support business needs.
- [ ] Uses relevant examples/evidence.

## A.P2

- [ ] Explains audience profiles of different social-media websites.
- [ ] Uses evidence rather than unsupported stereotypes.
- [ ] Makes audience/platform distinctions clear.

## A.M1

- [ ] Assesses different ways a business can use social media to attract a target audience.
- [ ] Considers strengths, limitations and context.
- [ ] Makes comparisons and supported judgements.

## A.D1

- [ ] Evaluates business use of social media for interaction and promotion to a target audience.
- [ ] Weighs significant benefits and A3 risks/issues.
- [ ] Considers consequences/mitigation where relevant.
- [ ] Reaches a reasoned overall conclusion.

## Evidence-quality checks

- [ ] Claims are supported by credible/current sources where appropriate.
- [ ] Sources are traceable and referenced consistently.
- [ ] Higher-level writing is not merely longer Pass-level description.
- [ ] Learner wording and conclusions remain independent.
