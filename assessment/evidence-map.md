# Evidence Map

## Assignment 1

Expected evidence is a learner-authored written report supported by relevant real-world examples and research.

## Assignment 2

Potential evidence includes:

- social-media plan and content posting schedule
- business requirements and measurable success criteria
- target audience and platform-selection evidence
- keyword/hashtag strategy
- original and improved plan versions
- stakeholder/client/peer review evidence
- justification of planning decisions
- screenshots or system-generated evidence of profiles and published content
- interaction logs and community-management evidence
- analytics exports, screenshots or recorded metrics
- review of social-media usage and interaction
- before/after optimised content
- evaluation against business requirements
- evidence of individual responsibility, creativity and self-management

DojoLearn should be used to make the practical evidence repeatable and controllable while preserving learner ownership of decisions.
