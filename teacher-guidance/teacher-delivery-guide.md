# Teacher Delivery Guide

## Delivery philosophy

Use the repository as a teaching sequence, not as a set of model assignment answers. The practical progression is:

**plan → create → publish → interact → analyse → optimise → evaluate**.

## Assessment phases

- Weeks 1–5: teach Learning Aim A.
- Week 6: Assignment 1 assessment workshop.
- Weeks 7–11: plan/review Assignment 2.
- Weeks 12–15: implement, analyse and optimise.
- Week 16: BC.D2 evaluation plus BC.D3 professional-behaviour evidence audit.
- Week 17: final evidence QA/submission.

## DojoLearn

Treat the simulator as a controlled practical environment. Use only features/metrics actually available. If the simulator changes, adapt teaching wording rather than inventing functionality.

## Evidence habits to establish early

- stable content/evidence IDs;
- baseline and target capture;
- implementation logs;
- community-management records;
- analytics evidence;
- before/after optimisation evidence;
- refinement/deviation records;
- evidence of individual responsibility, creativity and self-management.

## Command terms

- **Explain:** make clear how/why.
- **Assess:** consider relevant factors/strengths/limitations and reach supported judgement(s).
- **Evaluate:** weigh significance against criteria and reach a reasoned conclusion.

## BC.D3

Do not wait until Week 16 to ask for BC.D3 evidence. Learners should naturally demonstrate responsibility, creativity and self-management across Weeks 7–15. Logs, versions, deadlines, independent choices, professional responses and evidence-led adjustments can make the behaviour visible without requiring artificial extra tasks.
