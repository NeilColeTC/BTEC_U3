# Assessment Boundaries and Learner Independence

## Principle

Repository resources should prepare learners to produce independent assessed work without becoming a completed response to the live assignment.

## Appropriate support

Teachers may generally support process through:

- clarification of task/criterion wording within current centre/Pearson rules;
- retrieval of taught concepts;
- generic command-term guidance;
- neutral formative examples;
- organisational/evidence templates;
- simulator operation guidance;
- questions that help learners locate gaps in their own evidence;
- accessibility and reasonable-adjustment support as authorised.

## Avoid

- writing or rewriting assessed paragraphs;
- selecting the learner's final platform/content/optimisation decision;
- supplying personalised distinction conclusions;
- manufacturing historical evidence, reasons, dates, targets or outcomes;
- converting neutral practice material into a live assignment answer;
- treating peer/teacher feedback as authorship of the assessed response.

## Evidence authenticity

Encourage contemporaneous evidence. Where historical evidence is genuinely absent, learners should identify the limitation rather than reconstruct an event that did not occur.

## Annual check

Before delivery, compare this repository with the current approved assignment brief, Pearson guidance and centre assessment/authentication procedures.
