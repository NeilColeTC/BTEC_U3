# DojoLearn Simulation Guidance

DojoLearn is the practical environment for Unit 3. It should be used as a teaching and evidence-generation tool rather than as a substitute for learner thinking.

## Intended progression

1. Plan
2. Create
3. Publish
4. Interact
5. Analyse
6. Optimise
7. Evaluate

## Simulated platforms

Learners may encounter The Wall, The Pulse, The Grid, The Metronome, The Pin and The Tube. Teaching should compare their characteristics with real-world social-media platform types without implying that the simulated platforms are exact replicas.

## Assessment boundary

Teacher resources may model techniques, demonstrate platform features and provide practice scenarios. They must not provide completed answers to live assignment tasks. Learners must make and justify their own campaign decisions, respond to feedback, interpret their own analytics and produce their own evaluation.
