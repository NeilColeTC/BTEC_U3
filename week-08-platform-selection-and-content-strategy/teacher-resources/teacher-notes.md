# Week 8 Teacher Notes — Platform Selection and Content Strategy

## Curriculum intent

The repository scaffold specifies B2/B3, matching platform profiles to business requirements, measurable targets and audience-focused content planning, with DojoLearn used to select simulated platforms and establish targets for available campaign metrics.

## Build directly on Week 7

Do not allow Week 8 to become a fresh-start platform lesson. Every decision should begin with the Week 7 planning foundation:

**requirements + audience + objectives + baseline + constraints**.

Learners who cannot state these clearly should revisit Week 7 before committing to platform/content decisions.

## Platform selection

Avoid teaching universal claims such as “Platform X is for young people” unless supported by appropriate evidence and relevant to the simulated context. The reasoning target is suitability **for this campaign**.

Useful selection dimensions include audience, functionality, content format, interaction, measurement, resources, constraints and risk. Encourage weighting: a critical requirement should influence the decision more than a minor convenience.

## Content strategy

Learners often jump from platform choice directly to writing posts. Week 8 instead establishes the role and balance of content. Detailed wording, hashtags/keywords and scheduling belong primarily in Week 9.

## Metrics and targets

Use only DojoLearn capabilities actually available. The original scaffold names followers, reach, interaction, likes/shares and click-throughs where available; this does not imply every simulated platform exposes every metric.

Targets should be connected to objectives and baselines. Challenge arbitrary precision and distinguish useful indicators from vanity metrics.

## B.P3 → B.M2 progression

At Pass preparation level, learners need a coherent plan that meets identified requirements. Merit reasoning requires them to justify why planning choices are appropriate.

A useful formative reasoning chain is:

**choice → evidence → requirement/audience link → likely benefit → limitation/trade-off → judgement**.

This should remain formative when using neutral scenarios. Do not write the learner's live Assignment 2 justification.

## Adaptive teaching

### Support
- pre-populated factor headings;
- platform-comparison matrices;
- content-purpose cards;
- oral rehearsal of reasoning;
- sentence stems such as “This capability matters because…” without supplying substantive answers.

### Stretch
- weight selection criteria;
- distinguish complementary vs duplicated multi-platform roles;
- identify opportunity cost;
- consider unavailable metrics/proxy measures;
- test whether an apparently strong metric actually demonstrates the objective.

## Preparation for Week 9

Learners should leave Week 8 with a defensible platform approach, content-strategy direction and measurable targets. Week 9 can then convert these into a content calendar, keyword/hashtag strategy and draft posts.
