# Week 8 Retrieval Quiz — Indicative Answers

1. A need, expectation or condition the campaign should satisfy because of the business/client context.
2. An aim is broad direction; an objective is a more specific intended result that can be judged/measured.
3. It provides a starting point for comparison and target setting.
4. Evidence/condition used to judge whether an objective/requirement has been met.
5. Its target may be arbitrary, unrealistic, irrelevant or unsupported by baseline/business evidence.
6. A limitation/condition restricting available campaign choices.
7. Connecting decisions back to requirements/objectives and forward to evidence/measures.
8. Popularity may not match the intended audience, objective, content needs, resources, available measurement or constraints; any two well-explained reasons.
9. Reach concerns exposure; engagement concerns interaction.
10. objective; measure.
