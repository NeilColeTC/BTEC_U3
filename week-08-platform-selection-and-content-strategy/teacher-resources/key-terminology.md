# Week 8 Key Terminology

| Term | Working definition | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Platform suitability | How well a platform's characteristics match a campaign's audience, objectives, content needs and constraints. | Replaces preference-led selection with evidence-based choice. |
| Platform capability | A function, content format, interaction method or measurement feature supported by a platform. | Capabilities should connect to campaign requirements. |
| Content strategy | Planned approach to content purposes, formats, messages and audience response across a campaign. | A campaign needs coherence beyond isolated posts. |
| Content mix | Combination of different content purposes/formats used across the campaign. | Helps balance promotion, information, engagement and action. |
| Content purpose | The intended role of a piece/type of content, such as informing, promoting or engaging. | Makes content decisions objective-led. |
| Call to action | Clear prompt indicating an intended next step for the audience. | Connects content to action-oriented objectives. |
| KPI / performance measure | Evidence used to monitor progress towards an objective. | Enables later analytics and evaluation. |
| Campaign target | Intended level/value for a relevant measure within a defined timescale. | Makes performance expectations checkable. |
| Baseline | Starting value/state before campaign implementation. | Gives targets and later change context. |
| Vanity metric | A metric that can look impressive without necessarily demonstrating the intended campaign outcome. | Encourages critical interpretation of likes/followers/reach. |
| Trade-off | A disadvantage/cost accepted in order to gain another benefit. | Supports B.M2-level justification. |
| Weighting | Giving greater importance to selection factors that matter most to the campaign. | Prevents simplistic checklist decisions. |
| Multi-platform strategy | Use of more than one platform with defined complementary roles. | Can extend campaign capability but increases resource demand. |
| Accessibility | Designing communication so it can be accessed/understood by a wider range of users. | Should influence content-format decisions. |
| Traceability | Ability to connect choices to requirements/objectives and later evidence. | Maintains coherence from planning to evaluation. |
