# Week 8 — Assessment and Understanding Checks

## Criteria progression

**B.P3:** coherent campaign planning that meets identified requirements.  
**B.M2:** justification of planning decisions/choices.

## Core checks

By the end of Week 8, learners should be able to:

- [ ] recall Week 7 requirements, objectives, audience, measures and baseline;
- [ ] compare platform characteristics against campaign needs;
- [ ] select platforms for reasons other than personal preference/popularity;
- [ ] explain how platform capabilities support objectives;
- [ ] consider audience fit without unsupported stereotypes;
- [ ] consider resource/time implications;
- [ ] plan a purposeful content mix;
- [ ] distinguish content strategy from individual posts;
- [ ] connect content purposes to objectives;
- [ ] establish relevant campaign measures/targets;
- [ ] use baseline information when setting targets;
- [ ] recognise vanity metrics and measurement limitations;
- [ ] record platform/target evidence in DojoLearn;
- [ ] identify at least one limitation/trade-off;
- [ ] justify why the overall approach is appropriate for the campaign.

## B.P3 readiness indicators

Learner's plan identifies suitable platform/content directions, measurable targets and clear links to campaign requirements.

## B.M2 readiness indicators

Learner can move beyond describing choices by using evidence and contextual reasoning, including significant benefits, limitations/trade-offs and a supported judgement.

## Common misconceptions

- [ ] “Most popular = best.”
- [ ] “The platform I personally use is the best evidence.”
- [ ] “More platforms automatically improve reach.”
- [ ] “A list of platform features is justification.”
- [ ] “Every campaign needs every content format.”
- [ ] “A content strategy is a collection of finished posts.”
- [ ] “More likes means the campaign succeeded.”
- [ ] “Any percentage target is SMART.”
- [ ] “All DojoLearn platforms expose the same analytics.”

## Evidence sources

Use retrieval, platform-selection matrix, content-strategy planner, target-setting workshop, DojoLearn practical, B.M2 formative justification and exit ticket.

## Transition check for Week 9

Before detailed content production, learners should be able to explain:

1. which platform(s) they intend to use;
2. why those platforms fit requirements/audience/objectives;
3. what roles different content will perform;
4. which measures/targets will be monitored;
5. what significant limitation/trade-off remains.

If these are unclear, detailed post creation in Week 9 risks becoming disconnected from the campaign plan.
