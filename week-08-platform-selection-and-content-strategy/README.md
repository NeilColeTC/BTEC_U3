# Week 8 — Platform Selection and Content Strategy

**Specification focus:** Learning Aim B — B2/B3  
**Assessment preparation:** B.P3 / B.M2  
**Practical environment:** DojoLearn social-media simulation

## Week purpose

Week 8 takes the requirements, target audience, objectives and baseline established in Week 7 and turns them into justified platform and content-strategy decisions. Learners should move beyond “this platform is popular” towards selecting campaign channels because their characteristics support a specific audience, purpose, objective and available resource.

The central reasoning chain is:

**requirement → audience/objective → platform capability → content approach → measure → justification**

## Learning outcomes

Learners should be able to:

1. Compare platform characteristics against campaign requirements.
2. Select simulated social-media platforms using audience, purpose, functionality and resource evidence.
3. Distinguish platform suitability from personal preference or general popularity.
4. Plan an appropriate content mix for the intended audience and objectives.
5. Establish measurable campaign targets using relevant DojoLearn metrics where available.
6. Explain how platform and content choices work together as a strategy.
7. Develop reasoned justification that prepares learners for B.M2.

## B2/B3 focus

This week concentrates on:

- platform selection;
- audience/platform fit;
- campaign targets;
- content purpose and format;
- content mix;
- resource/constraint considerations;
- links between planned content and measurable objectives.

Detailed post scheduling, keywords/hashtags and publishing preparation are reserved for Week 9.

## DojoLearn practical

Learners inspect/select the simulated platforms available to their campaign and record campaign targets for relevant metrics such as followers, reach, interaction, likes/shares and click-throughs **where the simulator provides them**. Learners should not invent unavailable metrics or treat every metric as equally important.
