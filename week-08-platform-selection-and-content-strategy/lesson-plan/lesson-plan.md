# Week 8 Lesson Plan — Platform Selection and Content Strategy

**Duration:** 3 hours  
**Learning Aim:** B  
**Specification:** B2/B3  
**Criteria preparation:** B.P3 / B.M2

## Learning outcomes

Learners will select platforms and develop a content strategy that can be justified against campaign requirements, target audience and measurable objectives.

## Lesson sequence

### 1. Retrieval from Week 7 — 15 minutes

Retrieve requirement, objective, target audience, success criterion, measure, baseline, constraint and traceability. Learners reconstruct the Week 7 chain:

**requirement → objective → activity → measure**.

### 2. Platform selection is a decision, not a preference — 20 minutes

Discuss why popularity alone is insufficient. Introduce selection factors:

- intended audience;
- campaign purpose/objectives;
- available platform features;
- suitable content formats;
- interaction/community features;
- measurement capability;
- time/resources;
- constraints and risks.

### 3. Platform selection matrix — 30 minutes

Learners compare several neutral/simulated platform profiles against a practice campaign. They score only where evidence supports the score, then explain which factors matter most. Emphasise that weighted reasoning is stronger than simply adding ticks.

### 4. Content strategy — 30 minutes

Teach the difference between a **content strategy** and an individual post. Learners plan a mix of content purposes and formats linked to objectives and audience needs.

Consider:

- inform/educate;
- promote;
- engage/interact;
- direct users towards an action;
- reassure/support;
- maintain campaign continuity.

### 5. Target-setting workshop — 20 minutes

Using Week 7 baseline thinking, learners select meaningful measures and construct defensible targets. Revisit vanity metrics and arbitrary percentages.

### Break — 10 minutes

### 6. DojoLearn platform and target setup — 35 minutes

Learners inspect/select appropriate simulated platforms and record:

- platform capability evidence;
- relationship to target audience/objectives;
- selected campaign metrics;
- baseline values where visible;
- target values and rationale;
- any simulator limitations.

No invented functionality or metrics.

### 7. B.M2 justification practice — 25 minutes

Learners justify platform/content decisions for a neutral formative scenario. Require a reasoning chain rather than feature description:

**choice → evidence → requirement/audience link → likely benefit → limitation/trade-off → judgement**.

### 8. Exit ticket — 15 minutes

Learners identify a weak platform justification, improve it and explain how one content decision supports a measurable campaign objective.

## Adaptive teaching

**Support:** comparison matrices, factor prompts, content-purpose cards and justification sentence stems that structure reasoning without supplying decisions.  
**Stretch:** weighting of selection factors, multi-platform role differentiation, trade-offs, opportunity cost and measurement limitations.

## English, maths, EDI and employability

**English:** comparative and justificatory writing.  
**Maths:** baselines, targets, change, proportions and interpretation of campaign metrics.  
**EDI:** avoid assuming that demographic labels automatically determine platform/content preferences; consider accessibility and inclusive content formats.  
**Employability:** channel selection, strategic planning, resource allocation, KPI selection and evidence-based justification.

## Teacher reflection

- Are platform choices requirement-led?
- Can learners explain why a feature matters rather than merely naming it?
- Are targets defensible against baseline/objective evidence?
- Does the content mix serve different campaign purposes?
- Are learners beginning to reason at B.M2 level?
