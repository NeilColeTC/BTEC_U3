# Activity — Campaign Target-Setting Workshop

## From objective to target

Use this chain:

**objective → measure → baseline → target → timescale → interpretation**

| Objective | Relevant measure | Baseline | Proposed target | Timescale | Why this target is defensible | What would achievement mean? |

## Metric challenge

For each proposed metric ask:

- Does it actually relate to the objective?
- Is it available in DojoLearn/the campaign environment?
- Is it a count, rate, percentage or other measure?
- Could the number increase without the campaign actually succeeding?
- Is another measure needed to provide context?

## Target challenge

For each target ask:

- What is the baseline?
- Why this value rather than a random number?
- Is the timescale sufficient?
- Is it plausible given campaign resources?
- Would missing the target necessarily mean total failure?

## Stretch

A campaign doubles its likes but generates no increase in the intended click-through action. Has it succeeded? Explain against the original objective.
