## Consistency decisions

Record principles for:

- tone of voice;
- key message(s);
- visual/brand consistency;
- accessibility/inclusive communication;
- balance between promotion and useful/engaging content.

## Strategy check

1. Is each content role linked to an objective or requirement?
2. Is there unnecessary duplication?
3. Does the mix provide value to the audience as well as promote the business?
4. Can the selected platform support the intended formats/interactions?
5. What evidence would later indicate whether the strategy worked?
