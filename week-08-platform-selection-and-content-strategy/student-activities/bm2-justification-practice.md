# B.M2 Formative Practice — Justifying Platform and Content Strategy

**Formative scenario — not a completed Assignment 2 response.**

## Scenario

A fictional community arts centre is promoting a month of low-cost evening workshops. Its campaign needs to raise local awareness, communicate what participants can expect and encourage visits to a programme-information page. Staff can create simple images and short videos but have limited time to manage multiple active channels.

Your teacher will provide neutral simulated platform profiles or direct you to appropriate DojoLearn platform information.

## Task

Recommend a platform approach and outline a content strategy. Justify your decisions using:

**choice → evidence → requirement/audience link → likely benefit → limitation/trade-off → judgement**

Your response should address:

- which platform(s) would be suitable and why;
- which significant selection factors influenced the decision;
- how content purposes/formats would support the campaign objectives;
- how resource constraints affect the strategy;
- which metrics could provide useful evidence;
- at least one limitation or trade-off;
- why the overall approach is appropriate for this scenario.

## Avoid

- “I use this platform.”
- “Everyone is on it.”
- feature lists with no explanation;
- unsupported audience stereotypes;
- arbitrary targets;
- treating more platforms as automatically better.

## Peer-review questions

1. Can you identify the requirement behind each major decision?
2. Is platform evidence used, not merely asserted?
3. Is there a meaningful comparison/trade-off?
4. Does the content strategy support measurable objectives?
5. Is the final judgement contextual?
