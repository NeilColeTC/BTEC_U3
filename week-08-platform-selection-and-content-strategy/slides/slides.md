# Week 8 Slides — Platform Selection and Content Strategy

## Slide 1 — Week 8
### Platform Selection and Content Strategy
Choose channels because they fit the campaign — not because you personally use them.

---
## Slide 2 — Week 7 foundation
You should already know: requirements • purpose • audience • objectives • measures • baseline • constraints.

---
## Slide 3 — Today's reasoning chain
**Requirement → audience/objective → platform capability → content approach → measure → justification**

---
## Slide 4 — Platform popularity ≠ suitability
A popular platform can still be a poor campaign choice if its audience, features, content demands or measurement do not fit the requirement.

---
## Slide 5 — Selection factors
Audience • purpose • functionality • content formats • interaction • measurement • resources • constraints • risk.

---
## Slide 6 — Audience fit
What evidence suggests that the platform can help reach the intended audience? Avoid unsupported demographic stereotypes.

---
## Slide 7 — Objective fit
Which platform capability directly supports the campaign objective?

---
## Slide 8 — Content fit
Can the platform support the type of communication/content needed for the campaign?

---
## Slide 9 — Interaction fit
Does the campaign require comments, sharing, community interaction, direct responses or another interaction mechanism?

---
## Slide 10 — Measurement fit
Can the platform/simulator provide evidence relevant to the objective?

---
## Slide 11 — Resource fit
A platform that requires more frequent or specialist content may be less suitable when time/resources are constrained.

---
## Slide 12 — Compare, don't describe
Description: “Platform A supports video.”  
Reasoning: “Video capability matters because…”

---
## Slide 13 — Weight important factors
Not every factor is equally important. A campaign with a strict audience requirement may weight audience fit more heavily than convenience.

---
## Slide 14 — Content strategy
A planned approach to what types of content will be used, for what purpose and audience, and how those choices support campaign objectives.

---
## Slide 15 — A post is not a strategy
One attractive post does not demonstrate how the campaign will communicate consistently over time.

---
## Slide 16 — Content purposes
Inform • educate • promote • engage • direct action • support/reassure • maintain awareness.

---
## Slide 17 — Content formats
Text • image • video • links • questions/polls • other simulator-supported formats. Select formats because they serve a purpose.

---
## Slide 18 — Content mix
A campaign may need different content roles. Avoid repeating the same promotional message in every post.

---
## Slide 19 — Call to action
Where an objective requires an action, content should make the intended next step clear and appropriate.

---
## Slide 20 — Consistency
Tone, message, visual identity and purpose should feel connected across campaign activity.

---
## Slide 21 — Targets
Targets turn objectives into something that can later be checked — but they need a meaningful measure and defensible value.

---
## Slide 22 — Baseline → target
Without a starting point, claims about improvement may be difficult to interpret.

---
## Slide 23 — Vanity metrics
Followers, likes or reach may be useful, but only when they relate to what the campaign is trying to achieve.

---
## Slide 24 — DojoLearn
Use only metrics and capabilities actually available in the simulation. Record limitations rather than inventing data.

---
## Slide 25 — B.P3
Your plan should make clear what you intend to do and how it meets the identified requirements.

---
## Slide 26 — Towards B.M2
Move from **what I chose** to **why this combination is appropriate**.

---
## Slide 27 — Justification chain
**choice → evidence → requirement/audience link → benefit → limitation/trade-off → judgement**

---
## Slide 28 — Weak justification
“I chose Platform B because it is popular and easy to use.”

---
## Slide 29 — Stronger reasoning
Identify the relevant capability, explain its relationship to the campaign, recognise a limitation and reach a contextual judgement.

---

Turn the strategy into a detailed content calendar and planned posts.
