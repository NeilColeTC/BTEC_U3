# Week 11 Teacher Notes — Reviewing and Refining the Plan

## Curriculum intent

The repository scaffold defines Week 11 as B6: gather stakeholder feedback, document review activity, refine the campaign plan and justify decisions against business requirements. DojoLearn is used to prepare the final implementation configuration after review. Assessment focus is B.P4/B.M2/BC.D3.

## This is a gateway week

Week 11 should function as a deliberate checkpoint. Learners should not simply “finish whatever is missing” and start publishing. The goal is to establish that the plan is coherent and sufficiently complete to implement.

## Four review lenses

Use:

1. **requirements** — does the plan satisfy the business/client need?
2. **coherence** — do its elements work together?
3. **feasibility** — can it realistically be implemented?
4. **measurability** — can outcomes later be judged against objectives?

These keep feedback focused on campaign quality rather than taste.

## Stakeholder feedback

The existence of peer comments is not enough. Learners need useful, recorded feedback and should show what they did with it. Encourage focused questions rather than “What do you think?”.

Feedback should not automatically be accepted. Strong review practice includes **accept, adapt and reject/retain** decisions where these are justified.

## Refinement and dependencies

Changes can propagate through the plan. For example, a platform change can affect content format, scheduling, audience assumptions, measures, community workload and policy arrangements. Require a dependency check after significant refinements.

## B.P4

The emphasis is evidence of review and refinement: feedback obtained, issues identified, changes/decisions recorded and the plan improved appropriately.

## B.M2

Learners should justify significant planning choices and refinements against requirements rather than merely report what changed.

## BC.D3 progression

Week 11 develops evaluative reasoning through alternatives, consequences, constraints and supported judgement. However, distinguish **evaluating the quality of a plan** from **evaluating campaign performance**. The latter requires implementation evidence from later weeks.

## DojoLearn

After review, verify that the simulation configuration reflects the final plan. Capture the pre-implementation state/baseline where possible. If DojoLearn cannot represent a planned feature or measure, record the limitation rather than inventing functionality.

## Assessment boundary

Provide review structures and questions, not the learner's decisions. Do not tell a learner which feedback to accept or supply final assessed justifications.

## Preparation for implementation

A learner is ready to implement when the campaign has traceable requirements/objectives, coherent platform/content/community decisions, realistic responsibilities/timescales, measurable targets, documented review/refinement and a matching DojoLearn configuration.
