# Week 11 Retrieval Quiz — Indicative Answers

1. A need, expectation or condition the campaign should satisfy because of the business/client context.
2. So progress/achievement can be judged using evidence relevant to what the objective actually intends.
3. How well a platform's characteristics match campaign audience, objectives, content needs and constraints.
4. Planned approach to content purposes, formats, messages and intended audience response.
5. To organise timing, sequence, content purpose, responsibilities and campaign coverage.
6. Planned monitoring, interaction and response activity supporting an online community.
7. Passing an issue to someone with appropriate authority/expertise.
8. Any valid reason such as consistency, professionalism, confidentiality, security, accountability or reputation protection.
9. Ability to connect decisions back to requirements/objectives and forward to activity/evidence.
10. To identify gaps, contradictions, feasibility problems and measurement weaknesses before resources are committed to implementation.

Use diagnostically; learners' assessed refinements remain their own.
