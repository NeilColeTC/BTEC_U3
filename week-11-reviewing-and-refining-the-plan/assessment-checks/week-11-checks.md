# Week 11 — Assessment and Understanding Checks

## Criteria

**B.P4:** review with others and improvements informed by feedback.  
**B.M2:** justification of planning decisions/refinements.

## Checks

- [ ] review against requirements, coherence, feasibility and measurability;
- [ ] formulate useful stakeholder-feedback questions;
- [ ] record feedback accurately;
- [ ] distinguish feedback from instruction;
- [ ] prioritise feedback using impact, evidence and feasibility;
- [ ] accept/adapt/reject feedback with justification;
- [ ] document significant refinements and dependencies;
- [ ] align final DojoLearn configuration to the refined plan;
- [ ] capture the pre-implementation baseline;
- [ ] preserve authentic evidence of independent decisions/organisation that may later support BC.D3.

## Misconceptions

- [ ] review means proofreading only;
- [ ] every suggestion must be accepted;
- [ ] changing more things means stronger refinement;
- [ ] BC.D3 is a Week 11 campaign-evaluation criterion;
- [ ] a strong plan proves later campaign success.
