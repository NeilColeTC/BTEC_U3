# Week 11 Slides — Reviewing and Refining the Plan

## Slide 1 — Week 11
### Reviewing and Refining the Plan
Quality assurance before implementation.

---
## Slide 2 — Review cycle
Plan → test against requirements → stakeholder feedback → analyse → refine/retain → justify → implementation baseline.

---
## Slide 3 — Four review lenses
Requirements • coherence • feasibility • measurability.

---
## Slide 4 — Review ≠ proofreading
Check whether the campaign will meet requirements, not only presentation.

---
## Slide 5 — Useful feedback
Ask focused questions tied to audience, objectives, feasibility and measurement.

---
## Slide 6 — Feedback is not an instruction
Accept • adapt • reject/retain — all can be valid when justified.

---
## Slide 7 — Prioritise
Impact on requirement/objective × validity/evidence × feasibility.

---
## Slide 8 — Refinement log
Original issue → feedback → decision/change → reason → dependent changes → expected improvement.

---
## Slide 9 — Dependencies
A change to platform/content/timing can affect metrics, resources and policy.

---
## Slide 10 — B.P4
Evidence review with others and improvements informed by feedback.

---
## Slide 11 — B.M2
Justify significant decisions/refinements against campaign purpose and business requirements.

---
## Slide 12 — BC.D3 awareness
Responsibility, creativity and self-management should be evidenced across the process, not treated as a final Week 11 evaluation task.

---
## Slide 13 — DojoLearn
Final configuration should reflect the **refined** plan.

---
## Slide 14 — Freeze baseline
Capture the final pre-implementation state for later comparison.

---
## Slide 15 — Implementation readiness
Can another person understand what should happen, why, when, by whom and how success will be judged?
