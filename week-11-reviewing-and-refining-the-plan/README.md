# Week 11 — Reviewing and Refining the Plan

**Specification focus:** Learning Aim B — B6  
**Assessment preparation:** B.P4 / B.M2  
**Practical environment:** DojoLearn social-media simulation

## Week purpose

Week 11 is the quality-assurance gateway between planning and implementation. Learners gather stakeholder feedback, audit the campaign plan against requirements, identify weaknesses or inconsistencies, make controlled refinements and justify what they changed — or deliberately retained.

**Review cycle:** plan → test against requirements → stakeholder feedback → analyse → refine/retain → justify → implementation baseline.

## Important criterion distinction

B.P4 is evidenced through review with others and improvements. B.M2 is evidenced through justification of planning decisions. BC.D3 is not a review/evaluation criterion; evidence for individual responsibility, creativity and effective self-management should be accumulated throughout the planning and implementation process.

## Assessment boundary

Teachers/resources should not decide which feedback a learner must accept or write the learner's final justification.
