# Week 11 Lesson Plan — Reviewing and Refining the Plan

**Duration:** 3 hours  
**Learning Aim:** B  
**Specification:** B6  
**Criteria preparation:** B.P4 / B.M2 / BC.D3

## Learning outcomes

Learners will conduct a structured review of a campaign plan, gather and analyse stakeholder feedback, document refinements and prepare the final DojoLearn implementation configuration.

## Lesson sequence

### 1. Retrieval and planning reconstruction — 15 minutes

Learners reconstruct the planning chain from Weeks 7–10:

**requirements → audience/objectives → platform/content strategy → publication plan → community/policy → measures**.

Ask learners to identify where a weakness in one element could create problems elsewhere.

### 2. Review is more than proofreading — 20 minutes

Introduce four review lenses:

- **requirements:** does the plan satisfy what the business needs?
- **coherence:** do the elements work together?
- **feasibility:** can the plan realistically be implemented with available time/resources?
- **measurability:** will implementation produce evidence against objectives?

### 3. Campaign-plan audit — 25 minutes

Learners audit a neutral practice plan or their own planning evidence as permitted by assessment conditions. They identify gaps, contradictions, unsupported choices, duplicated activity and unmeasurable objectives.

### 4. Gathering stakeholder feedback — 20 minutes

Teach purposeful feedback questions. Contrast:

Weak: **“Do you like my plan?”**  
Stronger: **“Does the planned content make the intended action clear to the target audience? Identify one example and one improvement.”**

Discuss stakeholder relevance and the need to record feedback accurately.

### 5. Feedback exchange — 25 minutes

Learners use the stakeholder feedback form. Feedback should address requirements, audience, strategy, practicality, policy/community arrangements and measurement rather than spelling/appearance alone.

### Break — 10 minutes

### 6. Prioritise and refine — 30 minutes

Learners classify feedback using:

**impact on requirement/objective × evidence/validity × feasibility/constraint**.

They decide to accept, adapt or reject each significant suggestion and record the reasoning.

### 7. DojoLearn final configuration check — 25 minutes

Learners compare the refined plan with the actual simulation configuration. Verify only options available in DojoLearn. Capture the final pre-implementation state and note simulator limitations.

### 8. Distinction reasoning workshop — 20 minutes

Using a neutral scenario, learners move from “I changed it because someone suggested it” towards:

**issue/feedback → requirement → alternatives → consequence → decision → justified judgement**.

Clarify that BC.D3-level thinking requires evaluative judgement across planning/implementation evidence; Week 11 develops the reasoning but does not fabricate later performance evidence.

### 9. Exit ticket — 15 minutes

Learners identify one meaningful refinement, one piece of feedback they would not automatically accept and one final implementation-readiness check.

## Adaptive teaching

**Support:** audit headings, feedback question stems, accept/adapt/reject structure and worked neutral examples.  
**Stretch:** conflicting stakeholder feedback, opportunity cost, dependencies between changes, unintended consequences and justification for retaining an original decision.

## English, maths, EDI and employability

**English:** precise feedback, professional review comments and evidence-based justification.  
**Maths:** checking consistency of baselines, targets, timescales and campaign measures.  
**EDI:** seek feedback on accessibility/inclusive communication and avoid treating one stakeholder's preferences as representative of the entire target audience.  
**Employability:** peer/client review, quality assurance, change control, stakeholder communication and implementation readiness.

## Teacher reflection

- Are learners reviewing against requirements rather than taste?
- Is feedback specific enough to act on?
- Can learners reject feedback with evidence rather than defensiveness?
- Are refinements traceable?
- Is DojoLearn configuration aligned with the final plan?
