# Week 11 Lesson Plan — Reviewing and Refining the Plan

**Duration:** 3 hours  
**Learning Aim:** B  
**Specification:** B6  
**Criteria preparation:** B.P4 / B.M2

## Learning outcomes

Learners will review a campaign plan systematically, gather stakeholder feedback, document refinements and prepare the final DojoLearn implementation configuration.

## Lesson sequence

1. **Retrieval and planning reconstruction — 15 min:** requirements → audience/objectives → platform/content → publishing → community/policy → measures.
2. **Review is more than proofreading — 20 min:** requirements, coherence, feasibility and measurability lenses.
3. **Campaign-plan audit — 25 min:** identify gaps, contradictions, unsupported choices and measurement weaknesses.
4. **Stakeholder feedback — 20 min:** create focused questions tied to requirements rather than preference.
5. **Feedback exchange — 25 min:** record specific evidence-based feedback.
6. **Break — 10 min.**
7. **Prioritise/refine — 30 min:** accept, adapt or reject/retain using impact, validity and feasibility.
8. **DojoLearn final configuration — 25 min:** align the refined plan with simulator settings and capture the final pre-implementation baseline.
9. **Justification workshop — 20 min:** issue/feedback → requirement → alternatives → consequence → decision → justified judgement.
10. **Exit — 15 min:** identify meaningful refinements and implementation-readiness actions.

## Assessment relationship

- **B.P4:** review the plan with others to identify and inform improvements.
- **B.M2:** justify planning decisions and significant refinements against purpose/business requirements.
- **BC.D3:** not a Week 11 evaluation criterion. However, authentic evidence of individual responsibility, creativity and self-management may begin/continue here through independent review decisions, organisation and response to feedback.

## Assessment boundary

Teachers may provide review structures and questions but should not decide which feedback the learner must accept or write assessed justifications.
