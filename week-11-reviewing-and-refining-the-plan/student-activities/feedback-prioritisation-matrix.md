## Prioritisation questions

1. Does the feedback expose failure to meet an important requirement?
2. Is it based on relevant evidence or mainly preference?
3. Would the proposed change improve the intended audience outcome?
4. Is the change feasible within time/resources/platform limitations?
5. Would the change create a new problem elsewhere?
6. Is there a better adaptation than accepting the suggestion exactly as given?

## Conflicting feedback

When reviewers disagree, do not vote automatically. Compare each suggestion with the requirements, audience evidence, campaign objectives and constraints.
