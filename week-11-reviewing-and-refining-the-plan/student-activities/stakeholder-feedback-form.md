## Review questions

1. What do you understand the campaign is trying to achieve?
2. Which element most clearly supports that purpose? Give evidence.
3. Is the intended audience clear from the plan? Identify evidence or a gap.
4. Do the selected platform/content choices appear appropriate to the stated audience/objectives? Explain.
5. Is the intended audience action/call to action clear?
6. Is the planned level of content/interaction realistic for the available resources?
7. Are response/moderation arrangements sufficiently clear?
8. Can the proposed measures show whether the objectives have been achieved?
9. Identify one significant risk, contradiction or missing element.
10. Recommend one specific improvement and explain the likely benefit.

## Feedback quality

Useful feedback is **specific, evidenced and connected to the campaign purpose**. Avoid comments that only express personal taste.
