# Higher-Level Planning Reasoning Practice

**Neutral formative exercise — supports B.M2 and later BC.D2 reasoning.**

A fictional local learning centre plans a four-week campaign. Reviewer A recommends daily posting on every platform; Reviewer B recommends fewer posts on the strongest-fit channels because staff time is limited.

## Task

Evaluate the feedback **as planning evidence** and decide how the plan should be refined.

Use:

**feedback → business requirement → alternatives → likely consequence → decision → justification**

Address objective relevance, resource consequences, audience fit and evidence still needed.

## BC.D3 note

The learner's own responsible, creative and self-managed handling of review activity can later contribute authentic BC.D3 evidence, but BC.D3 is not itself the evaluative judgement being practised here.
