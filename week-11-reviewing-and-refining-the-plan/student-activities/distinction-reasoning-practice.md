# Distinction Reasoning Practice — Review and Refinement

**Neutral formative exercise — not a completed Assignment 2 response.**

## Scenario

A fictional local learning centre plans a four-week campaign to increase awareness of free evening digital-skills workshops and encourage visits to a registration-information page.

Two reviewers provide conflicting feedback:

- **Reviewer A:** “Post every day on all available platforms so as many people as possible see it.”
- **Reviewer B:** “Use fewer planned posts on the two strongest-fit platforms because staff only have limited time to create content and respond to users.”

The campaign objective prioritises relevant local awareness and information-page visits. Staff can monitor social media for only a limited period each working day.

## Task

Evaluate the feedback and decide how the plan should be refined.

Use:

**issue/feedback → requirement → alternatives → consequence → decision → justified judgement**

Address:

- relevance of each reviewer's suggestion;
- likely benefits;
- resource/interaction consequences;
- relationship to the objective;
- what additional evidence would strengthen the decision;
- whether you would accept, adapt or reject each suggestion;
- your overall judgement.

## Important distinction

This exercise develops evaluative reasoning towards **BC.D3**. Do not claim that a refined plan “worked” unless implementation/performance evidence exists.
