## Orphan/contradiction check

Identify:

- requirements with no planned activity;
- activities with no requirement/objective;
- objectives with no useful measure;
- measures with no decision purpose;
- content that does not fit the intended audience/platform;
- community activity with insufficient response resource;
- policy controls that conflict with planned activity;
- inconsistent dates, responsibilities or targets.

## Audit judgement

What are the three highest-priority issues to resolve before implementation? Rank them and explain why.
