# Week 3 Teacher Notes

## Curriculum intent

Week 3 develops A.P2 while preparing the conceptual bridge to A.M1. Learners need more than demographic vocabulary: they need to understand how evidence about an audience affects business/platform decisions.

## Specification fidelity

The existing Unit 3 framework identifies audience profiles in A1 and business-use considerations in A2. The planned Week 3 emphasis is age, gender, interests and income, with learners considering why different platforms appeal to different audiences.

## Avoiding stereotypes

This is a central teaching issue. Statements such as “young people use X” or “women prefer Y” should be challenged unless appropriately qualified and supported by evidence.

Encourage language such as:

- “The supplied data suggests…”
- “A higher proportion of…”
- “This could make the platform relevant when…”

rather than absolute claims about individuals.

## Evidence literacy

Audience data can measure different things. Encourage learners to identify whether evidence concerns registered users, active users, frequency, survey responses, advertising reach or another measure. The source must support the claim being made.

Because social-media platforms evolve, current research is preferable when making contemporary claims. Teacher-provided datasets should therefore be refreshed when necessary rather than hard-coded into these repository resources.

## Maths opportunities

Use audience tables/charts to reinforce percentage and proportion interpretation. Ask learners to compare percentage-point differences and identify misleading interpretations where appropriate.

## DojoLearn

The simulation provides platform characteristics but should not be treated as a source of real-world demographic statistics unless the simulation explicitly supplies such data. The practical therefore separates:

**what the simulated platform enables** from **what real-world audience evidence would be needed**.

This distinction is pedagogically useful and reduces invented demographic claims.

## Assessment boundary

Do not supply learners with the exact platform research or completed audience-profile explanations they intend to use in Assignment 1. The formative A.P2 exercise should use practice data/examples so that learners learn the method but retain ownership of assessed research and writing.

## Preparation for Week 4

Week 4 should combine A1 and A2 thinking. Learners move from “who uses the platform?” to assessing **different ways a business can use social media to attract a target audience**, introducing the judgement and comparative reasoning required for A.M1.
