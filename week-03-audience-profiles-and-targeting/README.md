# Week 3 — Audience Profiles and Targeting

**Specification focus:** Learning Aim A — A1/A2  
**Assessment preparation:** A.P2, with links back to A.P1 and forward to A.M1  
**Practical environment:** DojoLearn social-media simulation

## Week purpose

Week 3 shifts attention from what businesses can do with social media to **who they are trying to reach**. Learners investigate audience profiles, including age, gender, interests and income, and explain why different social-media websites attract different audiences.

The lesson emphasises evidence-based audience reasoning rather than stereotypes. Learners should recognise that demographic characteristics can help describe an audience but do not determine every individual's behaviour.

## Learning outcomes

Learners should be able to:

1. Define target audience and audience profile accurately.
2. Explain how age, gender, interests and income can contribute to an audience profile.
3. Explain why different social-media websites may appeal to different audiences.
4. Use research/data appropriately when making claims about platform audiences.
5. Match business/audience scenarios to suitable DojoLearn platforms and justify decisions.
6. Produce formative A.P2-style explanations comparing audience profiles.

## Learning progression

**characteristic → evidence → audience implication → platform suitability → business relevance**

## DojoLearn practical

Learners work with audience personas and select simulated platforms that could plausibly reach them. The important evidence is the learner's reasoning, not finding one predetermined “correct” platform.
