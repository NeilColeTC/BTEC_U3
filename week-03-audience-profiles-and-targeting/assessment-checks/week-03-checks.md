# Week 3 — Assessment and Understanding Checks

## Primary progression

**A.P2 preparation:** explain the audience profiles of different social-media websites.

## Core checks

By the end of Week 3, learners should be able to:

- [ ] define target audience;
- [ ] define audience profile;
- [ ] explain the relevance of age, gender, interests and income;
- [ ] distinguish an evidence-supported audience claim from an assumption;
- [ ] identify why source/date/context matter;
- [ ] explain an audience profile rather than simply list statistics;
- [ ] identify similarities and differences between platform audiences;
- [ ] explain why an audience difference could matter to a business;
- [ ] avoid treating demographic trends as universal truths;
- [ ] select a plausible DojoLearn platform for a persona and justify the decision using characteristics;
- [ ] identify additional real-world evidence required before making a genuine platform recommendation.

## A.P2 explanation quality

### Secure
Learner uses evidence to explain relevant audience characteristics, interprets what the evidence suggests, compares platforms and avoids unsupported generalisation.

### Developing
Learner identifies appropriate characteristics/statistics but explanation of significance is limited or comparisons are mostly descriptive.

### Requires intervention
Learner lists demographic labels without evidence, stereotypes audience groups or cannot connect audience information to platform/business relevance.

## Common misconceptions

- [ ] every user of a platform fits its “typical” profile;
- [ ] one demographic statistic describes the whole audience;
- [ ] percentages can be compared without understanding what population they refer to;
- [ ] personal experience is sufficient evidence of a platform's audience;
- [ ] platform audiences never change;
- [ ] two platforms cannot share the same users;
- [ ] a persona should contain invented detail simply to make it look realistic.

## Formative evidence

Review retrieval quiz, audience-profile builder, platform-audience comparison, DojoLearn matching task, A.P2 practice and exit ticket.

## Progression to Week 4

Learners ready for A.M1 should be able to combine:

**business use (A.P1) + target audience/profile (A.P2) + platform/content approach + evidence + reasoned judgement**.

Learners who cannot yet explain A.P1 or A.P2 securely should receive targeted retrieval/scaffolding before the Week 4 assessment-level work.
