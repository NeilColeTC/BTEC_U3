## Explain the evidence

For two platforms write:

1. What does the evidence suggest about the audience profile?
2. Which claims are strongly supported?
3. Which claims would require more evidence?
4. What important similarities/overlap exist?
5. What differences could matter to a business?

## Source check

For each source consider:

- Who produced the data?
- When was it produced?
- What population/sample does it represent?
- Does it measure users, usage frequency, advertising audience or something else?
- Is your conclusion actually supported by the data?

## Challenge

Identify one statement that would **overstate** the evidence and rewrite it more accurately.
