# A.P2 Formative Practice — Audience Profiles

**This is practice, not Assignment 1 evidence.**

Use the practice audience data supplied by your teacher for at least two social-media websites/platform types.

## Task

Explain the audience profile of each platform. Where supported by the evidence, consider:

- age;
- gender;
- interests;
- income.

For each important point use:

**characteristic → evidence → interpretation → why it matters**

Then explain at least one meaningful difference and one overlap between the platform audiences.

## Quality checklist

- [ ] I have explained rather than simply listed demographic statistics.
- [ ] My claims are supported by the supplied evidence.
- [ ] I have not treated a demographic trend as true for every individual.
- [ ] I have identified the source/date of evidence.
- [ ] I have explained why an audience characteristic could matter.
- [ ] I have compared different platform audiences.

## Stretch

Explain one limitation of the evidence and what additional information would make your audience profile more reliable.
