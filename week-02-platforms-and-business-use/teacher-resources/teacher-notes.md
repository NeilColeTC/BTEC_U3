# Week 2 Teacher Notes

## Curriculum intent

Week 2 is the first substantial A.P1 preparation lesson. The central teaching move is from **naming uses** to **explaining business value**.

## A1 fidelity

Keep teaching anchored to the A1 areas in the course specification: branding/image, product/service promotion, communication, customer service/query handling, advertising, links to websites/mobile apps, SEO, business profiles and usage data/analytics.

Avoid over-expanding into detailed audience demographics (Week 3), risks/issues (later Learning Aim A work), or campaign planning (Learning Aim B).

## Explanation model

Repeatedly use:

**What does the business do? → How does social media enable it? → What aim/need does this support? → Why could this be beneficial? → Example/context**

A learner who merely adds examples to a list has not necessarily explained the use.

## SEO nuance

Teach SEO as part of the business's wider online visibility/discoverability strategy. Avoid simplistic claims that posting on social media automatically or directly guarantees improved search ranking.

## Analytics nuance

At this stage learners need to understand why usage data exists and how it can support decisions. Detailed metrics, campaign analysis and optimisation are developed later.

## DojoLearn

Use the simulation to make A1 concrete. Learners should infer business opportunities from observed features. The desired reasoning is:

**feature → possible activity → business objective → likely value/limitation**.

Do not require learners to identify a single real-world commercial equivalent for each simulated platform.

## Adaptive teaching

Support learners with vocabulary, worked examples and causal sentence stems. Stretch learners through comparison, limitations and trade-offs.

## Assessment boundary

The fictional activity-centre exercise deliberately differs from the Assignment 1 scenario. It teaches the structure of an A.P1 explanation without giving learners a response they can simply transplant into assessed work.

## Preparation for Week 3

Retrieve A1 business uses, then shift attention to the people businesses are trying to reach: audience profiles, demographics, interests, income and platform targeting.
