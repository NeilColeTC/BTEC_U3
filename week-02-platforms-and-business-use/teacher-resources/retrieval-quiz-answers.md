# Week 2 Retrieval Quiz — Indicative Answers

1. **Business aim:** broad outcome a business wants to achieve.
2. **Target audience:** group the business intends to reach.
3. **Brand:** identity/image associated with a business/product/service.
4. Examples: structure, content formats, interaction methods, target audience, communication style.
5. Business use is purposeful organisational communication and carries professional/reputational responsibilities; personal use is primarily on the individual's own behalf.
6. Examples: branding, promotion, communication, customer service, query resolution.
7. Suitability depends on business needs, audience and platform characteristics, not size/popularity alone.
8. Interaction with an account/content.
9. Data/tools used to understand audience/activity/performance.
10. Accept justified answers referring to aims, audience, requirements or intended communication.

Use answers diagnostically rather than as assessment-ready wording.
