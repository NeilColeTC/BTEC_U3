# Week 2 Key Terminology

| Term | Working definition | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Brand image | The identity/impression a business communicates to its audience. | Social-media content and communication can reinforce how a business is perceived. |
| Promotion | Activity intended to increase awareness or interest in products/services. | A core A1 business use. |
| Customer communication | Exchange of information between a business and customers. | Social media enables both publication and interaction. |
| Customer service | Support provided before, during or after purchase/use. | Platforms can support questions, updates and issue handling. |
| Query resolution | Responding to and addressing customer questions/problems. | Public or direct responses can affect customer experience and reputation. |
| Advertising | Paid promotional communication intended to reach an audience. | Can extend reach and support selected business objectives. |
| Business profile | An organisational presence/account containing business information and content. | Provides an identifiable base for publishing and interaction. |
| Integration/link | A connection between social media and another business service such as a website or mobile application. | Helps social activity contribute to wider customer journeys/actions. |
| SEO | Search-engine optimisation: practices intended to improve the visibility/discoverability of online content in search. | A1 places social media within a wider online-presence context. |
| Usage data | Information about how a platform/account/content is being used. | Provides evidence that can inform decisions. |
| Analytics | Tools/processes used to examine performance and audience/interaction data. | Later used extensively to analyse and optimise campaign performance. |
| Business objective | A specific outcome the business wants activity to contribute towards. | Platform/content choices should be justified against objectives. |
| Call to action | A prompt encouraging an audience to take a desired next step. | Helps connect communication with a business/customer action. |

These are teaching definitions, not ready-made assignment responses.
