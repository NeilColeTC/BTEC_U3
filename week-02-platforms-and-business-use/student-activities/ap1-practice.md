# A.P1 Formative Practice — Explaining Business Uses

**This is practice, not Assignment 1 evidence.**

## Scenario

A fictional independent activity centre offers climbing, cycling and outdoor-skills sessions. It wants to improve awareness of its services, communicate effectively with customers and develop a stronger online presence.

## Task

Explain **at least three different ways** the business could use social media.

For each explanation include:

- the business use;
- how the activity would work;
- the business aim/need it supports;
- why it could benefit the activity centre;
- an appropriate example of the type of content/action involved.

Include at least one idea connected to the wider online presence, such as a business profile, website/mobile link, advertising, discoverability or analytics.

## Self/peer checklist

- [ ] I have explained different uses rather than repeatedly describing promotion.
- [ ] I have explained **how** each use works.
- [ ] I have linked each use to a business aim/need.
- [ ] I have explained a likely benefit.
- [ ] My examples support my explanation.
- [ ] I have used relevant Unit 3 terminology.

## Stretch

For one use, explain a limitation or condition that would affect how useful it is.
