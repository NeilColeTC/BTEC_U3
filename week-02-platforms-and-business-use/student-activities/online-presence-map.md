# Activity — Mapping a Business Online Presence

A business's social-media account does not necessarily work in isolation.

Create a diagram with **the business/customer relationship** at the centre and connect:

- social-media profile;
- business website;
- mobile application;
- search/discovery;
- advertising;
- published content;
- customer communication;
- analytics/usage data.

For each connection annotate:

1. what information/action can move between the elements;
2. what business purpose the connection supports.

## Questions

1. Why might a business direct a social-media user to its website?
2. What is the role of a business profile?
3. Why can discoverability matter to a business?
4. What can usage data help a business understand?
5. Why is collecting data not useful unless it informs a decision?

## Challenge

Trace a possible customer journey from discovering social content to completing a useful business action. Identify where the business could measure performance.
