## Objective matching

Recommend a platform or combination of platforms for each objective. Justify using **observed features**, not assumptions.

1. Build a recognisable visual brand.
2. Publish frequent customer updates.
3. Showcase richer media explaining a product/service.
4. Encourage discovery/saving of visual ideas.
5. Maintain ongoing customer/community communication.

## Decision challenge

Choose one objective for which **two** platforms are defensible. Compare them and recommend one, identifying a trade-off.
