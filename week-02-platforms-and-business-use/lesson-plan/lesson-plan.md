# Week 2 Lesson Plan — Social Media Platforms and Business Use

**Duration:** 3 hours  
**Learning Aim:** A  
**Specification:** A1  
**Primary criterion preparation:** A.P1

## Learning outcomes

Learners will explain core business uses of social media, connect those uses to business aims, investigate supporting online-presence features, and justify suitable DojoLearn platform choices.

## Lesson sequence

### 1. Retrieval from Week 1 — 15 minutes

Complete the retrieval quiz covering business aim, target audience, brand, platform characteristics and personal/business use. Review misconceptions from Week 1 exit tickets.

### 2. From listing to explaining — 20 minutes

Model the difference between:

- **identify:** “A business can use social media for customer service.”
- **describe:** “A business can respond to customer questions through its account.”
- **explain:** “A business can respond directly to customer questions through its account, helping customers obtain information quickly and potentially improving their experience of the business.”

Establish the writing frame: **use → mechanism → benefit → context/example**.

### 3. Core business uses — 40 minutes

Teach and question around:

- image/brand;
- promotion of products/services;
- customer communication;
- customer service;
- resolving queries and issues;
- advertising.

Learners complete the business-use explainer activity.

**Check:** learners must explain a benefit rather than merely repeat the activity.

### 4. Connected online presence — 30 minutes

Introduce:

- links to business websites;
- links/integration with mobile applications;
- search-engine optimisation;
- business profiles;
- usage data and analytics.

Learners construct an online-presence map showing how these elements can work together.

### Break — 10 minutes

### 5. DojoLearn business-feature audit — 40 minutes

Learners investigate the six simulated platforms and identify features that could support particular business uses. They record evidence and match features to objectives.

Require reasoning from observed feature to business value; avoid simplistic “platform X is best” conclusions.

### 6. A.P1 explanation practice — 30 minutes

Learners respond to a neutral practice business scenario. They explain at least three different ways social media could be used and connect each to a business aim/need.

Peer review using the explanation checklist. This is formative practice, not assignment evidence.

### 7. Exit ticket — 15 minutes

Learners independently explain one business use, one connected online-presence feature, and one justified DojoLearn platform choice.

## Adaptive teaching

**Support:** vocabulary bank, worked explanation, sentence stems, chunked business-use cards and paired DojoLearn investigation.  
**Stretch:** compare two approaches, identify limitations/trade-offs, and explain how two social-media uses could reinforce each other.

## English, maths, EDI and employability

**English:** causal explanation using because/therefore/which means; professional terminology.  
**Maths:** interpretation of the purpose of usage/analytics data; detailed numerical analysis follows later.  
**EDI:** avoid assuming a platform or communication style reaches all customers equally; accessibility and audience diversity should inform business choices.  
**Employability:** customer communication, branding awareness, digital marketing reasoning, evidence-based decision making.

## Teacher reflection

- Can learners distinguish a business use from its benefit?
- Are explanations causal rather than lists?
- Can learners connect platform features to objectives?
- Do learners understand analytics as decision-support data rather than merely “likes”?
- Which concepts need retrieval in Week 3?
