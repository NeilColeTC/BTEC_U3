# Week 2 — Social Media Platforms and Business Use

**Specification focus:** Learning Aim A — A1 Social media websites  
**Assessment preparation:** explicit development towards A.P1  
**Practical environment:** DojoLearn social-media simulation

## Week purpose

Week 2 develops the introductory ideas from Week 1 into the detailed business uses identified in A1. Learners move from identifying that businesses use social media to **explaining how and why** particular uses can support business aims and needs.

## Learning outcomes

Learners should be able to:

1. Explain how businesses use social media to create an image or brand.
2. Explain how businesses promote products and services and communicate with customers.
3. Explain customer-service and query-resolution uses.
4. Describe advertising, website/mobile integration and search-engine optimisation as parts of a wider online presence.
5. Explain the value of business profiles and platform analytics.
6. Match DojoLearn platform features to realistic business objectives and justify choices.

## A1 coverage

This week develops:

- creating an image or brand;
- promoting products and services;
- communicating with customers;
- customer service and resolving queries/issues;
- advertising;
- links between social media and business websites/mobile applications;
- search-engine optimisation;
- business profiles;
- usage data and analytics.

## Learning progression

**identify → describe → explain → apply → justify**

Learners should increasingly write in the form:

**business use → how it works → business benefit → relevant example/context**

## DojoLearn practical

Learners conduct a business-feature audit across The Wall, The Pulse, The Grid, The Metronome, The Pin and The Tube, then recommend suitable simulated platforms for different business objectives.
