# Week 2 — Assessment and Understanding Checks

## Primary progression

**A.P1 preparation:** explain different ways in which a business can use social media.

## Core checks

By the end of Week 2, learners should be able to:

- [ ] explain branding/image as a social-media business use;
- [ ] explain promotion of products/services;
- [ ] explain customer communication;
- [ ] explain customer service and query/issue resolution;
- [ ] recognise advertising as a distinct business use;
- [ ] explain the role of business profiles;
- [ ] explain how social media can connect with websites/mobile applications;
- [ ] describe the relevance of discoverability/SEO;
- [ ] explain why usage data/analytics can support business decisions;
- [ ] connect a DojoLearn feature to a business objective;
- [ ] write an explanation rather than a list of uses.

## Explanation quality

### Secure
Learner consistently links **use → how → business aim/need → benefit**, with relevant context/examples.

### Developing
Learner identifies appropriate uses and some benefits but causal links are incomplete or repetitive.

### Requires intervention
Learner mainly lists features/uses, confuses business activity with benefit, or bases platform decisions on personal preference.

## Common misconceptions

- [ ] promotion and advertising are identical concepts;
- [ ] social media is separate from the wider online presence;
- [ ] SEO means simply adding hashtags;
- [ ] analytics means only counting likes;
- [ ] creating an account automatically builds a brand;
- [ ] customer service is only private messaging;
- [ ] more activity automatically means better business performance.

## Formative evidence

Review retrieval quiz, business-use explainer, online-presence map, DojoLearn feature audit, A.P1 practice and exit ticket.

## Assessment boundary

These materials build the knowledge and explanation skills needed for A.P1. They should not be treated as a completed Assignment 1 submission or a teacher-provided model response to the assessed scenario.
