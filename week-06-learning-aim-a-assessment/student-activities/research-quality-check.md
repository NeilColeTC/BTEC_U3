## Questions

### Authority
Who produced the information? Are they in a position to know?

### Relevance
Does the source directly support the claim you intend to make?

### Currency
Does the date matter for this claim? Platform/audience information can change.

### Evidence
Does the source show where its figures/claims come from?

### Purpose
Is the source informing, researching, selling, persuading or promoting? How might that affect interpretation?

## Strong research habits

Prefer original/first-party evidence where appropriate, official documentation, reputable research organisations and sources whose methods/data can be inspected. Do not treat a search-engine result snippet as the source.

## Referencing check

Can another person identify and locate the source from your reference? If not, improve the reference before submission.
