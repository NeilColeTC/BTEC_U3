## Progression questions

1. What does A.M1 require that goes beyond Pass-level explanation?
2. What does A.D1 require that goes beyond Merit-level assessment?
3. Which lower-level knowledge must remain accurate for higher-level reasoning to be convincing?
4. Where might one piece of research support reasoning relevant to more than one criterion without simply repeating text?
