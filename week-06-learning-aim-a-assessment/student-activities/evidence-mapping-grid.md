## Evidence audit

For each item ask:

- Is it relevant to the criterion?
- Is it specific enough to support my claim?
- Is the source credible?
- Is the evidence current enough for the claim?
- Can I trace it back to the original source?
- Have I explained what the evidence means rather than simply inserted it?

## Reasoning audit

Mark each planned/drafted section:

- **D** — description/listing;
- **E** — explanation;
- **A** — assessment/comparison;
- **V** — evaluation/judgement.

Does the reasoning level match the criterion you are trying to evidence?
