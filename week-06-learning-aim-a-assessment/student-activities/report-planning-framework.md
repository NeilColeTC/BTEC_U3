# Activity — Independent Report Planning Framework

This framework organises your thinking. **Do not treat it as a model answer or compulsory report structure.**

## Context and purpose

- What does the assignment ask you to produce?
- Who is the intended audience for the report?
- What business/social-media context must your report address?

## Business uses

- Which different business uses are relevant to your independently selected/researched examples?
- Have you explained how and why each use matters?
- What evidence supports factual claims?

## Audience and attraction

- What evidence identifies or characterises the relevant target audience?
- How do platform/content/engagement choices relate to that audience?
- Which alternatives can you meaningfully compare?
- What strengths and limitations matter in this context?

## Interaction, promotion and risk

- What significant benefits arise from customer interaction and promotion?
- Which risks/issues are most relevant?
- What consequences could they create?
- Can those risks be managed or reduced?
- How does mitigation affect your judgement?

## Conclusions

- Does each judgement follow from evidence already discussed?
- Have you weighed significance rather than simply summarised points?
- Is your conclusion contextual rather than absolute?

## Final planning question

What is the logical order that best communicates **your** argument to the intended reader?
