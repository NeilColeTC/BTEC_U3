# Week 6 Lesson Plan — Learning Aim A Assessment Workshop

**Duration:** 3 hours  
**Criteria:** A.P1, A.P2, A.M1, A.D1  
**Mode:** assessment workshop / supervised independent production

## Learning outcomes

Learners will interpret the Learning Aim A criteria, audit their research and planning, then make sustained independent progress on the Chamber of Commerce report.

## Lesson sequence

### 1. Learning Aim A retrieval — 15 minutes

Complete the cumulative retrieval activity covering A1, A2 and A3. Review only misconceptions that affect learners' ability to begin the assessment confidently.

### 2. Criteria and command-term briefing — 20 minutes

Learners complete the criteria decoder. Revisit:

- **explain** — make clear how/why;
- **assess** — consider relevant strengths, limitations and context to reach supported judgements;
- **evaluate** — weigh significance and competing factors to reach a reasoned overall conclusion.

Do not translate these into business-specific answers.

### 3. Evidence-mapping audit — 25 minutes

Learners map their existing notes/research against A.P1, A.P2, A.M1 and A.D1. They identify:

- evidence already available;
- evidence needing stronger sourcing;
- areas requiring further research;
- where comparison/evaluation is currently missing.

Teacher checks should ask learners to show where evidence exists rather than suggesting what they should write.

### 4. Research quality and referencing check — 20 minutes

Learners review the credibility, relevance and currency of their sources and ensure they can trace claims to evidence. Where possible, encourage first-party/platform/business evidence and reputable research rather than unsupported blogs or search-result snippets.

### 5. Report planning — 20 minutes

Learners use the planning framework to organise their own report. The framework provides questions and coverage prompts, not headings that must be copied or pre-written content.

### Break — 10 minutes

### 6. Independent assessed production — 60 minutes

Learners work independently on Assignment 1.

Teacher role:

- monitor progress;
- clarify wording of the assignment/criteria where permitted;
- ask learners to locate evidence in their own work;
- remind learners about source attribution and command terms;
- record progress/concerns.

Avoid supplying sentences, conclusions, business-specific analysis or corrections that would become assessed evidence.

### 7. Submission-readiness audit — 20 minutes

Learners independently use the readiness checklist. They identify their own next actions and prioritise them.

### 8. Progress record / exit — 10 minutes

Learners record:

- what they completed;
- which criterion is currently strongest;
- which criterion needs most attention;
- specific research/evidence still needed;
- their next independent action.

## Adaptive teaching

Support should reduce barriers to **process**, not reduce the intellectual demand of the assessment. Appropriate support includes chunking time, criterion checklists, vocabulary/command-term reminders, assistive technology and organisational templates. Avoid scaffolds containing assessable answers.

## English, maths, EDI and employability

**English:** formal report organisation, analytical/evaluative writing and source attribution.  
**Maths:** accurate interpretation of any audience/usage evidence selected by learners.  
**EDI:** learners should question stereotypes and represent audiences responsibly when interpreting evidence.  
**Employability:** independent research, report writing, deadline management, evidence-based decision making and quality assurance.

## Teacher reflection

- Were learners able to locate their own evidence against each criterion?
- Did support remain process-focused?
- Which command-term misconceptions remain?
- Are learners relying on weak/untraceable sources?
- Is additional independent assessment time required?
