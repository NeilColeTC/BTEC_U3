# Week 6 Slides — Learning Aim A Assessment Workshop

## Slide 1 — Week 6
### Learning Aim A Assessment Workshop
Today: consolidate, audit, plan and produce independent assessed work.

---
## Slide 2 — Criteria
**A.P1 • A.P2 • A.M1 • A.D1**

---
## Slide 3 — No new content
Weeks 1–5 developed the knowledge and reasoning. Today is about applying it independently to Assignment 1.

---
## Slide 4 — Retrieval
Can you explain the major ideas from A1, A2 and A3 without notes?

---
## Slide 5 — Explain
Make clear **how and why**. A list of points or features is not sufficient explanation.

---
## Slide 6 — Assess
Consider relevant strengths, limitations and context. Compare where appropriate and reach supported judgements.

---
## Slide 7 — Evaluate
Weigh significance and competing factors. Reach a reasoned overall conclusion supported by the preceding analysis.

---
## Slide 8 — More words ≠ higher grade
Merit and Distinction require different reasoning, not simply longer Pass-level descriptions.

---
## Slide 9 — Evidence first
Before writing, ask: What claim am I making? What evidence supports it? Is that evidence relevant and credible?

---
## Slide 10 — Audience evidence
Avoid stereotypes and unsupported assumptions. Audience/platform claims should be traceable to appropriate evidence.

---
## Slide 11 — Research quality
Check **authority, relevance, currency, evidence and purpose**.

---
## Slide 12 — Traceability
Can you return to the original source for every important factual claim?

---
## Slide 13 — Evidence mapping
For each criterion identify: what I already have → what is missing → what needs stronger evidence → what reasoning remains.

---
## Slide 14 — A.P1 check
Have you explained different ways businesses can use social media rather than merely listing them?

---
## Slide 15 — A.P2 check
Have you explained audience-related/platform considerations using evidence rather than assumptions?

---
## Slide 16 — A.M1 check
Have you assessed different ways of attracting the target audience, including contextual strengths/limitations and supported judgement?

---
## Slide 17 — A.D1 check
Have you evaluated interaction and promotion, weighing significant benefits and risks/issues and reaching a reasoned conclusion?

---
## Slide 18 — Coherence
A strong report can connect business purpose, audience, platform/content choices, opportunities and risks rather than repeating the same information under disconnected headings.

---
## Slide 19 — Independent work
The planning tools organise your thinking. They do not supply the answer.

---
## Slide 20 — Teacher support
Your teacher can clarify the task/process and help you identify gaps. Your assessed reasoning and wording must remain your own.

---
## Slide 21 — Quality check
Accuracy • relevance • evidence • explanation • comparison • evaluation • conclusion • referencing.

---
## Slide 22 — Prioritise
Do not spend equal time everywhere. Identify the most important gap in your own evidence and address it.

---
## Slide 23 — Final audit
Use the submission-readiness checklist before declaring a criterion complete.

---
## Slide 24 — Progress record
What have you completed? What remains? What evidence do you still need? What is your next action?

---
## Slide 25 — Next stage
Learning Aim B moves from understanding business use of social media into **planning a social-media campaign**.
