# Week 6 Teacher Notes — Learning Aim A Assessment Workshop

## Curriculum intent

Week 6 is an assessment-production workshop, not another content lesson. The repository scaffold states that learners consolidate Weeks 1–5 and independently produce the Assignment 1 Chamber of Commerce report.

## Core principle

**Support the process; do not supply the assessed response.**

The materials therefore focus on criterion interpretation, evidence mapping, research quality, organisation and self-audit.

## Suggested teacher questioning

Appropriate prompts include:

- “Which criterion are you currently working towards?”
- “Where is the evidence for that claim?”
- “What does the command term require?”
- “Which source supports this?”
- “How have you compared those alternatives?”
- “What makes that factor significant?”
- “Does your conclusion follow from your analysis?”
- “What is your next action?”

Avoid turning prompts into answers, e.g. telling learners which business use to select, which platform to recommend, what conclusion to reach or providing criterion-ready sentences.

## Feedback boundary

Feedback during assessed production should identify the nature/location of a gap and direct the learner back to the criterion, specification, their research or taught concepts. It should not rewrite work or prescribe the substantive assessed response.

## Evidence quality

Encourage learners to use evidence appropriate to the claim. Platform functionality may be supported by official documentation; audience/usage claims require credible evidence; business examples should be traceable. Currency matters particularly where platforms and audience behaviours change.

## Use of previous teaching resources

Weeks 1–5 remain available for retrieval of concepts, terminology and generic formative examples. Learners should not copy practice scenarios as if they were research evidence for the assignment.

## Adaptive teaching

Permissible support can include:

- breaking the workshop into timed chunks;
- command-term reminders;
- criterion/evidence checklists;
- organisational frameworks;
- accessibility tools;
- reminders to cite sources;
- asking learners to verbalise their reasoning before independently writing it.

The same assessment standard must remain.

## Academic integrity and AI

Apply centre and awarding-organisation requirements. Learners should understand that generative tools must not replace independently produced assessed evidence where this is not permitted. If tools are allowed for limited support, their use should remain within the centre's assessment/authentication rules.

## Progress monitoring

Record whether each learner:

- has research sufficient to proceed;
- understands the command terms;
- has mapped evidence to criteria;
- is making independent progress;
- has identifiable gaps;
- needs additional supervised assessment time.

## Transition to Learning Aim B

Once Learning Aim A production is complete, Week 7 moves into campaign aims, objectives and requirements. Preserve the distinction between **analysing existing/business social-media use** in Learning Aim A and **planning a campaign** in Learning Aim B.
