# Week 6 — Learning Aim A Assessment Workshop

**Criteria:** A.P1, A.P2, A.M1, A.D1  
**Purpose:** consolidation, evidence planning, research quality and independent Assignment 1 production

## Week purpose

Week 6 is deliberately different from Weeks 1–5. No new Learning Aim A content is introduced. Learners consolidate their understanding, interpret the assessment criteria, audit their evidence and independently produce the Assignment 1 Chamber of Commerce report.

Teaching support should focus on **process rather than assessed content**: interpreting command terms, planning structure, checking coverage, selecting credible evidence, referencing sources, monitoring progress and asking non-leading questions.

## Learning outcomes

Learners should be able to:

1. Distinguish the demands of A.P1, A.P2, A.M1 and A.D1.
2. Map independently researched evidence to relevant criteria.
3. Plan a coherent report without treating criteria as four disconnected tasks.
4. Select relevant and credible research evidence.
5. Use explanation, assessment and evaluation appropriately.
6. Audit their own work for coverage, reasoning and evidence gaps.
7. Produce independent assessed work within the permitted support boundaries.

## Criterion progression

**A.P1/A.P2 — explain**  
**A.M1 — assess**  
**A.D1 — evaluate**

Higher-level evidence should build upon accurate underlying knowledge rather than simply becoming longer.

## Assessment boundary

The resources deliberately avoid model paragraphs, completed criterion answers, business-specific conclusions or research that learners could transplant into assessed work. Feedback should identify gaps and prompt independent improvement rather than supply the missing assessed content.
