# Week 17 Key Terminology

| Term | Working definition | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Evidence | Material that demonstrates learner activity, decisions, analysis or judgement relevant to assessment. | Assessment decisions must be supportable. |
| Evidence chain | Connected sequence from requirements through planning, implementation, performance and evaluation. | Demonstrates coherent campaign development. |
| Traceability | Ability to follow a requirement, decision or claim through supporting evidence. | Makes assessment evidence easier to verify. |
| Authenticity | Confidence that evidence represents the learner's own work and decision-making. | Fundamental to valid assessment. |
| Annotation | Explanatory text attached to evidence to clarify what it demonstrates. | Adds context where an image/log alone is ambiguous. |
| Context | Information needed to understand what an evidence item represents. | Prevents isolated evidence being misinterpreted. |
| Legibility | Extent to which evidence can be read/inspected clearly. | Unreadable evidence cannot effectively demonstrate achievement. |
| Consistency | Agreement of identifiers, dates, targets and definitions across evidence. | Prevents contradictory or confusing submissions. |
| Cross-reference | Explicit link from one part of the submission to another relevant evidence item. | Reduces duplication and improves navigation. |
| RAG status | Red/amber/green indicator of evidence readiness. | Helps prioritise final completion work. |
| Evidence gap | Required evidence that is genuinely absent. | Must be addressed appropriately rather than hidden. |
| Retrospective invention | Creating historical actions, reasons, dates, targets or results that did not genuinely occur. | Undermines authenticity and assessment validity. |
| Quality assurance | Systematic checking that the final submission is complete, accurate, accessible and coherent. | Reduces avoidable assessment/submission failures. |
| Version control | Managing document/file versions so the correct evidence is submitted. | Prevents obsolete or incomplete work being assessed. |
| Submission readiness | State in which required evidence has been checked and the package is ready for final submission. | Final outcome of Week 17. |
