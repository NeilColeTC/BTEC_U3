## Purpose

Final evidence audit for B.P3, B.P4, B.M2, C.P5, C.P6, C.M3, BC.D2 and BC.D3.

## Evidence quality

Prioritise relevance, authenticity, context, legibility, annotation, traceability and consistency over screenshot volume.

## BC.D3

Help learners cross-reference authentic evidence from the process. Do not encourage a retrospective self-description unsupported by campaign records.

## Support boundary

Teachers can identify inaccessible/unclear evidence, clarify criteria and support technical submission issues. They should not rewrite assessed evaluation or manufacture missing historical evidence.

## Final evidence story

What was required → what was planned → how it was reviewed → what was implemented → what happened → what was optimised → how plan/use was evaluated → what evidence demonstrates professional behaviours.
