# Week 17 Teacher Notes — Assessment Completion and Evidence Review

## Curriculum intent

The repository scaffold defines Week 17 as an Assignment 2 evidence audit against B.P3, B.P4, B.M2, C.P5, C.P6, C.M3, BC.D2 and BC.D3, focusing on completeness, authenticity, annotation, traceability between plan and implementation and independent final submission.

## No new teaching requirement

This is principally a completion and QA week. Avoid introducing new campaign requirements that learners have not had an opportunity to implement naturally.

## Evidence quality over evidence volume

Learners often respond to uncertainty by adding more screenshots. Redirect them towards relevance, context, legibility, annotation and traceability. Duplicate evidence can make a submission harder to assess.

## RAG audit

Use green/amber/red as workflow status, not as an informal grade prediction:

- green = evidence ready;
- amber = evidence exists but needs quality/signposting work;
- red = genuine gap requiring appropriate learner action.

## Authenticity

The strongest safeguard is the historical evidence chain created throughout Weeks 7–16: planning records, IMP/COM/ANA/OPT IDs, analytics snapshots and before/after evidence. Do not encourage reconstruction of evidence that did not exist.

## Teacher support boundary

Teachers can:

- remind learners of criterion requirements;
- indicate that evidence cannot be located/read;
- ask learners to identify where a claim is supported;
- explain generic quality-assurance methods;
- identify technical submission problems.

Teachers should not:

- write or rewrite assessed evaluation;
- tell a learner which final judgement to reach;
- manufacture missing rationale/evidence;
- select personalised distinction conclusions for the learner.

## Peer checking

Peer review should focus on accessibility of evidence: “Can I locate what you are referring to?” rather than “Here is what you should write to get distinction.”

## Final submission

The final package should tell a coherent evidence story:

**what was required → what was planned → what was implemented → what happened → what changed → what was concluded → what should happen next**.

## After submission

Retain teaching exemplars/templates separately from learner assessed evidence and follow centre/Pearson processes for assessment, feedback, resubmission and internal verification.
