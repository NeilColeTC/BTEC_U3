# Week 17 Lesson Plan — Assessment Completion and Evidence Review

**Duration:** 3 hours  
**Learning Aims:** B/C  
**Assessment:** Assignment 2 completion

## Learning outcomes

Learners will audit, organise, cross-reference and quality-assure their evidence against B.P3, B.P4, B.M2, C.P5, C.P6, C.M3, BC.D2 and BC.D3 before independent submission.

## Lesson sequence

### 1. Retrieval and submission mindset — 15 minutes

Retrieve the evidence chain from Weeks 7–16 and distinguish campaign work from evidence of campaign work.

**requirements → plan → review/refinement → implementation → interaction → analytics → optimisation → evaluation**

Introduce the completion cycle:

**criterion → locate evidence → verify quality → identify genuine gap → improve/cross-reference → final QA → submit**.

### 2. Criterion-by-criterion audit — 30 minutes

Learners map each Assignment 2 criterion to evidence. Use RAG status:

- **Green:** evidence present, accessible and sufficiently signposted;
- **Amber:** evidence exists but needs organisation, annotation, explanation or cross-reference;
- **Red:** genuine evidence gap requiring learner action where permitted.

### 3. Evidence quality — 20 minutes

Test each important evidence item for:

**relevance • authenticity • context • legibility • annotation • traceability • consistency**.

Discuss why a screenshot without context may demonstrate less than learners assume.

### 4. Traceability audit — 25 minutes

Select several important business requirements and trace them through planning, implementation and evaluation. Check that deviations and optimisation decisions remain visible rather than being overwritten by the final state.

### 5. Authenticity and retrospective evidence — 15 minutes

Clarify the difference between:

- improving explanation of genuine evidence;
- organising/cross-referencing genuine evidence;
- completing work that is legitimately still outstanding;
- retrospectively inventing actions, reasons, dates, targets or results.

### Break — 10 minutes

### 6. Independent completion workshop — 45 minutes

Learners address their own audit priorities. Teacher support remains within assessment guidance and does not become editing/re-writing of assessed judgements.

### 7. Submission QA — 20 minutes

Check filenames, links, embedded evidence, page/order consistency, readability, criterion references, version control and required declaration/submission process.

### 8. Final peer/self check and exit — 15 minutes

Use a neutral accessibility check: can another reader locate the evidence referenced? Learners make final independent corrections and confirm submission readiness.

## Adaptive teaching

**Support:** RAG audit, criterion prompts, evidence-quality checklist, traceability matrix and ordered submission checklist.  
**Stretch:** contradiction audit, evidence sufficiency vs quantity, confidence/limitations review and removal of redundant evidence that obscures stronger material.

## English, maths, EDI and employability

**English:** proofreading, signposting, evidence referencing and clarity of evaluative argument.  
**Maths:** final verification of calculations, rates and percentage comparisons already used in analytics/evaluation.  
**EDI:** accessibility/readability checks and avoidance of unsupported audience assumptions.  
**Employability:** quality assurance, documentation, version control, audit trails, deadline management and professional submission standards.

## Teacher reflection

- Can every criterion be mapped to evidence?
- Is evidence accessible and understandable without learner narration?
- Are historical campaign decisions traceable?
- Have learners avoided retrospective invention?
- Are distinction judgements genuinely independent?
- Is the final evidence package coherent rather than merely large?
