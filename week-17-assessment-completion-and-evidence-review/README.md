# Week 17 — Assessment Completion and Evidence Review

**Learning aims:** B and C  
**Assessment focus:** B.P3, B.P4, B.M2, C.P5, C.P6, C.M3, BC.D2 and BC.D3

## Week purpose

Week 17 is the final quality-assurance session. Learners audit Assignment 2 for completeness, authenticity, annotation and traceability before independent submission.

**Completion cycle:** criterion → locate evidence → verify quality → identify genuine gap → improve/cross-reference → final QA → submit.

## Criterion reminders

- **B.P4:** review the plan with others in order to identify and inform improvements.
- **BC.D2:** evaluate the plan and use of social media against business requirements.
- **BC.D3:** demonstrate individual responsibility, creativity and effective self-management in planning and using social media in a business context.

## Core principle

Do not manufacture missing historical evidence. Improve organisation, annotation and cross-referencing of genuine evidence; identify real limitations where evidence is unavailable.
