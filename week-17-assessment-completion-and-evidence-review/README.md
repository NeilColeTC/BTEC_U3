# Week 17 — Assessment Completion and Evidence Review

**Learning aims:** B and C  
**Assessment focus:** B.P3, B.P4, B.M2, C.P5, C.P6, C.M3, BC.D2 and BC.D3

## Week purpose

Week 17 is the final assessment-completion and quality-assurance session. Learners do not begin a new campaign stage. They audit their Assignment 2 evidence for completeness, authenticity, annotation, criterion coverage and traceability between requirements, planning, implementation, analytics, optimisation and evaluation before making an independent final submission.

The central completion cycle is:

**criterion → locate evidence → verify quality → identify genuine gap → improve/cross-reference → final QA → submit**

## Learning outcomes

Learners should be able to:

1. Audit evidence against every Assignment 2 criterion.
2. Distinguish missing evidence from evidence that is present but poorly signposted.
3. Cross-reference evidence so an assessor can follow the campaign journey.
4. Check that screenshots and DojoLearn evidence are contextualised and annotated appropriately.
5. Verify that planning and implementation evidence remain traceable.
6. Check that analytics and optimisation conclusions use genuine recorded evidence.
7. Check that BC.D2/BC.D3 evaluation is balanced, evidenced and independent.
8. Identify authenticity and retrospective-evidence risks.
9. Complete presentation, file-management and submission QA.
10. Submit a coherent evidence package independently.

## Core principle

**Do not manufacture missing historical evidence.**

If an action, target, rationale or campaign result was not recorded when it occurred, learners should not retrospectively fabricate it. They may improve explanation, organisation and cross-referencing of genuine evidence and identify limitations where appropriate.

## Assessment boundary

Week 17 provides checking structures, not personalised answers. Teachers may identify where evidence appears absent, inaccessible or unclear and may explain criterion requirements, but learners remain responsible for producing, selecting, interpreting and evaluating their own assessed evidence.
