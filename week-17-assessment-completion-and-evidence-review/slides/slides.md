# Week 17 Slides — Assessment Completion and Evidence Review

## Slide 1 — Week 17
### Assessment Completion and Evidence Review
Make the evidence easy to verify.

---
## Slide 2 — Assignment 2 criteria
**B.P3 • B.P4 • B.M2 • C.P5 • C.P6 • C.M3 • BC.D2 • BC.D3**

---
## Slide 3 — No new campaign stage
Today is completion, evidence review and quality assurance.

---
## Slide 4 — Evidence chain
**Requirements → plan → refinement → implementation → interaction → analytics → optimisation → evaluation**

---
## Slide 5 — Completion cycle
**Criterion → locate evidence → verify quality → identify genuine gap → improve/cross-reference → final QA → submit**

---
## Slide 6 — Campaign work ≠ evidence
Doing something in DojoLearn does not automatically make it easy for an assessor to verify.

---
## Slide 7 — RAG audit
**Green:** clear evidence present. **Amber:** evidence needs signposting/quality improvement. **Red:** genuine gap.

---
## Slide 8 — Evidence quality
**Relevance • authenticity • context • legibility • annotation • traceability • consistency**

---
## Slide 9 — Relevance
Does the evidence actually support the criterion/claim it is attached to?

---
## Slide 10 — Authenticity
Does the evidence represent the learner's own campaign work and decision-making?

---
## Slide 11 — Context
Can the assessor tell what the screenshot/table/log represents and when/why it matters?

---
## Slide 12 — Legibility
Can the evidence actually be read at normal viewing size?

---
## Slide 13 — Annotation
Where needed, explain what the evidence demonstrates — do not merely repeat what is visible.

---
## Slide 14 — Traceability
Can a requirement be followed through planning, implementation and evaluation?

---
## Slide 15 — Consistency
Do names, dates, targets, content IDs and metric definitions remain consistent across evidence?

---
## Slide 16 — B.P3
Locate evidence for the campaign plan and its required components.

---
## Slide 17 — B.P4
Locate evidence for the relevant social-media policy/process expectations developed for the campaign.

---
## Slide 18 — B.M2
Locate evidence showing the plan was reviewed/refined and that decisions were justified.

---
## Slide 19 — C.P5
Locate evidence that the campaign was implemented and operated using appropriate content/features/community activity.

---
## Slide 20 — C.P6
Locate evidence of campaign data collection and analysis, including the required C4 evidence.

---
## Slide 21 — C.M3
Locate the analytics-to-optimisation evidence chain and analysis of the effect of improvements.

---
## Slide 22 — BC.D2
Locate balanced evaluation of the plan against business requirements and fitness for purpose.

---
## Slide 23 — BC.D3
Locate balanced evaluation of the implemented campaign supported by performance evidence and justified recommendations.

---
## Slide 24 — Evidence IDs
Use consistent references such as IMP, COM, ANA and OPT IDs where already established.

---
## Slide 25 — Screenshots
A screenshot is stronger when the reader knows **what it is, what it demonstrates and where it fits in the evidence chain**.

---
## Slide 26 — Do not screenshot everything
Evidence quantity does not replace evidence quality.

---
## Slide 27 — Preserve before/after states
Do not allow final campaign content to erase evidence of optimisation or deviation.

---
## Slide 28 — Broken traceability
If the plan says one thing and implementation shows another, explain the recorded deviation rather than pretending they match.

---
## Slide 29 — Missing ≠ zero
Do not replace unavailable analytics with zero.

---
## Slide 30 — Historical evidence
Do not invent a target, rationale, interaction or result retrospectively.

---
## Slide 31 — What you may improve
Organisation • cross-referencing • explanation • annotation • proofreading • presentation • genuine outstanding work where permitted.

---
## Slide 32 — What you must not manufacture
Actions • dates • reasons • targets • results • interactions • evidence that did not occur.

---
## Slide 33 — Evaluation QA
Check **criterion → evidence → comparison → judgement → significance → limitation → recommendation**.

---
## Slide 34 — Balance
Do not remove evidence simply because it challenges the overall judgement.

---
## Slide 35 — Recommendations
Every major recommendation should connect to an evidenced problem/opportunity.

---
## Slide 36 — Calculation QA
Recheck denominators, rates, percentage change and metric definitions.

---
## Slide 37 — File QA
Clear filenames • correct versions • working links • readable evidence • logical order.

---
## Slide 38 — Assessor test
Can someone who was not present during the campaign understand the evidence package?

---
## Slide 39 — Independence
Teacher/peer checking should help you find issues — the assessed decisions and writing remain yours.

---
## Slide 40 — Final submission
Submit the strongest coherent evidence package, not simply the largest one.
