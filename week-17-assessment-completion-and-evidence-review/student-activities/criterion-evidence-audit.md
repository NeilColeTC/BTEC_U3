## RAG definitions

- **Green:** evidence is present, accessible, relevant and sufficiently signposted.
- **Amber:** genuine evidence exists but needs organisation, annotation, explanation, cross-referencing or presentation improvement.
- **Red:** genuine gap requiring learner action where the assessment conditions permit.

## Audit questions

For each criterion:

1. Can I locate the evidence quickly?
2. Does it demonstrate the criterion rather than merely mention it?
3. Is the evidence my own work?
4. Is its campaign context clear?
5. Are important claims traceable to supporting evidence?
6. Is any part duplicated without adding value?
