## Traceability test

A strong campaign evidence chain should allow the assessor to answer:

1. What did the business require?
2. What did the learner plan in response?
3. Was the plan reviewed/refined?
4. What was actually implemented?
5. What happened?
6. What did the analytics indicate?
7. What changed in response?
8. What judgement was reached and why?
9. What should happen next?
