## Useful annotation

Where required, an annotation should explain:

- what the reader should notice;
- what campaign action/decision the evidence demonstrates;
- which requirement/objective/criterion it supports;
- how it connects to another evidence item where relevant.

## Avoid

- annotations that simply restate visible text;
- tiny screenshots containing unreadable dashboards;
- repeated screenshots of the same state;
- screenshots with no indication of campaign stage;
- annotations that invent reasons/results not recorded elsewhere.
