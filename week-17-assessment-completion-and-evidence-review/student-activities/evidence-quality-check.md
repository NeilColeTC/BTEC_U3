## Seven evidence-quality dimensions

### Relevance
Does it support the criterion or claim?

### Authenticity
Does it represent your own campaign work and decision-making?

### Context
Can a reader identify what the evidence represents and why it matters?

### Legibility
Can the evidence be read clearly?

### Annotation
Where explanation is needed, have you explained what the evidence demonstrates?

### Traceability
Can the evidence be linked to requirements, decisions and later outcomes?

### Consistency
Are identifiers, dates, targets and metric definitions consistent across the submission?
