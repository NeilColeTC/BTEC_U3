## Evidence quality

- [ ] Important screenshots are readable.
- [ ] Evidence has sufficient campaign context.
- [ ] Evidence IDs/references are consistent.
- [ ] Links/embedded items work.
- [ ] Before/after evidence is preserved where required.
- [ ] Missing data has not been represented as zero.
- [ ] Calculations and denominators have been rechecked.
- [ ] No important claim depends on evidence that cannot be located.

## Traceability

- [ ] Major requirements can be traced into the plan.
- [ ] Major implementation decisions can be traced back to planning or recorded deviations.
- [ ] Analytics findings can be traced to genuine data.
- [ ] Optimisations can be traced to analytics evidence.
- [ ] Evaluation judgements can be traced to requirements/success criteria and evidence.
- [ ] Recommendations can be traced to evaluation findings.

## Authenticity

- [ ] Work represents my own campaign and decisions.
- [ ] I have not retrospectively invented campaign evidence.
- [ ] Any genuine evidence limitation is identified rather than concealed.
- [ ] My final evaluative judgements are my own.

## Presentation

- [ ] Filenames are clear.
- [ ] Correct/latest versions are submitted.
- [ ] Documents open correctly.
- [ ] Evidence is in a logical order.
- [ ] Formatting is readable and consistent.
- [ ] Spelling/grammar have been proofread.
- [ ] Required learner details/declarations are complete according to the submission process.

## Final question

**Could an assessor who did not watch me complete the campaign understand what I planned, what I did, what happened, what I changed, what I concluded and why?**
