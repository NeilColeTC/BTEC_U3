# Week 10 — Community Building and Social Media Policy

**Specification focus:** Learning Aim B — B4/B5  
**Assessment preparation:** B.P3 / B.M2  
**Practical environment:** DojoLearn social-media simulation

## Week purpose

Week 10 develops the operational and governance side of campaign planning. Learners plan how a business will build and manage an online community while protecting professionalism, confidentiality, security and reputation through an appropriate social-media policy.

The central principle is:

**interaction opportunity → appropriate response → policy boundary → escalation/recording → community/reputation outcome**

## Learning outcomes

Learners should be able to:

1. Explain how planned interaction can help build an online community.
2. Select appropriate approaches for enquiries, positive engagement, criticism and complaints.
3. Distinguish responding, moderating, hiding/removing and escalating.
4. Develop practical social-media policy rules for staff/business use.
5. Address professionalism, confidentiality, security and personal/business separation.
6. Recognise relevant legal and ethical considerations without treating policy as a list of laws.
7. Rehearse and justify interaction decisions using DojoLearn.
8. Connect community-management and policy decisions to B.P3/B.M2 campaign planning.

## B4/B5 focus

This week concentrates on:

- community-building techniques;
- responding to customers/users;
- moderation and escalation;
- professional conduct;
- confidentiality and information handling;
- account/security practice;
- separation of personal and business activity;
- legal/ethical considerations;
- policy ownership and consistency.

## DojoLearn practical

Learners rehearse responses to simulated enquiries, complaints and other interactions. The emphasis is not on finding one scripted “perfect reply”, but on making defensible decisions that reflect the business purpose, audience, tone and policy boundaries.
