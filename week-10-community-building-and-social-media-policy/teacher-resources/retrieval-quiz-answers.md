# Week 10 Retrieval Quiz — Indicative Answers

1. A need, expectation or condition the campaign should satisfy because of the client/business context.
2. A sufficiently specific intended result whose achievement/progress can be judged using relevant evidence.
3. The group the campaign intends to reach/influence.
4. Planned approach to content purposes, formats, messages and audience response across a campaign.
5. It should encourage an action that supports the campaign objective rather than an unrelated activity.
6. Examples include reputational damage, inappropriate content, time/resource pressure, security threats and information exposure.
7. Harm to how stakeholders perceive/trust the business.
8. Passing an issue to a person with appropriate authority/expertise when it cannot/should not be resolved at the current level.
9. It can suppress legitimate criticism, reduce trust, intensify complaints and make moderation appear unfair.
10. Broadcasting is primarily one-way distribution; community building involves purposeful interaction/relationships.

Use diagnostically. Do not treat as live-assignment policy content.
