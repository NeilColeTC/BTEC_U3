# Week 10 Lesson Plan — Community Building and Social Media Policy

**Duration:** 3 hours  
**Learning Aim:** B  
**Specification:** B4/B5  
**Criteria preparation:** B.P3 / B.M2

## Learning outcomes

Learners will plan community-building activity, develop workable social-media policy controls and rehearse interaction decisions in DojoLearn.

## Lesson sequence

### 1. Retrieval — 15 minutes

Retrieve campaign requirement, objective, audience, content strategy, call to action, risk, reputation and escalation. Ask: **What happens after an audience member responds to campaign content?**

### 2. From audience to community — 20 minutes

Contrast broadcasting with community management. Introduce planned techniques:

- questions and conversation prompts;
- timely responses;
- acknowledging useful contributions;
- sharing/reposting where appropriate;
- consistent tone;
- useful follow-up information;
- handling criticism professionally;
- encouraging safe, relevant participation.

### 3. Interaction triage — 25 minutes

Learners classify neutral interactions as enquiry, positive engagement, constructive criticism, complaint, misinformation, spam, abuse or potential security/confidentiality issue. For each, decide whether to respond, moderate, escalate, record or take another justified action.

### 4. Why businesses need a social-media policy — 20 minutes

Move from individual response decisions to organisational consistency. Explore risks caused by unclear responsibility, inconsistent tone, accidental disclosure, weak account security and personal/business boundary failures.

### 5. Policy-building workshop — 30 minutes

Learners develop policy controls covering:

- authorised users/responsibilities;
- professional tone/conduct;
- confidentiality and sensitive information;
- account/security practice;
- personal vs business content;
- moderation;
- complaint/escalation routes;
- approvals/permissions;
- legal/ethical awareness;
- incident recording/review.

Policy statements must include **rule + reason + action/escalation**, not vague slogans.

### Break — 10 minutes

### 6. DojoLearn community-response practical — 35 minutes

Learners work through simulated enquiries, complaints and interactions. For each they record:

**classify → decide → respond/action → policy reason → escalation if needed → expected community/reputation effect**.

### 7. B.M2 justification practice — 25 minutes

Learners justify selected community/policy decisions for a neutral scenario. Require links to campaign requirements, audience, operational risk and likely consequence.

### 8. Exit ticket — 20 minutes

Learners explain why one community-building technique and one policy control are appropriate, then resolve a short escalation scenario.

## Adaptive teaching

**Support:** interaction category cards, policy headings, decision flow prompts and oral rehearsal before writing.  
**Stretch:** ambiguous cases, competing priorities, proportional moderation, public/private response choice and policy trade-offs between responsiveness and control.

## English, maths, EDI and employability

**English:** professional customer responses, tone, concise policy writing and justification.  
**Maths:** response-time targets or moderation/service measures where relevant to campaign planning.  
**EDI:** respectful interaction, accessible communication, non-discriminatory moderation and avoiding assumptions about users.  
**Employability:** customer service, digital professionalism, escalation, policy compliance, reputation management and cyber-aware working.

## Teacher reflection

- Do learners distinguish community management from posting content?
- Are moderation decisions proportionate?
- Do policies explain what staff should actually do?
- Can learners recognise confidentiality/security issues?
- Are B.M2 justifications contextual rather than generic?
