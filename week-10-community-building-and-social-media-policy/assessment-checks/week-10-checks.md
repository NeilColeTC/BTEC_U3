# Week 10 — Assessment and Understanding Checks

## Criteria progression

**B.P3:** community-management and policy arrangements form part of a workable campaign plan.  
**B.M2:** selected arrangements are justified against requirements, audience, risks and constraints.

## Core checks

By the end of Week 10, learners should be able to:

- [ ] explain the difference between audience and online community;
- [ ] plan appropriate community-building techniques;
- [ ] connect interaction techniques to campaign objectives;
- [ ] recognise resource implications of community management;
- [ ] classify enquiries, complaints, criticism, spam, abuse and security/confidentiality concerns;
- [ ] distinguish response, moderation and escalation;
- [ ] choose an appropriate public/private response route;
- [ ] explain why negative criticism should not automatically be deleted;
- [ ] develop operational social-media policy rules;
- [ ] address professionalism and accuracy;
- [ ] address confidentiality/sensitive information;
- [ ] address account/security practice;
- [ ] address personal/business separation;
- [ ] address moderation and complaint handling;
- [ ] recognise relevant legal/ethical considerations;
- [ ] identify responsibilities/escalation routes;
- [ ] rehearse interaction decisions in DojoLearn;
- [ ] justify significant policy/community choices at B.M2 level.

## B.P3 readiness

Learner's campaign planning includes realistic arrangements for interaction, moderation, responsibility, security/confidentiality and policy compliance.

## B.M2 readiness

Learner can explain why selected controls and community-management approaches are appropriate, recognising significant constraints and trade-offs.

## Common misconceptions

- [ ] “Community building means getting lots of followers.”
- [ ] “Every comment needs an immediate reply.”
- [ ] “All negative comments should be deleted.”
- [ ] “A complaint is abuse.”
- [ ] “A policy is just a list of things staff must not do.”
- [ ] “Be professional” is a complete policy rule.
- [ ] “Legal/ethical considerations are separate from operational decisions.”
- [ ] “Security is only the IT department's problem.”
- [ ] “Personal posts cannot affect a business account.”
- [ ] “Escalation means the original responder failed.”

## Evidence sources

Use retrieval, community-building planner, interaction triage, policy builder, DojoLearn response practical, B.M2 formative justification and exit ticket.

## Transition check for Week 11

Before plan review/refinement, learners should be able to show how their campaign addresses:

1. planned content/publication;
2. intended audience interaction;
3. response/moderation arrangements;
4. policy/security/confidentiality controls;
5. responsibilities and escalation;
6. links back to campaign requirements/objectives.

Any missing area becomes a Week 11 refinement priority.
