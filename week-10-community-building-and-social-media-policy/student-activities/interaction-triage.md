## Classification options

- enquiry;
- positive engagement;
- constructive criticism;
- complaint;
- misinformation;
- spam;
- abuse;
- potential security/confidentiality issue.

## Decision questions

- Does the user need useful information?
- Is the criticism legitimate even if uncomfortable?
- Does the message breach a stated moderation rule?
- Could a public reply expose private information?
- Does the responder have authority to resolve the issue?
- Could the interaction indicate a security problem?
- Would doing nothing create a larger reputational problem?

## Principle

**Negative is not the same as inappropriate.** A complaint should not automatically be removed merely because it criticises the business.
