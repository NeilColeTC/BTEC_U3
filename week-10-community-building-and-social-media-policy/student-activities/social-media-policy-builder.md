## Policy quality test

For each rule ask:

1. Could a member of staff understand what to do?
2. Is the rule proportionate to the risk?
3. Is responsibility clear?
4. Is there a clear escalation route where needed?
5. Does it support the business/campaign rather than merely restrict activity?

## Avoid vague statements

Weak: **“Employees must be professional.”**  
Improve by defining the expected behaviour, reason and action when the boundary is crossed.
