# DojoLearn Practical — Community Response and Moderation

## Purpose

Rehearse consistent, professional responses to simulated enquiries, complaints and other interactions before campaign implementation.

## Response workflow

For each DojoLearn interaction:

**classify → decide → respond/action → policy reason → escalate if needed → record outcome**

## Response quality check

Before submitting/recording a response ask:

- Is it accurate?
- Is the tone appropriate to the business and situation?
- Does it actually address the user's issue?
- Does it avoid requesting/disclosing sensitive information publicly?
- Am I authorised to resolve this?
- Should the issue be moved to another channel?
- Does the interaction need recording/escalation?

## Moderation check

Do not remove content solely because it is critical. Use the planned policy to distinguish legitimate criticism from spam, abuse or content that genuinely breaches moderation rules.

## Evidence capture

Retain evidence as directed by your teacher showing selected interactions, decisions/responses and any simulator feedback/outcomes. This can support later reflection on how effectively the campaign was managed.

## Reflection
