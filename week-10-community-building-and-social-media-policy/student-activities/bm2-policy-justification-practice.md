# B.M2 Formative Practice — Justifying Community and Policy Decisions

**Neutral formative scenario — not a completed Assignment 2 response.**

## Scenario

A fictional independent sports centre is planning a six-week social-media campaign promoting new evening activity sessions. The campaign encourages questions and comments, but only two staff members can monitor accounts. Users may ask about bookings, accessibility, age suitability and individual circumstances.

## Task

Recommend and justify:

- two community-building techniques;
- an approach to response times/responsibility;
- how complaints should be handled;
- when conversations should move away from a public thread;
- two moderation rules;
- one confidentiality control;
- one account-security control;
- one personal/business boundary rule.

For each significant decision use:

**decision → requirement/risk → likely benefit → limitation/trade-off → judgement**

## Challenge

The business wants “instant replies to every comment” but has only two staff members. Explain the conflict and propose a more workable policy approach without inventing resources.

## Peer review

1. Are rules operational rather than vague?
2. Are community techniques linked to campaign objectives?
3. Are moderation decisions proportionate?
4. Are confidentiality/security risks addressed?
5. Does the justification recognise constraints/trade-offs?
