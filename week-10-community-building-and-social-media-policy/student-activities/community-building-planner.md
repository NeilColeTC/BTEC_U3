## Community-quality questions

1. Does the approach create genuine opportunity for useful interaction?
2. Can the business realistically resource the expected responses?
3. How will tone remain consistent?
4. What happens when interaction becomes negative or sensitive?
5. Which policy rules are needed before implementation?
