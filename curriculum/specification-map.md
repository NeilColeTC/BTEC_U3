# Specification Map

## Learning Aim A

### A1 Social media websites
- changing platform features and audiences
- branding, promotion, communication, customer service and issue resolution
- advertising, website/mobile integration, SEO, profiles, analytics and audience profiles

### A2 Business uses of social media
- text, image, video, links, polls and quizzes
- information, promotion, humour, offers and customer service
- engaging audiences, keywords, networking, sharing and advertising
- links between social media and company/e-commerce websites

### A3 Risks and issues
- negative comments and reputational damage
- time constraints and return on time investment
- unforeseen consequences of content
- security threats and loss of sensitive information

## Learning Aim B

### B1 Social media planning processes
Business requirements, publishing, community building and policy.

### B2 Business requirements
Client requirements, timescales, responsibilities, success criteria, platform selection and measurable targets.

### B3 Content planning and publishing
Target audience, content choice, keyword strategy and publishing schedule.

### B4 Developing an online community
Promotional techniques, feedback, links, monitoring and responses.

### B5 Developing a social media policy
Philosophy, honesty, respect, confidentiality, security, personal/business separation, legal and ethical considerations.

### B6 Reviewing and refining plans
Feedback, communication, meetings, timescales and refinement.

## Learning Aim C

### C1 Creating accounts and profiles
Creation, administration, branding, configuration and privacy.

### C2 Content creation and publication
Research, creation, publication, visibility, interaction, integration and device adaptation.

### C3 Implementation of online community building
Hashtags, sharing, tagging, groups, following, monitoring, responses and automation.

### C4 Data gathering and analysis
Interaction data, audience data, likes/shares, target-vs-actual audience, high-performing content and click-throughs.

### C5 Skills, knowledge and behaviours
Planning, feedback, professionalism, etiquette, accountability, responsibility, evaluation, communication and decision making.
