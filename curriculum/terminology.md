# Unit 3 Core Terminology

| Term | Working definition | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Social media | Online platforms enabling users/organisations to create, publish, share and interact with content. | Core environment for the unit. |
| Target audience | Group the business/campaign intends to reach or influence. | Shapes platform, content and evaluation decisions. |
| Audience profile | Evidence-based description of audience characteristics such as age, gender, interests and income where appropriate. | Supports A.P2 and later campaign planning. |
| Brand | Identity/image associated with a business, product or service. | Should remain coherent across campaign activity. |
| Engagement | Audience interaction with content/account. | Important metric but not automatically a business outcome. |
| Reach | Extent/number of users exposed to content where measured. | Visibility measure that needs contextual interpretation. |
| Business outcome | Result linked to an objective, e.g. enquiry, click, registration, booking or sale. | Distinguishes useful outcomes from vanity metrics. |
| Analytics | Collection/examination of campaign data. | Supports C.P6 and optimisation. |
| Business requirement | Need, expectation or constraint the campaign must address. | Basis of Learning Aim B planning and BC.D2 evaluation. |
| Objective | Specific intended result against which progress can be judged. | Connects plan to evidence. |
| Success criterion | Measure/condition used to judge achievement. | Provides evaluation benchmark. |
| Baseline | Recorded starting state before campaign implementation. | Allows later comparison. |
| Campaign target | Intended performance level for a relevant measure. | Enables progress evaluation. |
| Platform suitability | Extent to which platform characteristics fit audience, objectives, content and constraints. | Supports evidence-based channel selection. |
| Content strategy | Planned approach to campaign content purposes, formats and intended audience response. | Prevents isolated posting. |
| Keyword | Relevant word/phrase used in content/research to communicate subject or audience intent. | Supports relevance/discoverability. |
| Hashtag | Platform-supported marker categorising/connecting content where relevant. | May support discovery/context. |
| Call to action | Prompt indicating an intended audience next step. | Connects content with action objectives. |
| Content calendar | Planned schedule of content IDs, platforms, timing, purpose, message and measures. | Makes the campaign implementable and traceable. |
| Community management | Planned monitoring and response activity around an online community. | Campaign operation continues after posting. |
| Moderation | Applying policy to user-generated interactions/content. | Supports safe, professional community operation. |
| Escalation | Passing an issue to someone with appropriate authority/expertise. | Protects customers, staff and business. |
| Social-media policy | Organisational rules/processes governing social-media use. | Supports professionalism, confidentiality, security and consistency. |
| Refinement | Controlled improvement to a plan based on review/feedback. | Central to B.P4/B.M2 evidence. |
| Implementation | Putting the reviewed campaign plan into operation. | Core Learning Aim C activity. |
| Traceability | Ability to follow requirements/decisions through evidence and outcomes. | Strengthens assessment authenticity and evaluation. |
| Metric | Defined quantitative measure of campaign activity/performance. | Basis for analytics. |
| Rate | Outcome divided by a relevant opportunity/denominator, often expressed as a percentage. | Supports fairer comparison than raw totals alone. |
| Optimisation | Evidence-led adjustment intended to improve performance. | Central to C.M3. |
| Evaluation | Supported judgement about effectiveness/value against relevant criteria. | Central to BC.D2. |
| Individual responsibility | Evidence that the learner owns and carries out decisions/tasks appropriately. | Part of BC.D3. |
| Creativity | Appropriate original/problem-solving decisions in the business campaign context. | Part of BC.D3. |
| Effective self-management | Organisation, time management, evidence management and response to change shown across the process. | Part of BC.D3. |
