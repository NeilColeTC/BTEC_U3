# Unit 3 Course Map

## 17-week sequence

| Week | Focus | Learning aim / criteria emphasis | DojoLearn practical emphasis |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Introduction to Social Media in Business | A1 | Platform induction and business/personal use comparison |
| 2 | Platforms and Business Use | A1 / A.P1 | Explore simulated platform features and match them to business aims |
| 3 | Audience Profiles and Targeting | A1/A2 / A.P2 | Match simulated platforms to audience personas |
| 4 | Attracting and Engaging Audiences | A2 / A.M1 | Create alternative posts for personas and compare likely effectiveness |
| 5 | Risks, Issues and Reputation | A3 / A.D1 | Respond to complaints, negative comments and reputation scenarios |
| 6 | Learning Aim A Assessment Workshop | A.P1, A.P2, A.M1, A.D1 | Evidence consolidation; no completed assessed response supplied |
| 7 | Client Requirements and Campaign Planning | B1/B2 / B.P3 | Establish campaign, requirements, objectives and measures |
| 8 | Platform Selection and Content Strategy | B2/B3 / B.P3, B.M2 | Select platforms and measurable targets |
| 9 | Content, Keywords and Publishing | B3 / B.P3, B.M2 | Build content calendar, keyword/hashtag strategy and draft posts |
| 10 | Community Building and Social Media Policy | B4/B5 / B.P3, B.M2 | Plan engagement and professional responses |
| 11 | Reviewing and Refining the Plan | B6 / B.P4, B.M2, BC.D3 | Stakeholder review, feedback and revised plan |
| 12 | Campaign Implementation | C1/C2 / C.P5 | Configure simulated business profiles and begin publishing |
| 13 | Content Publication and Community Building | C2/C3 / C.P5, BC.D3 | Publish, tag, share, follow and respond professionally |
| 14 | Analytics and Data Analysis | C4 / C.P6 | Gather and interpret simulated campaign analytics |
| 15 | Content Optimisation | C2/C4 / C.M3 | Change content, timing, format or features using performance evidence |
| 16 | Campaign Evaluation | C5 / BC.D2, BC.D3 | Evaluate campaign against plan and business requirements |
| 17 | Assessment Completion and Evidence Review | All B/C criteria | Final evidence audit, annotation and submission preparation |

## Delivery model

The course deliberately moves learners through **plan → create → publish → interact → analyse → optimise → evaluate**. Practical work should use DojoLearn as a controlled learning environment and should generate authentic learner evidence without supplying completed assessment answers.
