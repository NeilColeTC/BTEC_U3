# Week 7 — Assessment and Understanding Checks

## Primary progression

**B.P3 preparation:** planning a social-media campaign to meet client/business requirements.

## Core checks

By the end of Week 7, learners should be able to:

- [ ] distinguish Learning Aim A analysis from Learning Aim B planning;
- [ ] identify business/client requirements from a brief;
- [ ] distinguish explicit requirements from assumptions/implied needs;
- [ ] define campaign purpose;
- [ ] identify the intended target audience without inventing unsupported characteristics;
- [ ] distinguish aim, objective, requirement and success criterion;
- [ ] write measurable campaign objectives;
- [ ] select measures that correspond to objectives;
- [ ] explain the value of a baseline;
- [ ] identify timescales and responsibilities;
- [ ] recognise constraints/dependencies;
- [ ] establish a DojoLearn campaign baseline;
- [ ] trace a planning decision back to a requirement/objective.

## Quality levels

### Secure B.P3 preparation

Learner identifies requirements accurately and produces coherent, measurable objectives, measures, timescales and responsibilities that can form the foundation of a viable campaign plan.

### Developing

Learner identifies the main requirements but objectives are broad, targets arbitrary, measures weakly matched or planning details incomplete.

### Requires intervention

Learner begins with platform/content preferences, confuses requirements with solutions, invents unsupported client/audience needs or cannot define how success would be measured.

## Common misconceptions

- [ ] “The campaign is the social-media posts.”
- [ ] “SMART means add a percentage and deadline.”
- [ ] “More followers/likes is always the objective.”
- [ ] “A platform choice is a client requirement.”
- [ ] “An implied requirement can be treated as fact.”
- [ ] “Baseline data is only needed after the campaign.”
- [ ] “Any metric can demonstrate any objective.”
- [ ] “Planning ends once objectives are written.”

## Evidence sources

Use retrieval, brief deconstruction, objective builder, requirements grid, DojoLearn baseline/setup, B.P3 formative practice and exit ticket.

## Transition check for Week 8

Before platform selection, learners should be able to state clearly:

1. what the campaign needs to achieve;
2. who it is intended to reach;
3. what requirements/constraints shape it;
4. how success could be measured.

If these are unclear, platform/content decisions in Week 8 are likely to become preference-led rather than requirement-led.
