# Activity — Client Brief Deconstruction

Use the neutral practice brief supplied by your teacher.

## Step 1 — Annotate

Identify and colour-code:

- business need/problem/opportunity;
- campaign purpose;
- target audience;
- explicit requirements;
- timescale/deadlines;
- constraints/resources;
- responsibilities/stakeholders;
- evidence of success.
