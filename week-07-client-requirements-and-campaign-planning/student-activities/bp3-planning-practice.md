# B.P3 Formative Practice — Campaign Requirements and Objectives

**This is formative practice and does not provide a completed Assignment 2 plan.**

## Scenario

A fictional local museum wants a four-week social-media campaign to increase awareness of a new interactive exhibition and encourage more visits to its online ticket-information page. The museum wants communication to remain educational and family-friendly and has limited staff time for daily social-media management.

## Task

Produce the **requirements and objectives section** of a campaign plan.

Include:

- campaign purpose;
- intended target audience based only on information available in the scenario or clearly identified assumptions requiring research;
- relevant business/client requirements;
- constraints;
- campaign timescale;
- responsibilities that would need assigning;
- at least two measurable objectives;
- a suitable measure/success criterion for each objective.

Do **not** select a final platform/content strategy yet; that is the next stage of planning.

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Requirements come from the scenario rather than my personal preferences.
- [ ] I distinguish requirements from objectives.
- [ ] Objectives are specific enough to measure.
- [ ] Targets are meaningful rather than arbitrary.
- [ ] Measures match objectives.
- [ ] Timescale is clear.
- [ ] Constraints are recognised.
- [ ] I have not invented unsupported audience characteristics.

## Stretch

Identify one conflict or trade-off in the brief and explain how it could affect later campaign planning.
