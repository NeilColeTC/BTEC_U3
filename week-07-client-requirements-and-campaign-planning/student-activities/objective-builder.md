# Activity — Building Measurable Campaign Objectives

## Aim or objective?

Classify each statement as a broad **aim** or a more specific **objective**, then explain what would improve it.

1. Increase awareness of the business.
2. Get lots of engagement.
3. Encourage more people to visit the booking page.
4. Generate 100 relevant booking-page visits during a four-week campaign.
5. Improve communication with existing customers.

## Objective quality test

For each objective ask:

- **Specific:** is the intended result clear?
- **Measurable:** what evidence will be collected?
- **Achievable:** is the target plausible given baseline/resources?
- **Relevant:** does it support a requirement/business need?
- **Time-related:** when will achievement be judged?

## Challenge — meaningless precision

A learner changes “increase engagement” to “increase engagement by 37% in seven days” with no baseline or reason for the figure.

Explain why this is measurable but may still be a poor objective. What evidence would be needed to make the target defensible?
