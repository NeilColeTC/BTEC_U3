## Traceability check

For each row ask:

1. Does the objective genuinely respond to the requirement?
2. Would the measure tell us whether progress was made?
3. Is the timescale appropriate?
4. Is responsibility clear?
5. Is anything required before this can happen?

## Orphan check

- Is there a requirement with no objective?
- Is there an objective that supports no requirement?
- Is there a measure with no decision it can inform?

Resolve or justify each orphan.
