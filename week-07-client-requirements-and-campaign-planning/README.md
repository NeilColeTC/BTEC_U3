# Week 7 — Client Requirements and Campaign Planning

**Specification focus:** Learning Aim B — B1/B2  
**Assessment preparation:** B.P3  
**Practical environment:** DojoLearn social-media simulation

## Week purpose

Week 7 launches Assignment 2 and begins the transition from analysing business use of social media to **planning a purposeful campaign**. Learners interpret a client/business scenario, identify requirements and convert broad intentions into clear campaign objectives, success criteria and planning constraints.

The central planning principle is:

**requirement → objective → activity → measure**

## Learning outcomes

Learners should be able to:

1. Identify the business/client requirements that should shape a social-media campaign.
2. Distinguish business aims, campaign objectives, requirements, constraints and success criteria.
3. Define the campaign purpose and intended target audience from a supplied scenario.
4. Produce clear, measurable campaign objectives.
5. Identify timescales, responsibilities and resources that affect planning.
6. Select appropriate measures that could later demonstrate whether objectives were achieved.
7. Establish a campaign workspace/baseline in DojoLearn without prematurely implementing the campaign.

## B1/B2 focus

This week follows the repository scaffold by concentrating on:

- business/client requirements;
- campaign purpose;
- target audience;
- timescales;
- responsibilities;
- success criteria;
- measurable campaign objectives.

Detailed platform selection and content strategy are deliberately reserved for Week 8.

## Learning progression

**interpret brief → identify requirements → define purpose/audience → write objectives → identify measures → establish planning baseline**

## DojoLearn practical

Learners establish the simulated business/campaign context and record baseline requirements. The practical is planning-led: learners should not begin publishing campaign content simply because the environment makes publication possible.
