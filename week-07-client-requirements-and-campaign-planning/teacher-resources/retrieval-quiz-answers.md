# Week 7 Retrieval Quiz — Indicative Answers

1. The group a business/campaign intends to reach.
2. A specific intended business result/outcome; accept appropriate wording.
3. Reach concerns exposure/visibility; engagement concerns interaction.
4. Examples include enquiry, booking, registration, purchase or another objective-linked action.
5. Suitability depends on who needs to be reached, what must be achieved and platform characteristics.
6. Starting value/state against which later performance/change can be compared.
7. Engagement may not result in the intended business action/outcome.
8. Examples from Learning Aim A include reputation, time/resource demand, unforeseen consequences, security or information exposure.
9. Analysis examines what exists/has happened; planning specifies intended future actions/resources/measures to meet requirements.
10. Accept answers referring to a clear link with evidence, business requirements, objectives and/or audience rather than personal preference.

Use diagnostically. Do not treat these answers as assessed campaign-plan content.
