# Week 7 Teacher Notes — Client Requirements and Campaign Planning

## Curriculum intent

Week 7 launches Assignment 2 and Learning Aim B. The repository scaffold specifies B1/B2, business requirements, purpose, target audience, timescales, responsibilities, success criteria and measurable objectives, with DojoLearn used to establish the simulated campaign and baseline requirements.

## Protect the sequence

The course map deliberately separates:

- Week 7 — requirements/objectives;
- Week 8 — platform selection/content strategy;
- Week 9 — detailed content/keyword/publishing planning;
- Week 10 — community building/policy;
- Week 11 — review/refinement;
- Week 12 onward — implementation.

Do not let learners rush directly to creating posts. Planning quality depends on knowing what the campaign is trying to achieve first.

## Requirements vs solutions

A common mistake is to write solutions as if they were requirements, e.g. “The campaign must use video” when the client actually said “increase awareness among X”. Teach learners to preserve the distinction unless a particular platform/format is genuinely specified by the brief.

## Objectives

SMART is a useful quality check, not a formula. A target can be technically measurable yet indefensible. Encourage learners to justify measures/targets using baseline data, requirements, available simulator metrics or other appropriate evidence.

## Baselines

DojoLearn should provide an authentic starting state where possible. Learners must record what the simulator actually shows and avoid inventing values. This creates evidence that can later support C4/C5 analysis and evaluation.

## Assignment support boundary

During live Assignment 2 planning, teachers may clarify requirements and planning processes within centre/awarding-body rules, but should not supply the learner's campaign objectives, target audience decisions, platform choices or completed plan.

## Adaptive teaching

### Support
- colour-code briefs;
- separate requirement/objective/measure cards;
- use objective quality questions;
- chunk the planning grid;
- allow oral rehearsal before independent writing.

### Stretch
- identify ambiguity/conflicting requirements;
- challenge arbitrary targets;
- identify dependencies;
- distinguish leading indicators from meaningful outcomes;
- create a traceability chain from requirement to later evaluation evidence.

## DojoLearn evidence

Encourage learners to retain appropriately labelled evidence of the baseline/setup. They will need a coherent evidence trail as the course moves through implementation, analytics, optimisation and evaluation.

## Preparation for Week 8

Learners should arrive with sufficiently clear requirements, purpose, audience, objectives and measures that they can make reasoned platform/content decisions rather than selecting platforms first and retrofitting objectives afterwards.
