# Week 7 Key Terminology

| Term | Working definition | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Client requirement | A need, expectation or condition the campaign should satisfy for the client/business. | Campaign decisions should trace back to requirements. |
| Campaign purpose | The reason the campaign exists and the broad change/result it is intended to support. | Keeps planning focused on business need. |
| Aim | A broad intended direction or outcome. | Provides overall direction but may be too broad to measure directly. |
| Objective | A specific intended result against which progress can be judged. | B.P3 planning needs clear objectives. |
| SMART | Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant and Time-related quality check. | Helps test objective clarity, but does not replace business reasoning. |
| Target audience | The group the campaign is intended to reach/influence. | Content/platform decisions should later be justified against this audience. |
| Success criterion | Evidence/condition used to judge whether an objective or requirement has been met. | Enables later review/evaluation. |
| Measure | Data/evidence used to track performance against an objective. | Connects planning with later analytics. |
| Baseline | Starting value/state before campaign activity. | Allows change/performance to be compared with the starting position. |
| Timescale | Campaign duration, deadlines and milestones. | Affects feasibility, scheduling and measurement. |
| Responsibility | Ownership of a campaign task/decision. | Clarifies who is expected to do what. |
| Constraint | A limitation or condition restricting available choices. | Plans should be realistic within client/business circumstances. |
| Dependency | Something that must occur/be available before another activity can proceed. | Helps sequence a workable plan. |
| Explicit requirement | A requirement directly stated in the brief. | Should be identified accurately. |
| Implied requirement | A possible requirement inferred from context. | Should be treated cautiously and clarified rather than silently invented. |
| Traceability | Ability to connect a campaign decision back to a requirement/objective and later evidence. | Supports coherent planning and evaluation. |

These are working definitions for teaching and planning.
