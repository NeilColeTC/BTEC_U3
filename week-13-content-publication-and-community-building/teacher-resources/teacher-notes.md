# Week 13 Teacher Notes — Content Publication and Community Building

## Curriculum intent

The repository scaffold defines Week 13 as C2/C3: publish varied engaging content, improve visibility through hashtags/tags/sharing/following and respond professionally to audience interaction. DojoLearn provides the live campaign/community-management phase. Assessment focus is C.P5/BC.D3.

## Continuity from Week 12

Week 12 established implementation discipline and the evidence log. Continue those processes rather than treating Week 13 as a separate practical.

## Purposeful variety

Do not encourage learners to create every possible content format merely to demonstrate “variety”. Content should earn its place through an audience need, campaign objective or communication purpose.

## Visibility techniques

Teach these as strategic tools rather than a checklist. A hashtag, tag, share or follow should have an intended effect and something that can later be observed/evaluated where the simulator supports it.

## Community building

The emphasis is repeated useful interaction rather than vanity measures alone. Learners should understand that publishing an invitation to interact creates an operational responsibility to monitor/respond.

## Interaction triage

Reuse the Week 10 logic:

**classify → decide → respond/action → policy reason → escalate if needed → record**.

The key misconception remains **negative = inappropriate**. Legitimate criticism and complaints can be valuable evidence and should not be removed merely to protect apparent campaign positivity.

## Evidence

Maintain the Week 12 implementation log and add a dedicated community-management log. Capture meaningful interactions and actions rather than every trivial event.

## BC.D3 progression

Week 13 is a valuable point to begin an evaluative observation log. Learners can notice patterns, but should use cautious reasoning:

**action → evidence → possible interpretation → limitation/alternative explanation → next evidence needed**.

This produces much stronger raw material for Week 16 than retrospective comments such as “this post did well”.

## Assessment boundary

Teachers can explain simulator functionality, classification frameworks and policy principles. Do not write assessed responses, decide which content the learner should publish or supply the learner's final campaign judgement.

## Preparation for Week 14

At the end of Week 13, learners should have enough live activity and evidence to begin systematic analytics: content performance, audience interaction, progress towards targets and platform/content comparisons.
