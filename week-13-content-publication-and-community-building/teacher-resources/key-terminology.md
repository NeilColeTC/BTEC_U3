# Week 13 Key Terminology

| Term | Working definition | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Content publication | Making planned campaign content available on the selected platform. | Demonstrates active implementation under C2/C.P5. |
| Content variety | Use of different content purposes, messages or formats within a campaign. | Can address different audience needs when strategically justified. |
| Visibility | Likelihood that relevant users can encounter campaign content. | Content cannot contribute to objectives if the intended audience does not see it. |
| Hashtag | Keyword/phrase marker used on some platforms to categorise or aid discovery of content. | Can support context/discovery when relevant. |
| Tag | Reference linking or identifying another account/entity in content where platform features allow. | May support relevant connection/discovery but should not be used indiscriminately. |
| Sharing | Redistributing content to another audience/feed/context. | Can extend visibility when audience fit is appropriate. |
| Following | Subscribing/connecting to an account's content on platforms that support it. | Can contribute to relevant network/community activity. |
| Community building | Developing repeated, useful relationships/interactions around the organisation/campaign. | Moves beyond one-way broadcasting. |
| Interaction | Audience action or communication involving campaign content/account. | Provides opportunities and risks requiring management. |
| Interaction triage | Classifying an interaction and selecting the appropriate response/moderation/escalation route. | Supports consistent professional handling. |
| Constructive criticism | Negative or critical feedback intended to identify a genuine issue/improvement. | Should not automatically be treated as abuse. |
| Moderation | Applying policy to manage inappropriate or prohibited content/behaviour. | Protects community, users and reputation. |
| Escalation | Passing an issue to someone with appropriate authority/expertise. | Needed when the learner/operator should not resolve the issue independently. |
| Misinformation | Incorrect or misleading information presented as factual. | May require careful correction depending on relevance/risk. |
| Community-management log | Record of significant interactions, decisions, policy basis and outcomes. | Provides implementation evidence and supports later evaluation. |
| Evaluative observation | Evidence-based provisional interpretation recorded during campaign activity. | Preserves useful reasoning for later evaluation without premature conclusions. |
