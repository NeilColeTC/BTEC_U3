# Week 13 Retrieval Quiz — Indicative Answers

1. Putting the reviewed campaign plan into operation.
2. Ability to connect campaign actions to requirements/objectives and evidence.
3. Any two appropriate checks: audience/purpose, accuracy, tone, branding, accessibility, CTA, privacy, permissions, policy, timing or readability.
4. A prompt indicating an intended next step for the audience.
5. To create reliable contemporaneous evidence and avoid reconstructing activity from memory.
6. Monitoring, responding to and managing interactions around the business/campaign community.
7. Passing an issue to someone with suitable authority or expertise.
8. Because legitimate criticism/complaints may be relevant feedback and do not necessarily breach policy.
9. Any two relevant techniques such as hashtags, tags, sharing, following or purposeful interaction.
10. Because early/small samples may not represent overall performance and other variables may explain the result.

Use diagnostically; learners should make their own assessed campaign decisions.
