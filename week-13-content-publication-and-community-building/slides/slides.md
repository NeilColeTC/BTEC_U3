# Week 13 Slides — Content Publication and Community Building

## Slide 1 — Week 13
### Content Publication and Community Building
Operate the campaign, not just the posting schedule.

---
## Slide 2 — C2/C3
Publish purposeful content, improve visibility and manage audience interaction professionally.

---
## Slide 3 — Operational cycle
**Publish → attract interaction → monitor → classify → respond/action → record → learn**

---
## Slide 4 — Week 12 to Week 13
Week 12 established the campaign. Week 13 develops the live campaign and its community.

---
## Slide 5 — Content variety
Variation can help reach different communication purposes and audience behaviours.

---
## Slide 6 — Variety needs a purpose
More formats/posts do not automatically make a stronger campaign.

---
## Slide 7 — Content purposes
Awareness • information • engagement • trust/community • audience action.

---
## Slide 8 — Content question
**What should the audience think, feel, know or do after this item?**

---
## Slide 9 — Platform adaptation
Retain campaign identity while adapting presentation to the selected platform.

---
## Slide 10 — Visibility
Good content cannot contribute much if the intended audience is unlikely to encounter it.

---
## Slide 11 — Visibility techniques
Where available/relevant: hashtags • tags • sharing • following • interaction.

---
## Slide 12 — Hashtags
Use relevant terms that support discovery/context; quantity alone is not a strategy.

---
## Slide 13 — Tags
Tagging should have a genuine relevance/relationship to the content or campaign.

---
## Slide 14 — Sharing
Sharing can extend reach, but relevance and audience fit still matter.

---
## Slide 15 — Following
Following relevant accounts can support awareness/community activity where appropriate to the platform and plan.

---
## Slide 16 — Evaluate the technique
**Technique → intended effect → relevance → risk/limitation → measure**

---
## Slide 17 — Community building
A community is developed through repeated useful interaction, not simply follower numbers.

---
## Slide 18 — Publication creates responsibility
If you invite comments/questions, you need an appropriate response process.

---
## Slide 19 — Interaction categories
Enquiry • positive engagement • constructive criticism • complaint • misinformation • spam • abuse • security/confidentiality concern.

---
## Slide 20 — Negative ≠ inappropriate
A critical comment may be legitimate and valuable. Do not moderate merely because feedback is uncomfortable.

---
## Slide 21 — Triage
**Classify → decide → respond/action → policy reason → escalate if needed → record**

---
## Slide 22 — Enquiries
Respond accurately and helpfully where authorised; escalate if information/authority is insufficient.

---
## Slide 23 — Positive engagement
Acknowledge appropriately and consider how interaction can strengthen community.

---
## Slide 24 — Constructive criticism
Listen, respond professionally and avoid defensiveness.

---
## Slide 25 — Complaints
Recognise the concern, protect privacy and follow escalation/response procedures.

---
## Slide 26 — Misinformation
Correct carefully where appropriate using accurate information; do not amplify unnecessarily.

---
## Slide 27 — Spam and abuse
Apply policy consistently. Moderation should have a policy reason, not personal preference.

---
## Slide 28 — Security/confidentiality
Do not expose sensitive information. Escalate appropriately.

---
## Slide 29 — Tone under pressure
Professionalism matters most when the interaction is difficult.

---
## Slide 30 — Evidence
Capture both what the campaign published and how it handled meaningful interaction.

---
## Slide 31 — Community-management log
Record interaction type, action, policy basis, evidence and outcome/next step.

---
## Slide 32 — Early observations
You can identify patterns without claiming final causation or success.

---
## Slide 33 — Observation framework
**Action → response/evidence → possible interpretation → limitation → next evidence needed**

---
## Slide 34 — Avoid premature conclusions
One popular post does not prove the entire campaign succeeded.

---
## Slide 35 — BC.D3 progression
Strong later evaluation depends on evidence of decisions, outcomes, limitations and consequences gathered now.

---
## Slide 36 — Your job today
Publish • improve visibility • interact • moderate/escalate • evidence • observe.

---
## Slide 37 — Next phase
### Analytics and Data Analysis
Week 14 turns accumulated campaign evidence into systematic performance analysis.
