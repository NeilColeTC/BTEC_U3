# Week 13 — Assessment and Understanding Checks

## Criteria

**C.P5:** continued campaign implementation/content operation.  
**BC.D3:** authentic evidence of individual responsibility, creativity and effective self-management may be accumulated during live operation.

## Checks

- [ ] publish purposeful content in line with the refined plan;
- [ ] maintain campaign identity while adapting to platforms;
- [ ] use relevant visibility techniques appropriately;
- [ ] monitor and classify audience interactions;
- [ ] respond/moderate/escalate according to policy;
- [ ] maintain privacy/confidentiality;
- [ ] update implementation and community-management evidence;
- [ ] demonstrate independent ownership of campaign tasks;
- [ ] demonstrate appropriate creativity/problem solving;
- [ ] maintain organised evidence and campaign workflow;
- [ ] record analytical observations for later BC.D2 evaluation without premature conclusions.

## Misconception

BC.D3 is not demonstrated by a final evaluation paragraph; the behaviour should be visible in authentic process evidence.
