## Reasoning framework

**action → response/evidence → possible interpretation → limitation → next evidence needed**

## Example of cautious reasoning

Weak: “The campaign is successful because this post got the most interactions.”

Stronger structure: identify the relative response, connect it to the content/target, consider reach/timing/audience or other explanations, and state what further evidence would be needed before judging overall success.

## Important

This log supports later BC.D3 evaluation. It is not a substitute for the final evaluation and should not invent causal relationships that the available evidence cannot demonstrate.
