## Purpose test

For each item ask:

1. What should the intended audience **think, feel, know or do** after seeing it?
2. Does this add a useful campaign purpose or merely repeat existing content?
3. Is the format appropriate for the selected platform?
4. Is the campaign identity still recognisable?
5. Is any planned content missing from the campaign sequence?

## Overall judgement

Which campaign purpose is currently strongest? Which is under-represented, if any? Support the answer from the plan rather than adding content simply to make the table look balanced.
