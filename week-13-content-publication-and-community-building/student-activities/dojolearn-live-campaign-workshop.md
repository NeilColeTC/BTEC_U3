# DojoLearn Practical — Live Campaign Workshop

## Purpose

Continue implementing the campaign while demonstrating purposeful publication, visibility/community techniques and professional interaction management.

## Stage 1 — Campaign status

Record:

- content already published;
- content due during this stage;
- current campaign baseline/targets;
- outstanding interactions;
- relevant policy/response rules.

## Stage 2 — Publish planned content

For each significant content item:

1. confirm requirement/objective;
2. complete the pre-publication quality check where appropriate;
3. adapt appropriately for the selected platform;
4. publish according to the plan/simulation conditions;
5. record the implementation evidence ID.

## Stage 3 — Visibility/community activity

Use appropriate features available in DojoLearn, such as relevant hashtags, tags, sharing, following or interaction where these fit the plan.

Record why the activity is relevant rather than merely recording that it was used.

## Stage 4 — Monitor interactions

For significant interactions:

**classify → decide → respond/action → policy reason → escalate if needed → record**.

Do not remove legitimate criticism merely because it is negative.

## Stage 5 — Evidence

Update the Week 12 implementation evidence log and the Week 13 community-management log. Evidence should show campaign operation, not simply the planning intention.

## Stage 6 — Early observations

Record observations using:

**action → audience response/evidence → possible interpretation → limitation → what to monitor next**.

Avoid final judgements until sufficient campaign evidence has accumulated.

## End-of-workshop check

- What was published?
- What visibility/community activity occurred?
- What interactions were handled?
- What evidence was captured?
- What remains scheduled?
- What should Week 14 analytics investigate?
