# Week 13 — Content Publication and Community Building

**Specification focus:** Learning Aim C — C2/C3  
**Assessment preparation:** C.P5 / BC.D3  
**Practical environment:** DojoLearn social-media simulation

## Week purpose

Week 13 develops the live campaign established in Week 12. Learners publish a purposeful range of campaign content, use appropriate visibility techniques and manage audience interaction professionally so that implementation evidence shows an active campaign rather than a collection of isolated posts.

The central operational cycle is:

**publish → attract interaction → monitor → classify → respond/action → record → learn**

## Learning outcomes

Learners should be able to:

1. Continue publishing varied campaign content in line with the refined plan.
2. Adapt content appropriately to platform, audience and purpose while retaining campaign identity.
3. Use relevant visibility/community techniques such as hashtags, tags, sharing and following where available and appropriate.
4. Monitor simulated audience interaction rather than treating publication as the end of the process.
5. Classify interaction and select an appropriate response, moderation or escalation action.
6. Apply the social-media policy developed during planning.
7. Capture evidence of publication and community-management activity.
8. Explain the relationship between campaign actions, audience responses and business requirements.
9. Develop evidence and evaluative observations for later BC.D3 work without prematurely concluding that the campaign succeeded.

## C2/C3 focus

This week concentrates on publishing campaign content, improving visibility, building community and managing interactions professionally.

## DojoLearn practical

Learners operate the live campaign across their selected simulated platforms, publish planned content, use available discovery/community features, respond to generated audience interactions and maintain their implementation evidence log.

## Assessment boundary

Learners should make their own publishing and interaction-management decisions. Resources may provide neutral classification frameworks and policy reminders but should not compose assessed campaign posts/responses or decide the learner's final evaluative judgement.
