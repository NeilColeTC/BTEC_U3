# Week 13 — Content Publication and Community Building

**Specification focus:** Learning Aim C — C2/C3/C5  
**Assessment preparation:** C.P5 plus BC.D3 professional-behaviour evidence  
**Practical environment:** DojoLearn social-media simulation

## Week purpose

Week 13 develops the live campaign established in Week 12. Learners publish purposeful campaign content, use appropriate visibility techniques and manage audience interaction professionally so implementation evidence shows an active, responsibly managed campaign.

The operational cycle is:

**publish → attract interaction → monitor → classify → respond/action → record → learn**

Alongside C.P5 evidence, learners should preserve authentic evidence relevant to BC.D3: individual responsibility, creativity and effective self-management, such as independent decisions, organised logs, professional responses, adherence to deadlines/processes and justified handling of issues.

## Assessment boundary

BC.D3 should not be treated as a final evaluative paragraph. It is demonstrated through behaviours evidenced across the campaign process.
