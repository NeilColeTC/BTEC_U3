# Week 13 Lesson Plan — Content Publication and Community Building

**Duration:** 3 hours  
**Learning Aim:** C  
**Specification:** C2/C3  
**Criteria preparation:** C.P5 / BC.D3

## Learning outcomes

Learners will continue operating their campaign in DojoLearn, publish purposeful varied content, improve visibility, manage interaction professionally and capture evidence of the live campaign.

## Lesson sequence

### 1. Retrieval and campaign-status check — 15 minutes

Retrieve the Week 12 implementation cycle, evidence requirements, calls to action, visibility techniques, community management, escalation and social-media policy.

Learners establish what is already live, what remains scheduled and which interactions/metrics now require attention.

### 2. Publication is not the finish line — 15 minutes

Introduce the operational cycle:

**publish → attract interaction → monitor → classify → respond/action → record → learn**.

Discuss why a campaign consisting only of scheduled posts is incomplete if the strategy expects audience interaction/community building.

### 3. Content variety with purpose — 20 minutes

Review variation in content purpose and format. Learners check that variety supports campaign objectives rather than adding novelty for its own sake.

Possible purposes include awareness, information, engagement/conversation, trust/community and action/conversion where appropriate to the campaign.

### 4. Visibility techniques — 20 minutes

Review available and relevant techniques such as hashtags, tags, sharing and following. Evaluate each using:

**technique → intended audience/discovery effect → relevance → risk/limitation → evidence to monitor**.

### 5. Interaction triage refresher — 20 minutes

Use neutral examples to classify enquiries, positive engagement, constructive criticism, complaints, misinformation, spam, abuse and security/confidentiality concerns.

Reinforce: **negative ≠ inappropriate**.

### Break — 10 minutes

### 6. DojoLearn live campaign workshop — 65 minutes

Learners:

- publish appropriate planned content;
- use suitable visibility/community features available in the simulation;
- monitor audience interactions;
- classify each significant interaction;
- respond, moderate or escalate in line with policy;
- record evidence as activity occurs.

### 7. Community-management and evaluative observation logs — 20 minutes

Learners record interaction evidence and early observations using cautious language:

**action → audience response/evidence → possible interpretation → limitation → what to monitor next**.

This develops BC.D3 reasoning without turning an early observation into a final campaign judgement.

### 8. Exit ticket — 15 minutes

Learners identify one purposeful content variation, one community-management decision and one observation that needs more evidence before a conclusion can be reached.

## Adaptive teaching

**Support:** content-purpose prompts, interaction categories, response decision tree and structured logs.  
**Stretch:** ambiguous interactions, cross-platform differences, conflicting policy/community goals, opportunity cost of response effort and cautious interpretation of early evidence.

## English, maths, EDI and employability

**English:** audience-appropriate writing, professional responses and accurate evidence annotations.  
**Maths:** early comparison of interaction/visibility measures without over-interpreting small samples.  
**EDI:** accessible/inclusive content and respectful moderation of differing viewpoints.  
**Employability:** live campaign operation, customer/community service, reputation management, escalation and evidence-based decision-making.

## Teacher reflection

- Is content varied for a campaign reason rather than simply for variety?
- Are visibility techniques purposeful?
- Are learners distinguishing criticism from abuse/spam?
- Do responses follow the planned policy?
- Is community-management evidence being captured?
- Are early observations appropriately cautious?
