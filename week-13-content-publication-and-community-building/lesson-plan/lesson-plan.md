# Week 13 Lesson Plan — Content Publication and Community Building

**Duration:** 3 hours  
**Specification:** C2/C3/C5  
**Criteria preparation:** C.P5 plus BC.D3 process evidence

## Operational cycle

**publish → attract interaction → monitor → classify → respond/action → record → learn**

## Lesson sequence

1. Campaign-status retrieval — 15 min.
2. Publication is not the finish line — 15 min.
3. Purposeful content variety — 20 min.
4. Visibility techniques — 20 min.
5. Interaction triage refresher — 20 min.
6. Break — 10 min.
7. DojoLearn live campaign workshop — 65 min.
8. Community-management and observation logs — 20 min.
9. Exit — 15 min.

## C.P5

Evidence should show planned content being published/operated appropriately and meaningful community activity.

## BC.D3 evidence opportunity

Capture authentic evidence of:

- taking individual responsibility for campaign operation;
- creative but appropriate content/problem solving;
- effective organisation, monitoring and evidence management;
- professional response to interactions/issues;
- self-managed adherence to the plan, policy and timescales.

Analytical observations remain useful for later **BC.D2** evaluation; they should not be mislabeled as BC.D3 evaluation.
