# Week 9 Lesson Plan — Content, Keywords and Publishing

**Duration:** 3 hours  
**Learning Aim:** B  
**Specification:** B3  
**Criteria preparation:** B.P3 / B.M2

## Learning outcomes

Learners will research discoverability terms, draft audience-appropriate campaign content and construct a detailed publishing schedule linked to campaign objectives.

## Lesson sequence

### 1. Retrieval from Week 8 — 15 minutes

Retrieve requirements, objectives, audience, platform suitability, content strategy, target measures and calls to action.

### 2. From strategy to individual content — 20 minutes

Model the planning chain:

**objective → audience need → content purpose → message/format → visibility choice → timing → intended response → measure**.

Stress that an attractive post without a campaign role is weak planning.

### 3. Keywords, hashtags and tags — 30 minutes

Teach the distinction between keyword language that communicates relevance and platform-supported hashtags/tags used for context/discovery. Learners research terms for a neutral scenario and judge relevance, specificity, audience fit and risk of overuse.

### 4. Drafting campaign content — 30 minutes

Learners draft content for contrasting purposes such as information, promotion, engagement and action. Check tone, message, format, accessibility and CTA where relevant.

### 5. Publishing schedule — 25 minutes

Learners build a calendar including platform, date/stage, content ID, purpose, format, key message, keywords/hashtags, CTA, intended outcome, responsibility and success measure.

### Break — 10 minutes

### 6. DojoLearn drafting workshop — 35 minutes

Learners rehearse/draft content in the simulated environment where possible, checking platform constraints and recording any mismatch between the plan and simulator capabilities. Do not launch the live campaign ahead of the implementation stage.

### 7. B.M2 justification practice — 25 minutes

Learners justify selected content and scheduling decisions for a neutral scenario using:

**decision → evidence/requirement → audience/objective link → benefit → constraint/trade-off → judgement**.

### 8. Exit ticket — 10 minutes

Learners identify one strong content decision, one keyword/hashtag principle and one scheduling factor that affects campaign feasibility.

## Adaptive teaching

**Support:** content-purpose cards, keyword-quality prompts, worked calendar row and sentence stems.  
**Stretch:** content sequencing, repetition/fatigue, platform-specific adaptations, conflicting timings/resources and measurement trade-offs.

## English, maths, EDI and employability

**English:** concise audience-focused writing and keyword/message selection.  
**Maths:** dates, frequencies, intervals and workload distribution across the campaign.  
**EDI:** inclusive wording, accessibility, representation and avoiding stereotypical audience assumptions.  
**Employability:** editorial planning, scheduling, copywriting, digital marketing and resource management.
