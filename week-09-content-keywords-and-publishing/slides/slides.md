# Week 9 Slides — Content, Keywords and Publishing

## Slide 1 — Week 9
### Content, Keywords and Publishing
Turn strategy into an implementable content plan.

---
## Slide 2 — Planning chain
**Objective → audience need → content purpose → message/format → visibility choice → timing → intended response → measure**

---
## Slide 3 — Strategy vs post
A content strategy defines roles across the campaign. A post is one implementation of that strategy.

---
## Slide 4 — Content must earn its place
What objective or requirement does this item support?

---
## Slide 5 — Keyword
A relevant word/phrase used in the message/topic to communicate relevance and support discoverability.

---
## Slide 6 — Hashtag
A platform-supported marker that can categorise/connect content where relevant.

---
## Slide 7 — Tag
A platform-supported reference to another account/entity where appropriate.

---
## Slide 8 — Relevance over quantity
More hashtags or keywords do not automatically mean more useful reach.

---
## Slide 9 — Keyword quality
Audience language • topic relevance • specificity • intent • brand fit.

---
## Slide 10 — Avoid keyword stuffing
Repeated/artificial wording can reduce clarity and professionalism.

---
## Slide 11 — Content purpose
Inform • promote • engage • reassure/support • direct action.

---
## Slide 12 — Message
What should the audience understand after seeing the content?

---
## Slide 13 — Format
Choose the format because it supports the message, audience and selected platform.

---
## Slide 14 — Tone
Maintain the planned business identity while adapting language to the situation/platform.

---
## Slide 15 — Accessibility
Readable text • useful captions/alternative descriptions where supported • clear contrast/layout • understandable language.

---
## Slide 16 — Call to action
Make the intended next step clear where the campaign objective requires one.

---
## Slide 17 — Publishing calendar
A campaign needs sequence, timing, responsibility and purpose — not just a folder of drafts.

---
## Slide 18 — Calendar fields
Platform • date/stage • content ID • purpose • format • message • keywords/tags • CTA • intended outcome • measure • responsibility.

---
## Slide 19 — Timing
Consider audience opportunity, campaign milestones, resource availability and content dependency.

---
## Slide 20 — Frequency
Too little activity may reduce visibility; too much can create repetition and workload. Context matters.

---
## Slide 21 — Content sequencing
Some content prepares the audience for later content/action.

---
## Slide 22 — Resources
Can the planned frequency and formats actually be produced and managed?

---
## Slide 23 — DojoLearn draft check
Test available platform constraints before implementation.

---
## Slide 24 — Do not launch early
Week 9 creates the publishing plan and drafts; implementation follows review/refinement.

---
## Slide 25 — B.P3
The final plan should be sufficiently detailed to implement.

---
## Slide 26 — B.M2
Explain why significant content, keyword and scheduling choices are appropriate.

---
## Slide 27 — Justification chain
**Decision → requirement/evidence → audience/objective link → benefit → constraint/trade-off → judgement**

---
