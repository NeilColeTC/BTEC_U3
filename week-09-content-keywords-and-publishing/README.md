# Week 9 — Content, Keywords and Publishing

**Specification focus:** Learning Aim B — B3  
**Assessment preparation:** B.P3 / B.M2  
**Practical environment:** DojoLearn social-media simulation

## Week purpose

Week 9 converts the Week 8 platform/content strategy into a detailed, implementable publishing plan. Learners research and select appropriate keywords/hashtags, draft campaign content, plan calls to action and build a content calendar that connects each item to audience, objective, timing and intended outcome.

The central planning chain is:

**objective → audience need → content purpose → message/format → keyword/visibility choice → timing → intended response → measure**

## Learning outcomes

Learners should be able to:

1. Select content that serves defined campaign objectives and audience needs.
2. Distinguish keywords, hashtags/tags and calls to action.
3. Research and justify relevant keyword/hashtag choices without keyword stuffing.
4. Draft platform-appropriate content while maintaining brand/tone consistency.
5. Produce a detailed publishing schedule covering platform, purpose, timing and intended outcome.
6. Check that planned content is feasible within available resources.
7. Use DojoLearn to draft/rehearse content without prematurely implementing the live campaign.
8. Justify significant content and scheduling decisions towards B.M2.

The structures support planning quality but do not supply final assessed posts, keyword choices or scheduling decisions. Learners must make and justify their own decisions for Assignment 2.
