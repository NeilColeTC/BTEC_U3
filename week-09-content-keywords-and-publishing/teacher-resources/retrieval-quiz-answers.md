1. A need/condition the campaign should satisfy.
2. Group the campaign intends to reach/influence.
3. How well platform characteristics match audience, objectives, content needs and constraints.
4. Planned approach to content purposes, formats, messages and intended response.
5. Intended level/outcome for a relevant campaign measure.
6. Prompt indicating an intended audience next step.
7. Gives a starting point for comparison and target setting.
8. Popularity may not match audience, purpose, content, resource or measurement needs.
9. Justification explaining why choices are appropriate against purpose/business requirements, including relevant evidence/trade-offs.
10. message/format or equivalent.
