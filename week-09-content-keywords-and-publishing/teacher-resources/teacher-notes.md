# Week 9 Teacher Notes — Content, Keywords and Publishing

## Curriculum intent

Week 9 fills the operational planning gap between Week 8 strategy and Week 10 community/policy. The plan should become detailed enough to implement after review.

## Keywords and hashtags

Avoid teaching these as guaranteed reach mechanisms. Relevance, platform support and audience/context matter. The aim is a justified approach, not maximum quantity.

## Content calendar

Require traceability. Each significant content item should have a campaign purpose, intended audience response and link to an objective/requirement. Encourage stable content IDs because they become useful in Weeks 12–16.

## DojoLearn

Use the simulator to expose platform constraints before implementation. Record limitations and adapt the plan where justified. Do not launch the campaign before Week 11 review/refinement is complete.

## B.M2

Require justification, not feature description:

**decision → requirement/evidence → audience/objective link → benefit → constraint/trade-off → judgement**.

## Assessment boundary

Teachers can teach drafting and scheduling processes, but final assessed messages, keywords, timings and justifications must remain learner decisions.
