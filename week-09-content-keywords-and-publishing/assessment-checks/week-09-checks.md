# Week 9 — Assessment and Understanding Checks

## Criteria progression

**B.P3:** detailed campaign planning.  
**B.M2:** justification of significant content/publishing decisions.

By the end of Week 9 learners should be able to:

- [ ] connect content items to requirements/objectives;
- [ ] distinguish keyword, hashtag and tag;
- [ ] select relevant discoverability terms without overuse;
- [ ] draft audience/platform-appropriate content;
- [ ] use appropriate calls to action;
- [ ] maintain planned tone/branding;
- [ ] consider accessibility/inclusion;
- [ ] produce a detailed content calendar;
- [ ] plan timing/frequency realistically;
- [ ] identify responsibilities and dependencies;
- [ ] recognise DojoLearn platform constraints;
- [ ] adapt the plan where evidence justifies it;
- [ ] justify significant choices at B.M2 level.

## Common misconceptions

- [ ] “More hashtags always means more reach.”
- [ ] “Keywords and hashtags are the same thing.”
- [ ] “A content calendar is just dates and post titles.”
- [ ] “Every post needs a sales CTA.”
- [ ] “More frequent posting is always better.”
- [ ] “Drafting in DojoLearn means the live campaign has started.”
- [ ] “A list of content features is B.M2 justification.”

## Transition to Week 10

Learners should leave with content IDs, draft content and a realistic publishing calendar that Week 10 can extend with community-management and policy arrangements.
