## Calendar audit

- Does every item connect to an objective/requirement?
- Is the content mix purposeful rather than repetitive?
- Is timing realistic?
- Are responsibilities clear?
- Are relevant measures identified?
- Is community-response workload considered?
- Are dependencies/sequences visible?

## Resource check

Estimate whether the planned frequency and content formats can be produced and managed with the available time/resources.
