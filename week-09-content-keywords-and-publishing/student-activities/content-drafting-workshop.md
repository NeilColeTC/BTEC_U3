## Quality check

For each draft ask:

1. Which objective/requirement does it support?
2. Is the intended audience clear?
3. Is the message useful and accurate?
4. Is the format suitable for the selected platform?
5. Is the CTA necessary and clear?
6. Are keywords/tags relevant rather than decorative?
7. Does tone/branding remain consistent?
8. Is the content accessible and inclusive?
