# DojoLearn Practical — Draft Content Workshop

## Purpose

Test campaign drafts and platform constraints before the live implementation stage.

## Workflow

1. Select a planned content item.
2. Confirm its requirement/objective and audience.
3. Open the intended simulated platform.
4. Check available content/format/visibility features.
5. Draft or rehearse the item as directed by your teacher.
6. Record any platform limitation or required adaptation.
7. Update the content calendar where the evidence justifies a change.
