## Quality questions

- Would the intended audience understand/use this language?
- Is it genuinely connected to the content?
- Is it so broad that it adds little value?
- Is it so narrow that it loses relevance?
- Does the platform actually support the feature?
- Could overuse make the content look artificial or spam-like?

## Decision

Select a small set of justified terms for a neutral practice post and explain what each contributes.
