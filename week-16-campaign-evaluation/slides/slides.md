# Week 16 Slides — Campaign Evaluation and Professional Behaviours

## Slide 1 — Week 16
### Campaign Evaluation and Professional Behaviours
Two distinction strands: **BC.D2 evaluation** and **BC.D3 behaviours**.

---
## Slide 2 — Evidence chain
Requirements → plan → refinement → implementation → interaction → analytics → optimisation → evaluation.

---
## Slide 3 — BC.D2
Evaluate the **plan and use of social media in the business against business requirements**.

---
## Slide 4 — Description vs analysis vs evaluation
Description = what happened. Analysis = relationships/patterns. Evaluation = supported judgement against requirements/criteria.

---
## Slide 5 — Evaluation structure
**Requirement/question → evidence → comparison → judgement → significance → limitation → recommendation**

---
## Slide 6 — Evaluate the plan
Objectives • audience • platform • content • schedule • resources • policy • review/refinement.

---
## Slide 7 — Evaluate implementation
What was actually configured, published, managed and changed?

---
## Slide 8 — Performance evidence
Baseline/targets • content analytics • audience evidence • community interactions • optimisation results.

---
## Slide 9 — Mixed evidence
Success against one requirement does not guarantee success against another.

---
## Slide 10 — Limitations
Simulation conditions • observation period • metric availability • unequal exposure • confounding variables • audience-data limitations.

---
## Slide 11 — Recommendations
Recommendations should solve evidenced problems/opportunities and state how success could be measured.

---
## Slide 12 — BC.D3
Demonstrate **individual responsibility, creativity and effective self-management** in planning and using social media in a business context.

---
## Slide 13 — Responsibility evidence
Ownership of tasks/decisions • following through on plans • professional responses • handling issues appropriately • evidence accountability.

---
## Slide 14 — Creativity evidence
Original, appropriate content/solutions • adaptation to platform/audience • problem solving • evidence-led improvement.

---
## Slide 15 — Self-management evidence
Organisation • deadlines • content/evidence IDs • version control • response to feedback • prioritisation • managing changes.

---
## Slide 16 — BC.D3 is demonstrated
Writing “I was responsible” is weak unless authentic process evidence shows the behaviour.

---
## Slide 17 — Useful evidence sources
Planning versions • refinement logs • IMP/COM/ANA/OPT records • content development • response decisions • deadlines/task records.

---
## Slide 18 — Professional behaviours
Accuracy • communication • etiquette • accountability • decision making • response to evidence and feedback.

---
## Slide 19 — Legal/ethical implications
Use relevant campaign evidence to evaluate consequences under BC.D2 and demonstrate responsible conduct under BC.D3.

---
## Slide 20 — Traceability
Every important BC.D2 judgement and BC.D3 behaviour claim should point to evidence.

---
## Slide 21 — Avoid retrospective invention
Do not invent missing behaviours, reasons, targets or outcomes now.

---
## Slide 22 — Final test
BC.D2: **How effective was the plan/use against requirements?**  
BC.D3: **What authentic evidence demonstrates responsibility, creativity and self-management?**

---
## Slide 23 — Week 17
Cross-reference, quality-assure and submit the evidence package.
