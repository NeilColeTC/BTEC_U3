# Week 16 Lesson Plan — Campaign Evaluation and Professional Behaviours

**Duration:** 3 hours  
**Specification:** C5 / combined B-C criteria  
**Criteria:** BC.D2 and BC.D3

## Lesson outcomes

Learners will evaluate the plan/use against business requirements for BC.D2 and audit authentic evidence of individual responsibility, creativity and effective self-management for BC.D3.

## Sequence

1. Evidence-chain retrieval — 15 min.
2. Description/analysis/evaluation distinction — 20 min.
3. BC.D2 plan evaluation — 25 min.
4. BC.D2 implementation/performance evaluation — 25 min.
5. Legal/ethical/professional implications in context — 20 min.
6. Break — 10 min.
7. Evidence synthesis — 30 min.
8. BC.D3 behaviour-evidence audit — 25 min.
9. Recommendations/prioritisation — 20 min.
10. Final readiness/exit — 10 min.

## BC.D2 framework

**business requirement → evidence → comparison → judgement → significance → limitation → recommendation**

## BC.D3 framework

For each behaviour claim, require:

**behaviour → authentic evidence → context → significance**

Focus on individual responsibility, appropriate creativity and effective self-management across planning and use.

## Boundary

Do not manufacture behaviour examples or personalise final judgements for learners.
