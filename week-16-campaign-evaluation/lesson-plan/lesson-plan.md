# Week 16 Lesson Plan — Campaign Evaluation

**Duration:** 3 hours  
**Learning Aims:** B/C  
**Specification:** C5 and combined B/C evaluation  
**Criteria preparation:** BC.D2 / BC.D3

## Learning outcomes

Learners will synthesise evidence from planning through optimisation and reach balanced, justified conclusions about the quality of the plan and effectiveness of the implemented campaign.

## Lesson sequence

### 1. Retrieval and evidence-chain audit — 15 minutes

Retrieve requirements, success criteria, plan review, implementation evidence, community-management evidence, analytics findings, optimisation evidence and limitations.

Build the chain:

**requirements → plan → refinement → implementation → interaction → analytics → optimisation → evaluation**.

### 2. Description, analysis and evaluation — 20 minutes

Contrast:

- **description:** what happened;
- **analysis:** relationships/patterns and what they may indicate;
- **evaluation:** judgement of value/effectiveness against stated criteria, supported by evidence and limitations.

Introduce:

**criterion → evidence → comparison → judgement → significance → limitation → recommendation**.

### 3. Evaluate the plan — 25 minutes

Learners examine whether planning decisions were fit for purpose: requirements coverage, objectives/success criteria, audience, platform choices, content strategy, publishing, policy, resources, timescales and review/refinement.

### 4. Evaluate campaign performance — 25 minutes

Use baseline/target evidence, individual-content performance, intended/actual audience findings, community interaction and optimisation evidence. Require a balanced judgement rather than a list of metrics.

### 5. Legal, ethical and professional implications — 20 minutes

Review how campaign operation addressed relevant legal/ethical responsibilities, privacy/confidentiality, permissions/copyright, accessibility/inclusion, moderation, reputation and professional behaviour. Evaluate consequences rather than merely naming issues.

### Break — 10 minutes

### 6. Evidence synthesis workshop — 40 minutes

Learners organise evidence under evaluation questions and identify strongest supporting evidence, contradictory evidence, limitations and missing evidence.

### 7. Recommendations and prioritisation — 25 minutes

Develop future recommendations using:

**evidence/problem → proposed improvement → expected benefit → requirement/objective affected → priority → limitation/resource implication**.

### 8. BC.D2/BC.D3 readiness and exit ticket — 20 minutes

Learners check that their conclusions are balanced, evidence-based and traceable and that recommendations follow logically from the evaluation.

## Adaptive teaching

**Support:** evaluation matrices, evidence IDs, judgement vocabulary, significance prompts and recommendation structure.  
**Stretch:** conflicting evidence, trade-offs, confidence levels, counterfactual reasoning, opportunity cost and prioritised strategic recommendations.

## English, maths, EDI and employability

**English:** synthesis, evaluative vocabulary, structured argument and evidence referencing.  
**Maths:** use of relevant analytics, targets, rates and comparisons to substantiate judgements.  
**EDI:** evaluate accessibility/inclusion and avoid unsupported assumptions about audience groups.  
**Employability:** campaign reporting, reflective practice, stakeholder communication, professional judgement and continuous improvement.

## Teacher reflection

- Are learners evaluating against explicit criteria?
- Is evidence traceable?
- Are judgements balanced?
- Do learners explain significance rather than merely stating strengths/weaknesses?
- Are legal/ethical/professional issues evaluated in campaign context?
- Do recommendations follow from evidence?
