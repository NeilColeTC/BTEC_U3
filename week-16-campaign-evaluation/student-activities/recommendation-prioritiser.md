## Priority test

Rank recommendations using:

- significance to the business requirement/objective;
- strength of evidence;
- likely benefit;
- feasibility/resources;
- risk;
- measurability.

## Final recommendation statement

**Because [evidence/problem], the campaign should [improvement]. This is a [priority] recommendation because [business significance]. Success should be measured using [measure]. A relevant constraint is [constraint].**
