## Evaluation structure

For significant areas use:

**criterion → evidence → comparison → judgement → significance → limitation**.

## Overall plan judgement

Was the plan fit for purpose against the business requirements? Avoid a simple yes/no: state the extent, strongest evidence, most important weakness and its consequence.
