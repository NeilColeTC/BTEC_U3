# Activity — Campaign Performance Evaluation

## Additional evidence

Consider:

- individual high/low content performance;
- intended-versus-actual audience evidence;
- community-management outcomes;
- deviations from the plan;
- optimisation before/after evidence;
- qualitative as well as quantitative evidence.

## Overall campaign judgement

Use a balanced structure:

**overall judgement → strongest evidence → evidence challenging the judgement → most significant limitation → implication**.
