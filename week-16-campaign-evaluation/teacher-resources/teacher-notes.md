# Week 16 Teacher Notes — Campaign Evaluation and Professional Behaviours

## Critical criterion distinction

- **BC.D2:** evaluate the plan and use of social media in a business against business requirements.
- **BC.D3:** demonstrate individual responsibility, creativity and effective self-management in planning and using social media in a business context.

Do not treat BC.D3 as a second final-evaluation criterion.

## BC.D2

Use requirements and success criteria as benchmarks. Encourage balanced evidence, significance, limitations and recommendations.

## BC.D3

Look for authentic process evidence accumulated across Weeks 7–15: independent decisions, organised planning/evidence, creative content/problem solving, professional responses, time/deadline management, response to feedback and evidence-led changes.

A learner statement such as “I was responsible” is weak without traceable evidence.

## Legal/ethical/professional implications

These can inform BC.D2 evaluation where they affected campaign effectiveness and can also demonstrate responsible professional conduct under BC.D3 where authentically evidenced.

## Assessment boundary

Teachers may help learners locate evidence and understand criteria. Do not write evaluation conclusions or invent professional-behaviour evidence.
