# Week 16 — Campaign Evaluation and Professional Behaviours Review

**Specification focus:** Learning Aim C — C5 and combined Learning Aims B/C  
**Assessment preparation:** BC.D2 / BC.D3  
**Practical environment:** accumulated DojoLearn campaign evidence

## Week purpose

Week 16 brings together requirements, planning, review/refinement, implementation, community management, analytics and optimisation.

- **BC.D2:** learners evaluate the plan and use of social media in the business against business requirements.
- **BC.D3:** learners demonstrate individual responsibility, creativity and effective self-management in planning and using social media in a business context.

The week therefore has two complementary strands: an evidence-based campaign evaluation for BC.D2 and an evidence audit/reflection on authentic professional behaviours for BC.D3.

## BC.D2 evaluation cycle

**requirement/question → evidence → comparison → judgement → significance → limitation → recommendation**

## BC.D3 evidence themes

- individual responsibility and ownership;
- creativity appropriate to the business context;
- effective self-management, organisation and time management;
- professional communication and decision making;
- response to feedback, issues and changing evidence;
- organised evidence trails showing independent action.

## Assessment boundary

Teachers may help learners locate authentic behaviour evidence but should not manufacture examples of responsibility, creativity or self-management that did not occur.
