# Week 16 — Campaign Evaluation

**Specification focus:** Learning Aim C — C5 and combined Learning Aims B/C evaluation  
**Assessment preparation:** BC.D2 / BC.D3  
**Practical environment:** DojoLearn accumulated campaign evidence

## Week purpose

Week 16 brings together the complete campaign journey: requirements, planning, review/refinement, implementation, community management, analytics and optimisation. Learners evaluate the plan and implemented campaign against business requirements, success criteria and performance evidence, while considering legal/ethical implications and professional behaviours.

The central evaluation cycle is:

**criterion/question → evidence → comparison → judgement → significance → limitation → recommendation**

## Learning outcomes

Learners should be able to:

1. Distinguish evaluation from description and analysis.
2. Evaluate the quality and fitness for purpose of the campaign plan.
3. Evaluate implementation against business requirements and success criteria.
4. Judge campaign performance using accumulated analytics and interaction evidence.
5. Evaluate the effect of optimisation decisions and deviations from the original plan.
6. Consider legal, ethical, reputational and professional implications of campaign decisions.
7. Balance strengths and weaknesses rather than presenting a one-sided account.
8. Use traceable evidence from across Weeks 7–15.
9. State limitations that affect confidence in conclusions.
10. Make prioritised, evidence-based recommendations for future campaign development.

## Evidence chain

**requirements → plan → review/refinement → implementation → interaction → analytics → optimisation → evaluation**

## DojoLearn practical

No new campaign feature is required. Learners use accumulated DojoLearn evidence, analytics, implementation logs, community-management records and optimisation evidence to support reasoned conclusions.
