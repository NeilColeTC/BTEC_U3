# Week 12 Teacher Notes — Campaign Implementation

## Curriculum intent

The repository scaffold defines Week 12 as C1/C2: create/configure professional business profiles, apply branding consistently and begin publishing campaign content in line with the plan. DojoLearn is the major implementation workshop and C.P5 is the assessment focus.

## Change in learning mode

Weeks 7–11 were primarily about making and refining decisions. Week 12 begins the phase where learners need **observable evidence of carrying those decisions out**.

Keep returning to:

**refined plan → implementation → evidence**.

## Implementation gate

Learners should enter the workshop with the reviewed/refined plan and final Week 11 configuration/baseline. If major planning gaps remain, implementation can create evidence of an incoherent campaign. Resolve/document readiness issues first.

## Professional profiles

Judge against available simulator features and campaign requirements. Avoid implying that DojoLearn necessarily reproduces every feature of a commercial platform. Where a planned profile element is unavailable, record the limitation.

## Branding

Teach consistency rather than identical duplication. Platform-specific presentation may differ while maintaining common identity, tone and campaign message.

## Pre-publication QA

Require learners to check content before publishing. This provides a natural point to revisit accessibility, policy, privacy/confidentiality, permissions, accuracy and audience appropriateness.

## Evidence capture

This is a critical habit for Learning Aim C. Learners should capture evidence during implementation rather than attempting to recreate the process later.

Encourage an indexed evidence log such as IMP-01, IMP-02 and so on. Each evidence item should state what it demonstrates.

## Deviations

Implementation may reveal limitations or practical issues. Do not teach learners that following the plan blindly is always correct. Instead require significant deviations to be recorded using:

**difference → reason/evidence → decision → effect → follow-up**.

This creates useful material for later C.M3/BC.D3 analysis.

## C.P5

The learner needs to demonstrate campaign implementation in line with the plan. Evidence should therefore show actual configuration, profiles/content and campaign operation rather than restating intentions.

## Assessment boundary

Teachers can support use of the simulation and remind learners of their own plan/checklists. Avoid rewriting assessed posts, selecting the learner's content or making implementation decisions for them.

## Preparation for later weeks

Week 12 should leave learners with campaign activity underway, an organised evidence log, baseline/targets available and clear understanding of what needs monitoring next.
