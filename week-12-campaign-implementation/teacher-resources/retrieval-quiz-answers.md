# Week 12 Retrieval Quiz — Indicative Answers

1. A need, expectation or condition the campaign should satisfy because of the business/client context.
2. Ability to connect decisions/actions to requirements/objectives and evidence.
3. To identify weaknesses and improve/refine the plan before implementation.
4. A controlled improvement/adjustment made following review/evidence.
5. Because implementation should represent the final reviewed campaign rather than an obsolete draft.
6. Recorded campaign state immediately before implementation, used for later comparison.
7. Any two relevant examples such as authorised use, professional conduct, confidentiality, security, copyright/permissions, moderation or approval processes.
8. A prompt indicating an intended next step for the audience.
9. To identify errors, policy/privacy issues, inconsistencies and misalignment with the campaign before content becomes live.
10. Planning defines intended activity; implementation puts that activity into operation and produces evidence of what actually happened.

Use diagnostically; do not provide assessed implementation decisions.
