# Week 12 Slides — Campaign Implementation

## Slide 1 — Week 12
### Campaign Implementation
From plan to evidence.

---
## Slide 2 — Learning Aim C
The focus now changes from planning what should happen to demonstrating what you actually implement.

---
## Slide 3 — Implementation cycle
**Refined plan → configure → quality check → publish → capture evidence → compare with plan**

---
## Slide 4 — Week 11 gate
Do not implement an obsolete draft. Use the reviewed/refined plan and final configuration.

---
## Slide 5 — Traceability
For significant activity, you should be able to identify which requirement/objective it supports.

---
## Slide 6 — Plan vs implementation
**Planned action → implemented action → evidence → outcome/observation**

---
## Slide 7 — Professional profile
A business profile should communicate a clear, appropriate and consistent organisational identity using the features available.

---
## Slide 8 — Profile checks
Identity • description/information • branding • links/details where supported • consistency • appropriateness.

---
## Slide 9 — DojoLearn limitations
Use features actually available in the simulation. Do not invent missing profile functions.

---
## Slide 10 — Branding
Branding includes more than a logo: visual choices, tone, message, identity and consistency.

---
## Slide 11 — Consistency ≠ duplication
Different platform/content formats can be adapted while still representing the same campaign identity.

---
## Slide 12 — Tone of voice
Does the implemented communication sound appropriate for the business, audience and planned campaign?

---
## Slide 13 — Before you publish
Publication is harder to evidence/control retrospectively. Use a quality gate first.

---
## Slide 14 — Quality gate 1
Correct account/platform • intended audience • intended purpose/objective.

---
## Slide 15 — Quality gate 2
Message accuracy • tone • branding • appropriate media.

---
## Slide 16 — Quality gate 3
Accessibility • call to action • planned keywords/hashtags where relevant.

---
## Slide 17 — Quality gate 4
Policy/privacy • permissions/copyright • timing • readability.

---
## Slide 18 — Content should earn its place
What requirement/objective does this content support?

---
## Slide 19 — Calls to action
Where an objective depends on audience action, make the intended next step appropriately clear.

---
## Slide 20 — Platform-specific adaptation
The same campaign purpose may require different presentation across platforms. Adaptation should be planned/justified, not random.

---
## Slide 21 — Publish to the plan
Follow the refined content/publication schedule where the simulation and lesson timing allow.

---
## Slide 22 — C.P5
Implementation evidence should demonstrate that the campaign was actually created and operated, not merely described.

---
## Slide 23 — Evidence quality
Useful evidence is attributable, dated/staged, understandable and connected to what it is intended to prove.

---
## Slide 24 — Capture as you go
Do not rely on memory at the end of the campaign.

---
## Slide 25 — Evidence types
Simulator views/screenshots where permitted • implementation log • published content • profile/configuration evidence • metric snapshots • response records.

---
## Slide 26 — Annotate evidence
State what the evidence shows and why it matters. Avoid unexplained screenshot collections.

---
## Slide 27 — Deviations happen
Implementation may expose practical issues that were not visible during planning.

---
## Slide 28 — Do not hide deviations
Record them and explain the decision.

---
## Slide 29 — Deviation record
**Difference → reason/evidence → decision → effect on requirements/objectives → follow-up**

---
## Slide 30 — Good implementation judgement
Changing something because the evidence requires it can be stronger than blindly following a plan.

---
## Slide 31 — But avoid random changes
Every unplanned change can affect later evaluation and traceability.

---
## Slide 32 — Baseline and targets
Keep Week 11 baseline and campaign targets available for later monitoring.

---
## Slide 33 — Your job today
Configure • check • publish • evidence • record.

---
## Slide 34 — End-of-session check
What is live? What evidence proves it? What is due next? What should be monitored?

---
## Slide 35 — Next phase
### Monitoring and Optimisation
Implementation creates the data needed for evidence-based improvement.
