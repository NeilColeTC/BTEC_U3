# Week 12 Lesson Plan — Campaign Implementation

**Duration:** 3 hours  
**Learning Aim:** C  
**Specification:** C1/C2  
**Criteria preparation:** C.P5

## Learning outcomes

Learners will configure and begin implementing their refined campaign in DojoLearn while maintaining evidence of what they actually did.

## Lesson sequence

### 1. Retrieval and implementation gate — 15 minutes

Retrieve requirement, objective, target audience, platform choice, content strategy, baseline, stakeholder refinement and social-media policy.

Before implementation, confirm that the learner has a reviewed/refined plan and matching DojoLearn configuration from Week 11.

### 2. Plan vs implementation — 15 minutes

Introduce the principle that implementation is evidence of carrying out the plan, not an opportunity to abandon it without explanation.

Use:

**planned action → implemented action → evidence → outcome/observation**.

### 3. Professional business profiles — 20 minutes

Review available profile/configuration features in DojoLearn. Discuss professional identity, appropriate profile information, consistency and the relationship between the profile and campaign purpose.

### 4. Branding consistency — 20 minutes

Learners check planned visual identity, tone, message and profile/content consistency. Emphasise that consistency does not mean every post must be identical.

### 5. Pre-publication quality assurance — 20 minutes

Use a quality gate covering:

- correct account/platform;
- intended audience/purpose;
- message accuracy;
- tone/brand consistency;
- appropriate media;
- accessibility considerations;
- call to action where relevant;
- planned keywords/hashtags where relevant;
- policy/privacy/copyright checks;
- timing/schedule;
- spelling/readability.

### Break — 10 minutes

### 6. DojoLearn major implementation workshop — 65 minutes

Learners configure profiles/settings and publish the first appropriate planned campaign content across selected simulated platforms. They capture evidence as they work rather than attempting to reconstruct it afterwards.

Teacher circulates using the Week 12 checks but does not rewrite assessed content.

### 7. Implementation evidence and deviation log — 20 minutes

Learners organise evidence and record any significant difference between plan and implementation:

**difference → reason/evidence → decision → effect on requirements/objectives → follow-up**.

### 8. Exit ticket and readiness for monitoring — 15 minutes

Learners identify what has been implemented, what evidence exists and what must be monitored next.

## Adaptive teaching

**Support:** stepwise simulator checklist, pre-publication checklist, evidence-log template and verbal rehearsal of implementation decisions.  
**Stretch:** cross-platform consistency, justified platform-specific adaptation, dependency management and analysis of unavoidable implementation deviations.

## English, maths, EDI and employability

**English:** professional profile copy, concise campaign communication and accurate evidence annotation.  
**Maths:** dates/times, baseline/target checking and later metric comparison.  
**EDI:** accessibility and inclusive communication checks before publication.  
**Employability:** campaign deployment, quality assurance, digital professionalism, evidence management and change control.

## Teacher reflection

- Does implementation match the refined plan?
- Are profiles/business identities professional and consistent?
- Are learners quality-checking before publishing?
- Is evidence being captured contemporaneously?
- Are deviations explained rather than hidden?
