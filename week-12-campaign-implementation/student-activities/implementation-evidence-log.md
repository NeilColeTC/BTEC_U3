## Evidence quality check

Evidence should be:

- attributable to the learner/campaign;
- understandable without relying entirely on memory;
- linked to a specific implementation action;
- dated or placed clearly within the campaign sequence;
- retained in an organised location;
- sufficient to demonstrate what actually happened.

## Avoid

- dozens of unexplained screenshots;
- evidence with no connection to a requirement/objective;
- reconstructing dates/actions after the campaign where contemporaneous evidence was available;
- claiming a feature/action that the simulator does not show.
