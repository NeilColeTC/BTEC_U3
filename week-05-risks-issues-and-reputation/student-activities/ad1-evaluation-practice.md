# A.D1 Formative Practice — Evaluating Business Use of Social Media

**This is formative practice, not Assignment 1 evidence.**

## Scenario

A fictional independent fitness and wellbeing centre wants to use social media to promote classes and communicate with current and potential customers. It expects social media to improve awareness, answer common questions and encourage bookings, but management is concerned about staff time, public complaints and reputational risk.

## Task

Evaluate the business use of social media to:

- interact with customers; and
- promote products/services to the target audience.

Your evaluation should draw on knowledge from Weeks 2–5.

## Include

- significant benefits of social-media interaction/promotion;
- relevance to the intended audience and business objectives;
- significant risks/issues;
- consequences of those risks;
- ways risks could be managed or reduced;
- comparison of the significance of benefits and drawbacks;
- a reasoned overall conclusion about when/why social media would be worthwhile.

## Evaluation structure

**Benefit → why significant → risk/limitation → why significant → mitigation/condition → judgement**

Your final conclusion should answer:

1. Is social media valuable for this business?
2. Which use provides the greatest value and why?
3. Which risk matters most and why?
4. Can that risk be managed sufficiently?
5. What conditions would need to be in place for your recommendation?
