# Activity — Content Consequence Cases

For each fictional case identify the immediate issue, affected stakeholders, possible consequences and an appropriate response/control.

## Case A — Humour misfires

A business publishes a humorous post intended to attract attention. Some customers interpret it as insensitive and begin criticising the business publicly.

## Case B — Customer details

An employee responds publicly to a complaint and includes information about the customer's order that should have remained private.

## Case C — Unchecked promotion

A promotional post contains an inaccurate claim about a service. Users begin sharing screenshots before the business notices the error.

## Case D — Account compromise

A staff member follows a convincing login link received in a message. An unauthorised person gains access to the business account.

## Case E — Copyright/permission

A business finds an image online and republishes it in promotional content without checking whether it has permission to use it.
