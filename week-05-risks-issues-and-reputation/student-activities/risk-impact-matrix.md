## Prioritise

1. Which risk would you address first and why?
2. Is the highest-likelihood risk necessarily the most important? Explain.
3. Which controls reduce likelihood? Which reduce impact?
4. Can any risk be removed completely? What does this imply for business decision making?

## Stretch

Choose one risk and explain how the business benefit that creates the opportunity may also create the risk.
