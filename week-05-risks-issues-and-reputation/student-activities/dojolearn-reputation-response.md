# DojoLearn Practical — Reputation and Response

## Purpose

Practise professional decision making when a business receives comments, complaints or other problematic interactions.

## Response workflow

For each scenario supplied through the simulation:

1. **Classify** the interaction.
2. **Assess** whether a response is needed.
3. **Choose** public response, private follow-up, escalation, moderation or another proportionate action.
4. **Draft/respond** professionally as directed.
5. **Record** why you chose the action.
6. **Evaluate** how the action could affect the customer and wider audience.

## Professional-response checklist

Before responding, ask:

- Is the tone calm and professional?
- Have I acknowledged the issue appropriately?
- Am I making claims I cannot verify?
- Am I exposing personal/confidential information?
- Should detailed discussion move away from a public channel?
- Is this within my authority to resolve?
- Does it need escalation?
- How could another customer interpret this response?

## Reflection

Choose your most difficult scenario. Explain why the decision was difficult and what principle ultimately determined your response.
