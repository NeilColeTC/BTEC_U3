# Week 5 Key Terminology

| Term | Working definition | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Reputation | How a business is perceived by customers and other stakeholders. | Social-media interactions can strengthen or damage perception. |
| Negative comment | Public/private criticism or dissatisfaction expressed by a user. | Businesses need proportionate monitoring and response decisions. |
| Reputational damage | Harm to trust or perception of a business. | A3 identifies this as an important consequence of social-media activity. |
| Time constraint | Limitation caused by the staff time required to create, monitor and manage activity. | Businesses should consider return on time/resources. |
| Unforeseen consequence | An outcome that was not intended when content/action was created. | Online content can spread or be interpreted beyond its intended context. |
| Security threat | A potential event/action that could compromise an account, system or information. | Business accounts and information require protection. |
| Account compromise | Unauthorised access/control of an account. | Can lead to harmful posts, fraud, information exposure or loss of trust. |
| Sensitive information | Information requiring protection because disclosure could cause harm or breach responsibilities. | Businesses should not expose confidential/personal information through social activity. |
| Personal information | Information relating to an identifiable person. | Handling customer information requires care and appropriate protection. |
| Likelihood | Judgement of how probable a risk event is. | Helps prioritise risks. |
| Impact | Judgement of how serious the consequences could be. | High-impact risks may require strong controls even when less likely. |
| Mitigation/control | Action intended to reduce likelihood or impact of a risk. | Evaluation should consider whether drawbacks can be managed. |
| Escalation | Passing an issue to someone with appropriate authority/expertise. | Staff should recognise when an interaction exceeds their responsibility. |
| Moderation | Managing content/interactions according to appropriate rules or policy. | Supports safe/professional community management. |
| Evaluate | Weigh relevant factors and their significance to reach a reasoned conclusion. | Central command term for A.D1. |

These are working teaching definitions, not assignment-ready paragraphs.
