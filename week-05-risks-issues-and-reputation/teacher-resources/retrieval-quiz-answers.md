# Week 5 Retrieval Quiz — Indicative Answers

1. Group a business intends to reach.
2. Reach concerns exposure/visibility; engagement concerns interaction.
3. Examples: booking, enquiry, registration, purchase or another objective-linked action.
4. Text, image, video, link, poll, quiz — any three.
5. Examples: keywords, relevant tags/hashtags, networking, sharing, advertising.
6. Consider strengths/limitations/context, compare factors and reach a supported judgement.
7. Likes show interaction but may not demonstrate the intended business action/outcome.
8. Cost, poor targeting, unsuitable content or failure to produce useful outcomes are acceptable contextual limitations.
9. Other users may observe the interaction and form opinions about the business.
10. Accept reasoned answers such as rapid/public communication also allowing rapid/public criticism or mistakes to spread.

Use diagnostically and require learners to elaborate verbally where useful.
