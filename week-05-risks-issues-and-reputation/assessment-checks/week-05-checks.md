# Week 5 — Assessment and Understanding Checks

## Primary progression

**A.D1:** evaluate the business use of social media to interact with customers and promote products/services to a target audience.

## Core checks

By the end of Week 5, learners should be able to:

- [ ] identify the principal A3 risks/issues;
- [ ] explain consequences of negative comments/reputational damage;
- [ ] explain time/resource constraints and return on time;
- [ ] analyse unforeseen consequences of content;
- [ ] identify relevant security threats;
- [ ] explain why sensitive/personal information requires protection;
- [ ] distinguish likelihood from impact;
- [ ] propose proportionate controls/mitigation;
- [ ] classify different customer/reputation incidents;
- [ ] draft a professional response without exposing private information;
- [ ] distinguish **assess** from **evaluate**;
- [ ] weigh benefits and risks rather than merely list them;
- [ ] reach a supported overall judgement about interaction and promotion.

## Evaluation quality

### Secure Distinction preparation

Learner synthesises business purpose, target audience, interaction/promotion benefits and A3 risks; weighs significance; considers mitigation; and reaches a reasoned contextual conclusion.

### Developing

Learner explains relevant benefits and risks and gives a conclusion, but significance, mitigation or synthesis is limited.

### Requires intervention

Learner lists generic advantages/disadvantages, gives absolute claims, or discusses risks without connecting them to business use and target audience.

## Common misconceptions

- [ ] “Evaluate means give more advantages and disadvantages.”
- [ ] “All negative comments should be deleted.”
- [ ] “The customer is always wrong/right.”
- [ ] “Move it to private messages” solves every issue.
- [ ] “High engagement outweighs any risk.”
- [ ] Security is only the IT department's responsibility.
- [ ] Once a post is deleted, its consequences disappear.
- [ ] A low-likelihood risk can be ignored regardless of impact.

## Evidence sources

Use retrieval responses, risk matrix, consequence cases, DojoLearn response log, A.D1 formative evaluation and exit ticket.

## Assessment boundary

These checks demonstrate readiness for the Week 6 Learning Aim A assessment workshop. They are not a substitute for independently produced Assignment 1 evidence.
