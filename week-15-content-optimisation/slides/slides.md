# Week 15 Slides — Content Optimisation

## Slide 1
### Content Optimisation
Change because evidence gives a reason.

---
## Slide 2 — Cycle
Evidence → problem/opportunity → hypothesis → controlled change → implement → measure → compare → judgement.

---
## Slide 3 — C.M3
Use campaign data to optimise content, format and features in line with plan requirements.

---
## Slide 4 — Hypothesis
Because [evidence], changing [variable] may improve [measure/outcome] because [reason].

---
## Slide 5 — Preserve before state
Capture original content/configuration/metrics before changing it.

---
## Slide 6 — Control
Avoid unnecessary simultaneous changes; identify confounding variables.

---
## Slide 7 — Protect requirements
Optimisation should not abandon audience, brand, policy or accessibility merely to increase a metric.

---
## Slide 8 — Compare
Before evidence → after evidence → comparison → interpretation → limitation → decision.

---
## Slide 9 — Decisions
Retain • refine further • revert • insufficient evidence.

---
## Slide 10 — Failure can be useful
A well-evidenced unsuccessful optimisation can still demonstrate strong C.M3 reasoning.

---
## Slide 11 — BC.D3 evidence
Independent decisions, creative problem solving and organised iteration can demonstrate responsibility, creativity and self-management.

---
## Slide 12 — Distinction split
Evaluation of plan/use belongs to **BC.D2**. Professional behaviours belong to **BC.D3**.
