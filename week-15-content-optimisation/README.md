# Week 15 — Content Optimisation

**Specification focus:** Learning Aim C — C2/C4/C5  
**Assessment preparation:** C.M3 plus BC.D3 professional-behaviour evidence  
**Practical environment:** DojoLearn social-media simulation

## Week purpose

Learners use Week 14 evidence to identify a justified optimisation opportunity, make a controlled change and compare evidence where sufficient data exists.

**Optimisation cycle:** evidence → problem/opportunity → hypothesis → controlled change → implement → measure → compare → judgement.

C.M3 is evidenced through evidence-led optimisation. BC.D3 may also be supported by authentic evidence of creativity, individual responsibility and effective self-management when learners independently identify, implement, document and manage justified improvements.

## Assessment boundary

Teachers may teach optimisation methods but should not choose the learner's optimisation, rewrite assessed content or manufacture positive results.
