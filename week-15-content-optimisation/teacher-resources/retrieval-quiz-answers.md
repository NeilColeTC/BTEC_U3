# Week 15 Retrieval Quiz — Indicative Answers

1. A defined quantitative measure of campaign activity/performance.
2. Recorded starting state before implementation.
3. Intended performance level/outcome for a relevant measure.
4. Content performing strongly against a relevant objective/comparison.
5. A feature of available data that restricts what can safely be concluded.
6. Correlation is an association; causation means evidence that one factor produced an effect.
7. Because different content may have different exposure/opportunity, purpose or context.
8. A question generated from analysis about what evidence-based change might improve campaign performance.
9. So a reliable, traceable before/after comparison can be made.
10. It begins from campaign evidence, identifies a relevant issue/opportunity and changes something for a defined objective/measure rather than personal preference.

Use diagnostically; learners make their own assessed optimisation decisions.
