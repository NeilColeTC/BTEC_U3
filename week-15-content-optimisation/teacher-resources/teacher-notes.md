# Week 15 Teacher Notes — Content Optimisation

## Curriculum intent

The repository scaffold defines Week 15 as C2/C4: use analytics evidence to improve content, format, timing and platform features, document what changed/why and produce before/after optimisation evidence in DojoLearn. C.M3 is the assessment focus.

## Central principle

Optimisation should be **evidence-led**, not a late-stage redesign exercise.

Require the chain:

**Week 14 finding → optimisation decision → implementation → comparison → judgement**.

## Hypothesis discipline

Before changing anything substantial, learners should explain:

- what evidence identified the issue;
- what variable they will change;
- what outcome they expect may improve;
- why the change might influence that outcome;
- what measure they will monitor.

This makes later justification far stronger.

## Controlled change

This is not a laboratory experiment, but learners can still improve analytical quality by avoiding unnecessary simultaneous changes. Discuss confounders such as timing, exposure, platform, audience opportunity and campaign stage.

## Before evidence

This is operationally critical. Once content/settings are edited, the original may be difficult to reconstruct reliably. Require evidence capture first.

## Comparing performance

Use the same metric definitions and comparable opportunity where possible. An increase after optimisation is evidence of association, not necessarily proof that the changed feature caused it.

## C.M3

The merit-level emphasis is the use of data to optimise the campaign and analyse the effect of improvements. Learners should show the evidence chain, not simply state that they “made improvements”.

## Unsuccessful optimisation

Do not create an environment where learners think every change must succeed. A failed/mixed optimisation can generate excellent evidence if the learner identifies the result accurately and makes an appropriate decision.

## Insufficient evidence

Where the simulation/lesson duration does not provide enough post-change data, learners should document that limitation rather than inventing or extrapolating results.

## Assessment boundary

Teachers may teach optimisation methods and simulator functionality but should not choose the learner's change, rewrite assessed content or supply the final C.M3 judgement.

## Preparation for Week 16

Learners should leave with an optimisation evidence chain that connects planning, implementation, analytics, change and post-change judgement. Week 16 can then evaluate the campaign as a whole.
