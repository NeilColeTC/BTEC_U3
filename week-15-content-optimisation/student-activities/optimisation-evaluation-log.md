## Reasoning standard

Use:

**change → before evidence → after evidence → comparison → interpretation → limitation → decision**

## Important

A useful optimisation does not have to produce a positive result. Evidence that a change failed, had mixed effects or cannot yet be judged can still support C.M3 if the optimisation process is genuinely data-led and the conclusion is justified.
