# DojoLearn Practical — Optimisation Workshop

## Purpose

Use campaign analytics to make and evaluate controlled evidence-based improvements.

## Stage 1 — Select the opportunity

Start from a Week 14 analytics finding. Do not start by choosing something you simply want to redesign.

## Stage 2 — Preserve the original

Capture the original content/configuration and relevant performance evidence before making any change.

## Stage 3 — Define the hypothesis

Record:

**Because [evidence], changing [variable] from [before] to [after] may improve [measure/outcome] because [reason].**

## Stage 4 — Quality gate

Check that the optimisation remains consistent with:

- business requirements;
- campaign objective;
- intended audience;
- brand/tone;
- accessibility;
- social-media policy;
- simulator capabilities.

## Stage 5 — Implement

Make the controlled change using available DojoLearn features. Update implementation/deviation logs where appropriate.

## Stage 6 — Measure

Capture post-change analytics/evidence when the simulation provides sufficient opportunity. Use comparable metrics where possible.

## Stage 7 — Compare

Use:

**change → before evidence → after evidence → comparison → interpretation → limitation → decision**.

## Stage 8 — Decide

Choose and justify one:

- **retain** — evidence supports keeping the change;
- **refine further** — evidence suggests promise but another controlled improvement is justified;
- **revert** — evidence suggests the original approach was stronger;
- **insufficient evidence yet** — available data does not support a reliable decision.

## Evidence chain

Ensure the following can be traced:

**ANA finding → OPT decision → implementation evidence → post-change evidence → judgement**.
