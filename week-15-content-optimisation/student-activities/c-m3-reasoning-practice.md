# C.M3 Reasoning Practice — Evidence-Led Optimisation

**Neutral formative exercise — not a completed Assignment 2 response.**

## Scenario

A fictional campaign has two comparable information posts. Post A has a strong exposure level but a relatively weak intended-action rate. Post B has a clearer action prompt and a stronger intended-action rate, although several other differences also exist between the posts.

The campaign team proposes changing Post A's action prompt to make the next step clearer.

## Task

Evaluate whether this is a reasonable optimisation and how its effect should be tested.

Address:

1. What evidence supports testing the change?
2. What is the variable being changed?
3. What should remain similar if possible?
4. Which metric should be monitored and why?
5. What confounding variables could affect the result?
6. What before/after evidence is needed?
7. What would justify retaining, refining, reverting or withholding judgement?
8. Why would an improved result still not prove with certainty that the CTA change caused it?

## Reasoning chain

**analytics evidence → justified change → implementation → comparison → qualified judgement**
