## Priority test

Consider:

1. Is the issue relevant to an important campaign objective?
2. Is there enough evidence to justify testing a change?
3. Is the proposed variable plausibly connected to the issue?
4. Can the change be implemented within campaign/simulator constraints?
5. Is there a relevant outcome that can be measured afterwards?
6. Could the change harm accessibility, brand, policy compliance or another objective?

## Select

Identify the highest-value optimisation to test and explain why it deserves priority over the alternatives.
