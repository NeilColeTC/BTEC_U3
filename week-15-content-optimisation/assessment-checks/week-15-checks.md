# Week 15 — Assessment and Understanding Checks

## Criteria progression

**C.M3:** use campaign data to make justified improvements/optimisations and analyse their effect on campaign performance.

Week 15 also produces important evidence for **BC.D3** evaluation in Week 16.

## Core checks

By the end of Week 15, learners should be able to:

- [ ] identify optimisation opportunities from Week 14 analytics;
- [ ] prioritise opportunities against objective relevance, evidence, impact, feasibility and measurability;
- [ ] distinguish optimisation from arbitrary redesign;
- [ ] state an evidence-based hypothesis;
- [ ] identify the variable being changed;
- [ ] identify the relevant dependent measure/outcome;
- [ ] identify possible confounding variables;
- [ ] preserve before-state evidence;
- [ ] maintain business requirements/objectives during optimisation;
- [ ] maintain audience appropriateness/accessibility;
- [ ] maintain branding/policy compliance;
- [ ] implement the change using available DojoLearn features;
- [ ] capture after-state evidence where available;
- [ ] compare before/after performance fairly;
- [ ] use rates/percentage change only where valid;
- [ ] distinguish association from proof of causation;
- [ ] state limitations;
- [ ] decide to retain, refine, revert or withhold judgement;
- [ ] maintain a traceable optimisation evidence chain;
- [ ] prepare optimisation findings for Week 16 evaluation.

## Common misconceptions

- [ ] “Any change is optimisation.”
- [ ] “I should change the lowest-performing post without investigating why.”
- [ ] “Changing several things gives me a better chance of improvement.”
- [ ] “I can remember what the original looked like without capturing it.”
- [ ] “If the metric rises, my change definitely caused it.”
- [ ] “An unsuccessful optimisation means I have failed C.M3.”
- [ ] “I should hide an optimisation that made performance worse.”
- [ ] “I must claim success even if there is not enough post-change data.”
- [ ] “A higher metric is automatically better even if the audience/objective is less appropriate.”

## Evidence sources

Use Week 14 analytics findings, optimisation opportunity matrix, hypothesis/change plan, before/after evidence, DojoLearn implementation evidence, optimisation evaluation log and C.M3 reasoning.

## Transition to final evaluation

Before Week 16, learners should have:

1. traceable Week 14 findings;
2. at least one justified optimisation decision where campaign evidence supports it;
3. preserved before-state evidence;
4. implementation evidence of the change;
5. after-state evidence where sufficient data exists;
6. a qualified judgement about the optimisation;
7. documented limitations/confounders;
8. clear links between optimisation and campaign objectives/business requirements.
