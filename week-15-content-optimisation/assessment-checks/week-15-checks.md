# Week 15 — Assessment and Understanding Checks

## Criteria

**C.M3:** evidence-led optimisation of content, format and/or features meeting plan requirements.  
**BC.D3:** authentic professional-behaviour evidence may be generated through independent, creative and self-managed optimisation.

## Checks

- [ ] identify optimisation opportunity from analytics;
- [ ] state a testable hypothesis;
- [ ] identify variable and relevant measure;
- [ ] preserve before-state evidence;
- [ ] implement a justified change;
- [ ] preserve after-state evidence where available;
- [ ] compare fairly and state limitations;
- [ ] retain/refine/revert/withhold judgement appropriately;
- [ ] demonstrate independent ownership of the optimisation process;
- [ ] demonstrate appropriate creativity/problem solving;
- [ ] maintain organised evidence and change control.

Analytical conclusions from optimisation later support BC.D2 evaluation; BC.D3 is demonstrated through the professional behaviours themselves.
