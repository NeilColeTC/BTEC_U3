## Content-purpose challenge

Consider these purposes:

- information;
- promotion;
- humour;
- offers;
- customer service.

Choose three. For each, identify two possible content formats and compare their likely suitability.

## Accessibility check

For image/video content, identify adaptations that could make communication more accessible, such as useful alternative text, captions, readable text and clear visual design.

## Judgement challenge

A manager says: **“Video is always the most engaging format, so every important message should be video.”**

Assess this claim. Your answer should identify circumstances in which video may be strong and circumstances in which another format may be more appropriate.
