## Reach, engagement or outcome?

Classify each example primarily as evidence of **reach**, **engagement**, or **business outcome**:

1. 8,000 users were shown a post.
2. 260 users commented, shared or reacted.
3. 45 users completed a booking after following a link.
4. A post was shared into a relevant community.
5. 900 users clicked through to a product page.

Discuss why a high number at one stage does not automatically prove success at another stage.

## Comparison

Choose two techniques that could help the same fictional business attract an audience. Compare:

- control;
- cost/resources;
- likely reach;
- relevance to target audience;
- interaction potential;
- possible business outcome.

Reach a supported judgement about which you would prioritise and why.
