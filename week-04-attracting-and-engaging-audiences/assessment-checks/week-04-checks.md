# Week 4 — Assessment and Understanding Checks

## Primary progression

**A.M1:** assess the different ways in which a business can use social media to attract a target audience.

## Core checks

By the end of Week 4, learners should be able to:

- [ ] distinguish **explain** from **assess**;
- [ ] compare text, image, video, links, polls and quizzes;
- [ ] connect content purpose to business objectives;
- [ ] explain possible roles for humour, information, offers and customer-service content;
- [ ] explain how keywords, tags/hashtags, networking, sharing and advertising may help attract audiences;
- [ ] distinguish reach, engagement and business outcome;
- [ ] adapt a post concept to a defined target audience;
- [ ] identify strengths and limitations in context;
- [ ] compare alternatives directly;
- [ ] reach a supported judgement;
- [ ] avoid unsupported claims of guaranteed effectiveness.

## Assessment quality

### Secure Merit preparation

Learner compares multiple approaches, relates them consistently to target audience/business purpose, considers strengths and limitations, and reaches a judgement supported by the comparison.

### Developing

Learner explains several appropriate approaches and identifies some advantages/disadvantages but comparison or judgement is limited.

### Requires intervention

Learner mainly describes content/platform features, treats popularity/engagement as automatic success, or gives a preference without contextual reasoning.

## Common misconceptions

- [ ] “Assess means list advantages and disadvantages.”
- [ ] “Video is always more engaging.”
- [ ] “More hashtags always create more reach.”
- [ ] “High reach proves the campaign succeeded.”
- [ ] “High engagement proves a sale/booking occurred.”
- [ ] “Paid advertising guarantees the right audience.”
- [ ] “Humour works for everyone.”
- [ ] “One platform/content type is objectively best for all businesses.”

## Evidence sources

Review retrieval responses, content-format comparison, engagement-technique activity, DojoLearn alternatives/recommendation, A.M1 formative response and exit ticket.

## Assessment boundary

These are formative checks. Learners must independently apply the reasoning to their own researched evidence and the live Assignment 1 requirements.
