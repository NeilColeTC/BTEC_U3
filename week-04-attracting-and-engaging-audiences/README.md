# Week 4 — Attracting and Engaging Audiences

**Specification focus:** Learning Aim A — A2 Business uses of social media  
**Assessment preparation:** A.M1  
**Practical environment:** DojoLearn social-media simulation

## Week purpose

Week 4 brings together the business-use knowledge from Week 2 and audience-profile work from Week 3. Learners examine how businesses can use different content formats and engagement techniques to attract a target audience, then compare alternatives and reach supported judgements.

The emphasis changes from **explain** to **assess**.

## Learning outcomes

Learners should be able to:

1. Explain how text, images, video, links, polls and quizzes can be used for different purposes.
2. Compare informational, promotional, humorous, offer-based and customer-service content.
3. Explain how keywords, hashtags, networking, sharing and advertising can help attract audiences.
4. Relate content and engagement choices to a defined target audience and business objective.
5. Compare alternative approaches and identify strengths, limitations and trade-offs.
6. Reach supported judgements about which approaches are likely to be most appropriate in context.

## Learning progression

**explain → compare → analyse suitability → consider limitations → judge**

A useful A.M1 reasoning structure is:

**business objective + target audience → approach A → strength/limitation → approach B → strength/limitation → comparison → supported judgement**

## DojoLearn practical

Learners create alternative post concepts for the same fictional business objective and audience, adapting format, message and simulated platform. They then compare the likely effectiveness of their alternatives rather than assuming that one content type is universally superior.
