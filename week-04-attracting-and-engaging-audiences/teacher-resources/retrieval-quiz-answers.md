# Week 4 Retrieval Quiz — Indicative Answers

1. The group a business intends to reach.
2. Examples specified in earlier teaching include age, gender, interests and income.
3. To avoid assumptions/stereotypes and support defensible business decisions.
4. Examples include branding, promotion, customer communication, customer service, issue resolution and advertising.
5. A feature/structural quality of a platform that affects how users/businesses can communicate or interact.
6. Audiences, objectives and communication needs differ, so the relevance of platform characteristics differs.
7. To understand usage/audience/performance and support decisions.
8. An objective is an intended business result; a platform feature is a capability that may help achieve it.
9. Make clear how/why something works or matters using relevant detail/reasons.
10. Comparison, strengths, limitations, contextual significance and a supported judgement.

Accept equivalent accurate wording. Use diagnostically rather than as assessment-ready responses.
