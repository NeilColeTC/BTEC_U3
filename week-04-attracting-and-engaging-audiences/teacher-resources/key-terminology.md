# Week 4 Key Terminology

| Term | Working definition | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Attract | Gain the attention/interest of the intended audience. | Central to A.M1. |
| Engage | Encourage meaningful audience interaction or involvement. | Interaction may help develop relationships and provide feedback. |
| Content format | The form in which a message is presented, e.g. text, image, video, link, poll or quiz. | Format should fit purpose, audience and platform. |
| Content purpose | What the content is intended to achieve, e.g. inform, promote, entertain, offer support or encourage action. | Prevents content creation becoming activity without purpose. |
| Keyword | A relevant word/phrase associated with the topic, audience need or intended discovery. | Can help communicate relevance and support discoverability. |
| Hashtag/tag | A platform feature used to label/connect content, topics, users or conversations where supported. | Can support discovery/networking but effectiveness is context dependent. |
| Networking | Building connections and interactions with users, organisations or communities. | Can extend relationships and visibility. |
| Sharing | Redistributing content to another audience/network. | Can extend reach but the business cannot assume sharing will occur. |
| Paid advertising | Paying to promote content/messages to an audience. | Can extend/target reach but requires resources and appropriate targeting/content. |
| Reach | Number/extent of people who may be exposed to content. | Visibility is not the same as interaction or business success. |
| Engagement | Audience interaction with content/account. | Useful performance evidence but should be interpreted against objectives. |
| Business outcome | A useful result linked to the objective, such as an enquiry, booking, registration or sale. | Helps distinguish activity metrics from meaningful results. |
| Suitability | How appropriate an approach is for a particular purpose, audience and context. | Core concept when assessing alternatives. |
| Trade-off | A situation where gaining one advantage may involve accepting a limitation elsewhere. | Supports balanced merit-level reasoning. |
| Assess | Consider relevant factors, strengths and limitations to reach a supported judgement. | A.M1 requires more than explanation. |

These are teaching definitions and should be applied to independently researched examples in assessed work.
