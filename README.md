# BTEC Level 3 Unit 3 — Using Social Media in Business

This repository contains the teaching, learning and assessment-support resources for Pearson BTEC Level 3 Unit 3: **Using Social Media in Business**.

The course is organised as a 17-week programme aligned to the Pearson specification, the approved assignment briefs and the 2026–27 scheme of work.

## Learning aims

- **A** — Explore the impact of social media on the ways in which businesses promote their products and services.
- **B** — Develop a plan to use social media in a business to meet requirements.
- **C** — Implement the use of social media in a business.

## Practical simulation

Practical activities use the purpose-built **DojoLearn social-media simulation environment** in `NeilColeTC/dojolearn`. The simulation supports a controlled progression through:

**plan → create → publish → interact → analyse → optimise → evaluate**

Learners use simulated platforms including The Wall, The Pulse, The Grid, The Metronome, The Pin and The Tube to practise campaign planning, publishing, interaction, analytics and optimisation without requiring live public social-media accounts.

## Repository structure

The repository follows the curriculum-resource structure established in `NeilColeTC/BTEC_U18`:

- `curriculum/` — course map, specification mapping and weekly sequence
- `assessment/` — criteria maps, evidence guidance and assessment-support documents
- `templates/` — reusable learner and teacher templates
- `teacher-guidance/` — delivery guidance, simulation guidance and assessment boundaries
- `week-01-...` to `week-17-...` — weekly teaching packages

Each teaching week is designed to contain:

- `README.md`
- `lesson-plan/`
- `slides/`
- `student-activities/`
- `teacher-resources/`
- `assessment-checks/`

## Assessment structure

### Assignment 1 — Learning Aim A

Criteria: **A.P1, A.P2, A.M1, A.D1**

Learners produce a report evaluating how businesses use social media to interact with customers, promote products/services and reach target audiences, including consideration of risks and issues.

### Assignment 2 — Learning Aims B and C

Criteria: **B.P3, B.P4, B.M2, C.P5, C.P6, C.M3, BC.D2, BC.D3**

Learners plan, review, implement, analyse, optimise and evaluate a social-media campaign in response to business requirements.

## Delivery principle

Teaching resources develop knowledge and skills through worked examples, practice scenarios and simulation activities. They are intended to prepare learners for independent assessment rather than provide completed responses to assignment tasks.
