# Week 1 Exit Ticket

Complete independently without discussing your answers.

1. Give **three different ways** social media can support a business.

2. Explain **one difference** between using social media personally and using it on behalf of a business.

3. Choose **one DojoLearn simulated platform**. Identify one of its characteristics and explain how that characteristic could make the platform useful to a business.

4. Complete the sentence:

**A business should not choose a social-media platform simply because it is popular because…**

5. Write down one question you still have about business use of social media.
