# Week 1 — Introduction to Social Media in Business

**Specification focus:** A1

Learners are introduced to Unit 3, the three learning aims and the assessment structure. They distinguish personal from business social-media use, explore how social media has changed business promotion and communication, and identify major platform characteristics.

**DojoLearn:** induction to The Wall, The Pulse, The Grid, The Metronome, The Pin and The Tube; compare their purposes and features with real-world platform types.

**Assessment preparation:** baseline only; establishes vocabulary and prior knowledge.
