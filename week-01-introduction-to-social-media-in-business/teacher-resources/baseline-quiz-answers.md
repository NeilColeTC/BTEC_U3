# Baseline Quiz — Indicative Answers and Diagnostic Guidance

These are indicative points for discussion, not model assignment responses.

1. **Social media** — online platforms/services that allow users/organisations to create, share and interact with content and other users.
2. **Business reasons** — examples include branding, promotion, customer communication, customer service and resolving queries/issues.
3. **Target audience** — the group a business intends to reach with its communication/product/service.
4. **Brand** — the identity/image associated with a business, product or service.
5. **Content types** — examples include text, images, video, links, polls and quizzes.
6. **Engagement** — interaction with an account/content; learners may initially mention likes, comments or shares. Accept sensible examples while explaining that interaction measures vary by platform.
7. **Customer service** — for example answering a product question or responding to a customer issue through a social-media account.
8. **Platform suitability** — platforms differ in features, structure, audience and content/communication style; businesses also have different requirements.
9. **Success information** — learners may suggest followers, likes, shares, comments, reach or other interaction/performance data. Detailed analytics comes later.
10. **Risk** — examples may include negative comments/reputational damage, inappropriate content, time demands, security threats or loss of sensitive/personal information.

## Diagnostic interpretation

Look particularly for these misconceptions:

- “social media” means only the platforms learners personally use;
- the platform with the most users is automatically best for a business;
- business social media is only advertising;
- follower count alone proves success;
- personal familiarity with a platform automatically demonstrates business expertise;
- audience claims based on stereotypes rather than evidence.

Use misconceptions to shape questioning during Weeks 1–3.
