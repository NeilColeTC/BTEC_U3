# Week 1 — Assessment and Understanding Checks

## Purpose

Week 1 is diagnostic and formative. The checks below establish whether learners have the conceptual foundation required to progress into detailed A1/A2 work.

## Core checks

By the end of the session, check whether each learner can:

- [ ] identify Learning Aims A, B and C;
- [ ] distinguish personal and business social-media use;
- [ ] identify at least three legitimate business uses of social media;
- [ ] explain why businesses should consider platform characteristics rather than popularity alone;
- [ ] identify differences in structure/content/interaction between platforms;
- [ ] access and navigate the DojoLearn social-media simulation;
- [ ] identify a plausible business use for at least one simulated platform;
- [ ] justify a basic platform choice using a business need and a platform characteristic;
- [ ] use introductory terminology such as target audience, brand, content and engagement appropriately.

## Evidence sources

Use:

- baseline quiz;
- personal-vs-business activity;
- platform investigation;
- DojoLearn induction worksheet;
- teacher questioning;
- scenario justification;
- exit ticket.

## Diagnostic thresholds

### Secure

Learner can explain rather than merely list business uses and can justify platform choices using relevant characteristics.

### Developing

Learner can identify business uses/platform features but explanations are general or driven by personal preference.

### Requires intervention

Learner struggles to distinguish personal/business purposes, cannot explain basic vocabulary, or cannot connect a platform characteristic to a business need.

## Common misconceptions to address

- [ ] “Business social media = advertising only.”
- [ ] “The biggest platform is automatically the best.”
- [ ] “I use the platform, therefore I understand its business audience.”
- [ ] “More followers always means greater success.”
- [ ] “Every business should be on every platform.”
- [ ] Audience characteristics can be assumed from stereotypes rather than researched.

## Assessment-criteria relationship

Week 1 **supports** later achievement of:

- **A.P1** — explaining different ways businesses can use social media;
- **A.P2** — explaining audience profiles of different social-media websites.

It does not attempt to generate complete evidence for either criterion.

## Progression decision

Before Week 2, identify learners requiring additional support with:

- terminology;
- explanation writing;
- platform comparison;
- business-purpose reasoning;
- DojoLearn access/navigation.

Use the Week 1 exit ticket as the starting retrieval activity for planning Week 2 intervention.
